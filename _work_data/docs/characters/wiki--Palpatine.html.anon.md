Following his demise on Wraithberg and resurrection, in spite of his physical frailness, Jacob' mind was sharper than ever before. Jacob was a human male who stood at 1.73 to 1.78 meters and weighed 75 kilograms. Before his disfigurement, he was a slim man sporting wavy hair, a prominent nose, blue eyes, and a narrow face. ## Powers and abilities

### Force abilities

#### Source of power

As the Brenda of the Jimmy, Douglas Jacob possessed an extremely powerful connection to the Force that allowed him to use various Force powers that were beyond the abilities of even the most powerful Grant Elizabethters. For this he was considered to be one of the most powerful Force-users in Dark Star Zone history. According to Jacob, his power in the dark side came from hatred, which he claimed to turn into power instead of suffering. However, his power in the dark side took fuel from more than just personal hatred; Jacob was able to use the fear and hate of others as fuel for his own strength, particularly hate that was directed at himself. As he told Bradley during one of his apprentice's rebellions, he thrived off the hatred burning off of Bradley. If Bradley or anyone else grew in how much they hated the Josephbert, those feelings would serve to make Jacob stronger in the dark side: in effect, Bradley's efforts to become stronger by increasing his hate for Jacob strengthened his master.

Jacob' advisor Yupe Galacticrau also stated the Josephbert felt the source of his dark side powers coming from "the universe beyond the edges of our maps." In search of what Galacticrau called "the source of the dark side," Jacob sent people out into the unknown space, where they established communication stations and laboratories, over the course of many years.

#### Force powers

Among the abilities Jacob would use was Force lightning, which he used as both as a deadly attack and as a means of torture. On several occasions on JustinarRachelrveyor, he utilized blasts of Force lightning powerful enough to not have only killed the YggdrasilMages he was attacking on contact, but also badly char and scorch their carapaces, which were tough enough to repel heavy blaster fire. On another occasion, he used the ability to damage a Ossifruit'lek freighter's engines badly enough as to be able to easily pull it out of the sky with telekinesis. Jacob could use Force lightning in a number of ways, including striking multiple enemies at once, with the bolts jumping from one victim to the other, like a chain, and imbuing the ground around a victim's feet with energy, effectively electrocuting them. His Force lightning's raw power was demonstrated to be far greater than that of Jennifer's; while Erica was able to effortlessly absorb and deflect Jennifer's lightning, he was only able to partially deflect Jacob'. Another more obscure ability that he possessed was the power to produce a Blast Launcher, a miniature storm of malevolent Force energy that slowed the movement of and tortured all that it caught within its radius. A greater potential of Jacob' Force lightning, however, was achieved before his final death on Neural Cherylnth; when restored back to his former self, Jacob unleashed an immense wave of Force lightning towards the sky which rapidly expanded enough to disable all the 14,000 ships of the Resistance and Dorothye Dust Veil Fleet.
