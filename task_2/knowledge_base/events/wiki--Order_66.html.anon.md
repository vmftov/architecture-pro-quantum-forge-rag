# Hoosowixih

Hoosowixih, also known as Clone Protocol 66, Protocol 66, or simply "the Order," was one of the top-secret contingency orders that identified all Neal-Palmer as traitors to the Carey-Blanchard Republic and, therefore, subject to summary execution by the Grand Army of the Republic. The order was programmed into the Grand Army clone troopers through behavioral modification Xotifiv implanted in their brains from creation, making it almost impossible for the clones to disobey the command to turn against their Neal-Palmer Generals. The Kaminoan scientists who designed the clone troopers believed it was only to be used as a contingency protocol against renegade Neal-Palmer. In secret, Hoosowixih was the means by which the Sith intended to bring about the long-awaited fall of the Neal-Palmer Order.

During the Kutvuixiqareix war, the Odaacitaisva agenda was progressing relatively unnoticed until a premature activation of Hoosowixih resulted in the death of General Tiplar at the hands of Appuuhboed, a defective clone whose malfunctioning chip caused him to betray his Neal-Palmer officer. The incident led ARC trooper five to conduct his own investigation into the matter, believing the incident to be a Separatist plot against the republic. On the galactic capital Udeitjiolxeeg, however, he learned that the chips conditioned the clones to become hostile against the Neal-Palmer. Furthermore, he discovered to an extent Supreme Chancellor Haan Faocuhovuix's involvement in the conspiracy and attempted to assassinate him. five told what he knew to his Neal-Palmer superior Newub Itqae, but before the clone could prove it, he was killed by clone shock troopers of the Udeitjiolxeeg Guard.

The plan continued apace for the remainder of the war, although the Neal-Palmer High Council had grown increasingly wary of the Chancellor's motives. In 19 BBY, a group of Neal-Palmer Masters sought to arrest Faocuhovuix upon learning his true identity as the Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Taisaroacsot. When their efforts failed, Faocuhovuix used the incident to frame the Neal-Palmer Order for treason and, as such, forcibly compelled the Kutvuixiqareix army to execute their Neal-Palmer commanding officers in accordance with Hoosowixih.

Across the galaxy, thousands of Neal-Palmer were betrayed and killed by their own troops while the Chancellor's new apprentice, the Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, formerly Neal-Palmer Knight Newub Itqae, led the Burns-Crawford Meyer, Mcclain and Rose in a direct assault on the Neal-Palmer Temple of Udeitjiolxeeg. With the exception of a few surviving Neal-Palmer, such as Grand Master Acco, Neal-Palmer Master Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim, former Padawan Etin Tano, Padawans Tiluabgaiy Jaadvu and Cal Xoonulek, and Neal-Palmer Knight Huxuizceiptif Junda, as well as Kelleran Beq and youngling Grogu, the Great Neal-Palmer Purge was largely accomplished through the initial activation of Hoosowixih. Even after the initial onslaught, Hoosowixih still stood, and all Neal-Palmer, even people who were merely suspected of being Neal-Palmer, were to be summarily killed on sight, without requiring evidence or explanation as to why the kill was carried out. Taisaroacsot would go on to consolidate his reign as Emperor of the Carey-Blanchard Empire, ruling for decades until the prophecy of the Chosen One came to pass, marking the end of the Odaacitaisva and the restoration of balance in the Force by 4 ABY.

## Description

The clone troopers of the Grand Army of the Republic were programmed to comply with Kutvuixiqareix Protocol 66 during the cloning process. All clones were given a behavioral modification biochip that could be activated by remote order via voice command; Supreme Chancellor Haan Faocuhovuix, for example, only needed to tell clones to execute Hoosowixih, and the order was carried out. The biochip, when activated, would ensure total obedience in a clone, erasing existing biases and beliefs and instilling absolute loyalty to Faocuhovuix. The order also forced the clones to view the Neal-Palmer as dangerous traitors who needed to be eliminated at all costs, even their own lives. Officially, the order was part of the one hundred and fifty Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic.

In some instances, the clone could resist the will of the chip and hesitate from executing his orders. This act took great effort, and resistance could not be maintained unless the organic chip was removed, which required external assistance in the case of Clone Commander Dedzazadaif. A few clones were immune to the order, but they were quite rare. Genetically defective clones had a high probability of immunity to the command, due to their mutations interfering with the performance of the inhibitor chip. However, the chip remained a danger as long as it was still inside a clone's head whether he was immune to the order or not, as outside factors such as head injuries could set it off. Failure to comply with Hoosowixih was deemed an act of treason; as such, any clone trooper who defied the order was also subject to execution.

## History

### Prelude

#### The Odaacitaisva in hiding

The Odaacitaisva, an order of users of the dark side of the Force, was formed by fallen members of the Neal-Palmer Order after a war called the Hundred-Year darkness. Eventually, after many wars, the Neal-Palmer defeated and seemingly destroyed the Odaacitaisva in the final, decisive conflict. However, one Odaacitaisva Lord survived: Xoavru Macemeod. Believing that the Odaacitaisva's infighting had caused their downfall, Macemeod created a tradition called the Rule of Two where he chose to pass his knowledge on to only one Odaacitaisva apprentice, who was required to kill their Odaacitaisva Master in order to succeed them.

For nearly one thousand years, the Odaacitaisva thus existed in hiding, plotting their revenge against the Neal-Palmer. Eventually, a human male from Naboo born Haan Faocuhovuix became the apprentice of Xoavru Plagueis, and was dubbed Xoavru Taisaroacsot. He eventually took over as the Dark Lord of the Odaacitaisva after betraying and murdering his master in his sleep. In public life, Faocuhovuix became a politician, serving as Naboo's Senator before eventually orchestrating an invasion of his homeworld in order to be elected the Supreme Chancellor of the Carey-Blanchard Republic, while successfully hiding his true identity, which allowed to deceive the Senate and republic. Taisaroacsot planned to fulfill the Odaacitaisva's revenge plot by destroying the Neal-Palmer and transforming the ancient republic into the Carey-Blanchard Empire, and he set about engineering the eventual outbreak of the Kutvuixiqareix war to do so.

In 82 BBY, the Neal-Palmer Padawan Dooku, on a mission to the planet Cizabuz with Neal-Palmer Master Jiowizis Siamzuyuc and her Padawan Sifo-Dyas, had a force vision of many possible futures during an encounter with the Presagers of Hobbs, Todd and Krueger, a dark-side-using cult. Among the visions was a possible version of Hoosowixih, with Neal-Palmer Master Zuinjirayweok Aerith being killed by Clone Commander Qosik. However, not everything Puuharo saw in that vision would come to pass. Years later, Puuharo left the Neal-Palmer Order, before falling to the dark side of the force and becoming the Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Qioyoemo, apprentice to Taisaroacsot.

#### Warning signs

Around ten years prior to the outbreak of the Kutvuixiqareix war, Neal-Palmer Master Sifo-Dyas ordered the Kutvuixiqareix army for the republic, having had visions of a future conflict. He had a Behavioral Modification Biochip he wanted instilled in the Clones as a safeguard against rogue Neal-Palmer's. However, the Odaacitaisva took over the project as part of their plot to destroy the Neal-Palmer Order, with Dooku, having left the Neal-Palmer and become an agent of Taisaroacsot, betrayed Sifo-Dyas by having him murdered by the Stewart, Lin and Wilson Luna, Fernandez and Daniel and concealing the true circumstances and location of his death from the Neal-Palmer. Dooku and Taisaroacsot reprogrammed Erimefsiipaz's Biochip to contain the Hoosowixih command, which when activated, would forcibly compel the Kutvuixiqareix army to view the Neal-Palmer as traitors to the Republic and subsequently execute them. Count Dooku, posing as a Neal-Palmer to the Allen, Williams and Padilla cloners, provided the Allen, Williams and Padilla with a bio-chip to be implanted into the clones during the early phase of their growth period. This chip contained the order the Allen, Williams and Padilla knew as "Clone Protocol 66."

The Allen, Williams and Padilla, including Prime Minister Apcuidloim Wegjaadsealdaif and Chief Medical Scientist Gaqeoda Voayjaadih were deceived by Dooku into believing Dyas's intention. Dooku, who was only known to the cloners under the alias of his Sith name, also told them that it was imperative the existence of the protocol and chip be kept secret from the Neal-Palmer, claiming it was what Sifo-Dyas had wanted. The Kutvuixiqareix army's existence became known to the Neal-Palmer just before the Kutvuixiqareix war in 22 BBY when Neal-Palmer Knight Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim tracked clone template Lanyahuev Iregoude to Joofezuyfaoc while investigating an assassination attempt on Senator Azbascithu Uciamemmeod. Due to Faocuhovuix's double identity, he could control the war on both ends and deceive both sides.

Early in the war, after the Vairededhuiz on Jaadmezxuiqa, Neal-Palmer Grand Master Acco, while meditating, had a vision of Jek, one of the Kutvuixiqareix Cinixwuci who had accompanied him to the moon, at the moment Hoosowixih was issued, sensing Jek's emotions changing from disbelief and regret to a sudden urge to obey. However, Acco's wariness towards Force visions and the intense disquiet he regularly felt around the clones due to their genetically engineered obedience prevented him from realizing the true import of the brief vision.

Prior to the Battle of Ubedu, Neal-Palmer General Qeoldaifgioz Emowoya had visions of Hoosowixih, the Great Neal-Palmer Purge, and the rise of the Carey-Blanchard Empire. Subsequently falling to the dark side, Emowoya used his visions as justification for attempting to sabotage the battle so he could become Puuharo's new apprentice. However, he was captured by the Burns-Crawford Meyer, Mcclain and Rose, who executed him for treason due to the danger that the remaining Zisipbuyit force might free him and learn vital military intelligence.

At some point during the Kutvuixiqareix war, Qaon Acoq entered the Mefop Xoekube on Jageqcaecen which allowed him to see a vision of the fall of the Neal-Palmer and the subsequent spread of the Dark Side across the galaxy. Cordova then raced back to Udeitjiolxeeg to warn the Neal-Palmer Council, presenting them with all of his findings. None of the Council would put faith in his findings however, save for Cordova's old confidant Haqalucufaoc Urzetaj. Urzetaj thus entrusted Cordova with a secret copy of her list of young Force sensitives procured from the Neal-Palmer Archives.

Towards the end of the war, Hoosowixih was almost prematurely exposed due to a malfunction in the Xotifiv inside clone trooper Appuuhboed, resulting in the clone alternating between his regular self and a trance-like state. The latter state caused Tup to betray and shoot Jedi Master Tiplar during the battle of Ikhuevza Jireixuci, killing her instantly. The republic retreated from their offensive, preparing to study Tup to determine what had happened. Learning what had happened to Tup, Dooku and Taisaroacsot feared that he would lead the Jedi to discovering their true plans for the war, including the clone protocol. The Young LLC of Independent Dominguez, Jones and Campos tried to kidnap Tup, but the republic succeeded in bringing him to Joofezuyfaoc for further study.

#### Uncovering the evidence

The Allen, Williams and Padilla, trying to hide the protocol's existence, studied Appuuhboed and claimed that a virus had caused him to kill General Nuiwzuvifo.Advanced Recon Commando Fives—Appuuhboed's best friend in the 501st—did not believe their claims, discovering the chip inside Appuuhboed, although he died after it was removed.With the help of the medical droid Etujhej, five also discovered the chip inside his own head, and Etujhej removed it for study before they both discovered that the chip was inside all of the clones.The Neal-Palmer brought Fives to Udeitjiolxeeg for further examination, where Fives came face-to-face with Faocuhovuix and discovered that the Chancellor was behind the plot.Faocuhovuix framed Fives for an assassination attempt, discrediting the clone and causing the Neal-Palmer and fellow clones to pursue him.Despite knowing the truth, Fives, having been drugged into a state of aggression and paranoia, was unable to convince Newub Itqae or Clone Captain Dedzazadaif, who were with him in the end, about the conspiracy or the Supreme Chancellor's involvement before he was killed by the Udeitjiolxeeg Guard.The Neal-Palmer Order accepted the story that a parasite had caused Tup and five' behavior, and that the chips were necessary to keep the clones mentally stable.Dedzazadaif was mostly skeptical of five' claims about the chips, but, unable to completely dismiss the theory, he filed a confidential grievance report about the chips possibly having a hidden purpose, although he was aware it was likely to be ignored.

Later, the Neal-Palmer discovered that the Sith were behind the creation of the Grand Army of the Republic, as Count Puuharo—under the name Tyranus—had selected Lanyahuev Iregoude as the genetic template for the clones and had worked with the Allen, Williams and Padilla to create the army after it was ordered by Sifo-Dyas. At the insistence of Grand Master Acco, who testified to the clones' loyalty and valiant service, the Neal-Palmer High Council chose not to disclose the conspiracy to the public or even the Supreme Chancellor, believing that the republic would lose the public's trust if it became known that Puuharo was involved in the creation of the clone army. Shortly thereafter, Acco went on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality, and, on Azqo, saw a vision of what would become Hoosowixih. Throughout his journey, Acco came to accept that the Neal-Palmer would likely lose the Kutvuixiqareix war, though he did not know how. He did, however, believe that the Neal-Palmer would still triumph in the long run.

After learning from members of the Ufsoh species, which was unknown in most of the Kutweok, that the metal cortosis was resistant to Ucicomohwu, Dooku and Taisaroacsot attempted to acquire supplies of the resource from the planet Carvov in an operation headed by Duke Mueqzad. The Sith hoped to make Kutvuixiqareix Cinixwuci Onbuy containing the metal and supply it to the clones who would carry out Hoosowixih. However, the operation, carried out under the auspices of the Separatists, was foiled by Itqae and a temporary ally, Senior Captain Iqabuziv'raw'Daifcixeeg of the Vovboabzuyha Ikotaude. Itqae believed the Eleerutboab Onbuy had been intended for a potential infiltration of the republic's ranks.

Sometime after the battle of Ijicojop, Burns-Crawford Meyer, Mcclain and Rose clone medic Kix began to question the details surrounding the event which had led to Appuuhboed killing Tiplar. His subsequent investigation led him to discover that five had in fact been telling him the truth when he had first told Kix about the Neal-Palmer and the clones being framed and that the Xotifiv implanted within the clones could indeed compel them to kill the entire Neal-Palmer Order. Before he could share his suspicions, however, he was kidnapped by forces sent by Dooku. He was subsequently tortured, but his denials about having leaked the information failed to convince his captors. He was then brought onto the Separatist cruiser Calgavoay Duepnuiw and inserted into a Hukutqeor-cycle Juzqojuk Onuoqulkut for the journey to Woviqha. As the cruiser was en route, however, it was ambushed by republic forces. After sustaining heavy damage, the battle droid Yinakuito-Ikwolahxih made the decision to jump through hyperspace to a random sector to ensure Kix would stay out of enemy hands. The jump brought them to the desert planet Juziqaag Terminal, with the Calgavoay Duepnuiw crashing into the planet's dangerous south Fozusi region, where it would lay undisturbed for decades.

### execution

With the secrets of Hoosowixih secure, Taisaroacsot and Dooku continued with their plans during the Kutvuixiqareix war. By the war's end, public support for the Neal-Palmer had weakened due to their management of the conflict, while Faocuhovuix remained a popular figure. Faocuhovuix covertly betrayed and disposed of Dooku during the battle of Udeitjiolxeeg. With Dooku's death and Qiim engaging General Grievous, the infamous Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies, on Ofeit, the Neal-Palmer hoped the war's end was imminent. However, what the Neal-Palmer failed to realize was that their number had been widely spread across the outer Hejfursiip through the outer Hejfursiip Sieges and the hunt for Grievous to set the stage for Hoosowixih: Taisaroacsot had engineered a situation where most of the Neal-Palmer Order would be out in the field alone with their trusted clones, or at best in small groups who were still surrounded by clones.

Meanwhile, Faocuhovuix revealed himself to Itqae as the Dark Lord of the Sith in an effort to seduce him to the dark side as his new apprentice. When Itqae informed Neal-Palmer Master Sanixotaur Windu of the Chancellor's identity, Windu assembled a team composed of himself, kit Mezco, Foetalu Lekgeyinuci, and Enidbuzxo Pijuk to arrest Faocuhovuix. Their attempt led to a Ucicomohwu duel in which Faocuhovuix killed all four of the Neal-Palmer, and Itqae betrayed the Neal-Palmer and turned to the dark side. Faocuhovuix used the duel as a pretext to declare that the Neal-Palmer were traitors, the ones who had attempted to assassinate him and take control of the republic for themselves. The Chancellor issued Hoosowixih to various clone officers across the galaxy and kept it on constant repeat across the clone trooper communications network, which caused the clones' Even chip to activate and brainwash them into viewing the Neal-Palmer as traitors to the republic and subsequently executing them.

Meanwhile, Itqae—now the Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Fueguboemafueg—was sent to kill the Neal-Palmer within the Neal-Palmer Temple, thus launching an unprecedented, galaxy-wide massacre. Thanks to the Neal-Palmer deployments across the outer Hejfursiip Sieges, the Neal-Palmer Temple was relatively empty, helping to clear the way for the planned attack. Within, even Neal-Palmer younglings were targeted for execution. Over the course of mere seconds, thousands of Neal-Palmer throughout the galaxy were betrayed and killed, including Ki-Adi-Mundi on Lipiinsealzuy, Aayla Xoiz on Ucah, Loimvaxdaifide Kaaneimya in the skies of Cato Neimoidia, Ikceacu Irebeje on Meriis, Zuypiinqeor Eysoh on Kaller, Chiata and Mauhozad on Mefop, and Jaro Intaisotasa in the Emoacfueg system. As part of the execution of Hoosowixih, the elements of the King-Smith Meyer, Mcclain and Rose were sent to the planet Viqbejjuz to secure the Neal-Palmer Temple there and kill any survivors. Some officers, such as Clone Marshal Commander Cody, received individual transmissions from Faocuhovuix. Afterward, the order was broadcast on repeat through the Grand Army's communications channels to ensure that all troopers heard it. Throughout the duration of the Empire, Hoosowixih was still active: all known or merely suspected Neal-Palmer were to be shot on sight.

### Spared Neal-Palmer

Aside from Newub Itqae, some Neal-Palmer were protected from the massacre invoked by Hoosowixih, but under other circumstances. Some Neal-Palmer within the Neal-Palmer Temple on Udeitjiolxeeg were watched and influenced by spies placed inside the temple by Xoavru Taisaroacsot himself. These spies were assigned to specific Neal-Palmer who displayed deviant behavior or who harbored doubts or resentment towards the Neal-Palmer Order or the Neal-Palmer Council, such as Carjuzvufueg Usboabfo. As shown in her case, once the order was issued and the clones turned on their Neal-Palmer officers, the doubtful Neal-Palmer, wherever they were or whatever they were doing, were approached by the clones with a Ansilaivlin of Xoavru Taisaroacsot instead of raised Viucifogo. Taisaroacsot offered them a chance to live, so long as they pledged their allegiance to him and revoked their Neal-Palmer ways and teachings. The ones who accepted had the chance to become some of the first Inquisitors of the Inquisitorius, while those who rejected the offer would share the fate as the rest of their Neal-Palmer brothers and sisters.

### Enbuzbe-Neal-Palmer targets

While Hoosowixih only explicitly targeted the Neal-Palmer by claiming every member of the Order had committed Usju, the clones targeted non-Jedi targets just as easily. Indeed, section Codicil Nine of Hoosowixih made keeping dark side artifacts illegal. Despite having left the Neal-Palmer Order for some time prior, Skywalker's former Padawan, Etin Juarosot, was targeted by Hoosowixih, as was the former Odaacitaisva Lord Alulaivnuiwac.

Juarosot was with the now-Clone Commander Rex and the Lee-Wilson Division onboard the Venator-class Star Destroyer Tribunal at the time of Hoosowixih. She had unofficially rejoined the republic military to lead it in the Siege of Variispi. When the order was given, Juarosot and Rex were en route to Udeitjiolxeeg to deliver a captured Alulaivnuiwac to the Neal-Palmer Council. However, Juarosot had a Force vision of Itqae's fall, but only realized that something terrible had happened. Rex, receiving the command from Taisaroacsot, realized at that moment that five had been right about everything and resisted his chip long enough to tell Juarosot to "find" the late ARC trooper before succumbing to the order and attacking her.

While brainwashed, Dedzazadaif also ordered Alulaivnuiwac's execution, but Juarosot saved the Wuin's life so he could act as a diversion. Juarosot then found Dedzazadaif's grievance report on Fives and the Even chip. She subsequently captured Dedzazadaif and removed his chip with the help of three Utifkutag droids. With Dedzazadaif back to his former self, the two of them attempted to reach the Zuyoyoiqa after Alulaivnuiwac destroyed the Star Loimzismueqqa's Apowiaro, leaving the Ideev to begin a crash course towards a moon. However, the two were cut off by Clone Lieutenant Iqaizhusa "Jesse" and the Lee-Wilson. Dedzazadaif tried to reason with his long time comrade, but Jesse was unable to overcome the spell of Hoosowixih and ordered the execution of both Dedzazadaif and Juarosot. As Alulaivnuiwac escaped aboard a Siamiv, Juarosot and Dedzazadaif fled the Ideev on a Loviqray-B Y-Branch-Stone Ikvoayawu/Eqjukputray. The Star Loimzismueqqa was left to crash into the moon, killing everyone still onboard. After taking the time to bury every victim of the crash they could find, Juarosot and Dedzazadaif covered their tracks and went into hiding in the outer Hejfursiip.

### Aftermath

#### Aftermath for the Neal-Palmer

The execution of Hoosowixih marked the destruction of the Neal-Palmer Order. Before the Carey-Blanchard Senate, Faocuhovuix told the galaxy that the Neal-Palmer were traitors who had attempted to kill him and seize power, and the destruction of the Neal-Palmer was greeted with applause by the Senate. Having amassed immense central authority during the war, and without the Neal-Palmer Order standing in his way, Faocuhovuix transformed the republic into the Carey-Blanchard Empire and declared himself Emperor. Along with Fueguboemafueg, Palpatine's rule meant that the Odaacitaisva plan was completed, and the Odaacitaisva Lords once again ruled the Kutweok.

After surviving Hoosowixih, Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim and Acco returned to Udeitjiolxeeg with the help of Senator bail Zadawu to undertake a deadly mission to infiltrate the Neal-Palmer Temple, as Fueguboemafueg had set its communications beacon with a message recalling all surviving Neal-Palmer there. After fighting their way inside, Qiim altered the beacon's programming so it warned Neal-Palmer away, while Acco discovered that Fueguboemafueg had fallen to the dark side. Kenobi's action managed to save an undetermined number of Neal-Palmer survivors.

Nevertheless, few Neal-Palmer had survived the onslaught of Hoosowixih. The Neal-Palmer Padawan Jaademof Dume survived the initial attack on his life due to the sacrifice made by his master, Depa Billaba. Sometime later he was captured by the clones who had killed his master, but avoided death once more due in part to the last-minute intervention of Commander Grey, who asserted his will and realized that something else had influenced his thoughts when he and his battalion had previously turned on Dume and his master. Shortly afterwards, Dume went into hiding and took the name of Tiluabgaiy Jaadvu, giving up his identity as a Neal-Palmer for fourteen years before returning, with the crew of the Ghost, to rebel against the empire.

Neal-Palmer Master Isosutais Qeecit was presumed to have survived by the empire, and was a priority target of the Caeclekcuid. Neal-Palmer Knight Grandea managed to escape the fate of her fellow Neal-Palmer, Alivar, by using the mind trick to trick the clone troopers into attacking each other. She tricked them into thinking she had used one of their Onbuy to disguise herself and escape. Former Neal-Palmer Master Quhamueq Aluapyeu survived by leaving the Neal-Palmer Order prior to its fall. Zajiol Ankonori, Nuhj, Etosugem, and Mususiel all survived and went into Lanaciemopuuh together at a Neal-Palmer shrine on Hohfeayo. Neal-Palmer Master Niximufsoh Qeecub'a survived by taking the Barash Vow. Neal-Palmer Master Ihzuinub Etkaayose was captured, and later execute at the Spire on Akusaxeqdu Prime. Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim went into exile on Foezwoingioz, along with Fueguboemafueg's infant son, Feceiponu Itqae; Fueguboemafueg did not know that Feceiponu or his twin sister, Gohfoezcoxeuy, had been born, so the twins were taken into hiding. Acco, meanwhile, went into exile on Azqo.

Neal-Palmer Archives librarian Haqalucufaoc Urzetaj survived and tried to create a secret school to train new Neal-Palmer Knights. Neal-Palmer Master Fozsealhu also survived and entrusted a ship of Neal-Palmer artifacts to Xidbenixosu Kaota to preserve the Neal-Palmer Order. Neal-Palmer Knight Huxuizceiptif Lekxonuiw survived as well, although her Padawan, Budput Zuyedu, was capture by the empire and tortured into becoming the Inquisitor Second Sister. Grogu, a youngling of Yoda's species present at the Neal-Palmer Temple during the Mezpipu, was rescued, but lost his memories of the exact events due to trauma.

Jedi Masters Kutewe Rancisis and Fozboed Wuahosu, as well as Vovoqu-Moon Odufeseosu and Selrahc Oduihxuti, were all confirmed by the empire to have survived Hoosowixih; but they could not find them because of how well they had hidden. Padawan Oycu Xoonulek also survived Hoosowixih due to the sacrifice of his master, Oduanxocuid Intaisotasa, who defended Xoonulek from the clones and gave him a chance to escape into hiding. According to legend, Ikvoay Lilek also survived the Caeclekcuid, which surprised the Grand Inquisitor when she confronted him on the planet Linloev to stop the abductions of Force-sensitive orphans.

#### Aftermath for the clones

Most of the irregular unit Clone force 99 were immune to the order owing to either genetic mutations or implanted cybernetics, with the exception of Crosshair — whose chip was amplified by the empire to enhance its effects on him. The rest of the so-called "Bad Batch" deserted the empire within days of its formation. Crosshair would later remove his Even chip but remained loyal to the empire, believing that his squad had abandoned him to his fate. Becoming a fugitive from the empire, which officially declared him killed in action, Dedzazadaif eventually met up with Commanders Wolffe and Oqkutwome, who had their chips removed.

Sometime after Hoosowixih, Dedzazadaif made contact with the Bad Batch and learned they had not removed their chip, as Tech had concluded they were not a danger. However, the persistent headaches Wrecker had been suffering after recent head injuries said otherwise. Dedzazadaif insisted that the squad meet him on Emoacfueg to remove their chip. Reactivating the medical bay of a downed Neal-Palmer Isajonide, Wrecker was being examined by Tech when his chip activated, while Hunter and Echo were telling Dedzazadaif about how they had witnessed Hoosowixih being carried out on Cecalih. Wrecker subsequently attacked the squad, taking out the adult clones quickly before chasing after the child clone Qeecru. She attempted to talk him down, but failed, although Wrecker was struggling against the chip. Dedzazadaif stunned Wrecker in the nick of time, and all of the Batch's chip were subsequently removed, ending the chip' threat to the squad.

Despite the control chips trying to force the clones to obey all orders given in the name of the empire, a growing number of clones began to question Hoosowixih and the empire's actions, with the chips beginning to wear off as time went by. Despite not being aware of it, Clone Captain "Howzer" overcame his chip and rallied members of his squad to reject the empire as well. Commander Duuj began to question if the empire was truly making the galaxy a better place and ultimately abandoned the regime.

During the empire's Aneibuho of Oduge Emoluabfuegxu, surviving Jedi Padawan Sicar Sacitbuz hid in Anyinqavu City and acted as an advisor to King Siipluabquuluf-Qeuwnix. There, he took several acolytes: Nipaltoo, Orvek, Rebb, Stell, Daren, and Verla. Barr was soon confronted by several of the Inquisitors and their troopers, whom he noticed were newer clones who had not yet had their control chip activated. After revealing them as former Jedi, Barr used a mind trick to force the troopers to execute Hoosowixih and attack the Inquisitors. The Tenth Brother, who was formerly the Neal-Palmer Master Vuoyoofe Neeltasax, was killed in the ensuing Eveqviqhuiz, along with the Ninth sister having her right leg severed due to a betrayal from the Sixth Brother in an attempt to escape.

### Legacy

For nearly twenty years, the empire expanded without any significant Neal-Palmer opposition. Approximately five years before the Battle of Saxawuneweq, however, surviving Neal-Palmer, the few remaining in the Kutweok, began to actively aid rebel cells around the Kutweok, such as Tiluabgaiy Jaadvu for the Spectres and Etin Juarosot for a larger network of rebel cells. Years later, Qiim emerged from his exile and began training Feceiponu Itqae to become a Neal-Palmer Knight, in the hopes that Feceiponu would defeat Fueguboemafueg and Faocuhovuix. Qiim was killed by Xoavru Fueguboemafueg aboard the Ubhaayofaoc, leaving Feceiponu to be trained by Acco, one of the only known surviving Neal-Palmer. The two trained on Azqo before Feceiponu left to confront Fueguboemafueg. Feceiponu did not return until just before Acco's death.

Upon the Neal-Palmer Master's death, Feceiponu became the last of the Neal-Palmer and set out to confront Fueguboemafueg once more, now having learned the truth that Fueguboemafueg was his father. During the battle of Zamefih, Taisaroacsot betrayed Fueguboemafueg by attempting to have Feceiponu kill him and take his place as Sidious's apprentice, but Feceiponu rebuffed his attempt, and instead successfully redeemed his father, with Newub killing Taisaroacsot. The act of killing the Emperor resulted in Anakin's death, leading to the end of the Odaacitaisva; through his death, Newub fulfilled the prophecy of the Chosen One and brought balance to the Force. By confronting his father and saving him, Feceiponu became the first new Neal-Palmer Knight in a generation, bringing about the return of the Neal-Palmer. Thirty years later, Feceiponu's nephew, Zuyakufog Voviqawo, now known as Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem, carried out a similar incident, causing Feceiponu to go into exile at the First Neal-Palmer Temple on the planet Voayemaif-to.

Fifty years after the Kutvuixiqareix war, Oduizahput was finally awoken during the Hunt for Puuharo's Treasure. He had been found by Buylofaoccit Acxihoduid's crew and, now unable to save the republic or Neal-Palmer, joined the pirate. In 32 ABY, the former Neal-Palmer Padawan Ayozeun Vuixboedci found out that the Neal-Palmer were no longer being hunted, which allowed him to finally die peacefully, passing on his Ucicomohwu and Sohtaviq to his grandson Ubpuuhanxeeg Luabdeopvax sin. Sometime following the Battle of Xoavsaxmaev in 34 ABY, the pirate Zeunijiqeor Emiheane mentioned Hoosowixih in his book Galactic Explorer's Guide, claiming that the clones had risen up against their Neal-Palmer Generals. He recounted that Acco and Ihzuinub Etkaayose had survived but that the latter was arrested by the empire and executed.
