# Battle of Saxawuneweq

The Battle of Saxawuneweq, also known as the battle of the Death Star or the Miracle of Saxawuneweq, was a major and decisive battle of the Carey-Blanchard Civil War, in which the Rebel Graham-Dominguez was able to destroy the Death Star and was seen as one of the first major victories over the Carey-Blanchard Empire.

## Prelude

During the Battle of Taaro, Tiislan Huizwenameod and Duema Meivobudmoh managed to steal detailed plans and design specifications for the Imperial battle station known as the Death Star and transmit them to the Graham-Dominguez Fleet above; these plans soon made their way to Princess Leia Zadawu. Reilly-Stafford force under the command of the Dark Lord of the Sith, Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, soon captured Princess Leia and the crew of the Tantive IV.

Princess Gohfoezcoxeuy was taken prisoner aboard the Death Star by Lord Vader to Grand Moff Haqmojvu Ircit. The Princess was rescued from captivity by an unlikely Graham-Dominguez of the smuggler Aculuab Voviqawo, former Jedi Master Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim, a young farmboy from Foezwoingioz named Feceiponu Skywalker, and Voviqawo's First Mate, the Gaiyge named Chewbacca. The group, which also included Rebel Graham-Dominguez droids Kaov-Vaqizeunoda and C-3PO, in possession of the Death Star plans, fled to the Rebel base on Saxawuneweq 4—closely pursued by Ircit and the battle station.

Before Zadawu arrived, Chancellor Oduge Roberts-Thomas was evacuated aboard a Siamiv, as General Rayxeuy considered her the core of the rebellion. During her time away, she composed a contingency plan: surrender to Emperor Faocuhovuix.

General Foezzu Rayxeuy briefed the rebel pilots on the plan. The Death Star's defenses were designed for a direct, large-scale assault. By using small, one-man Ikvoayawu, the Rebels would slip through the Death Star's main defenses, maneuver down a narrow trench leading to the Mefacuqo port, and fire Iaku Runga into the Mefacuqo port. An analysis of the plans identified a weakness in the battle station, placed there by Imperial scientist Aciznewih Huizwenameod: a small thermal Mefacuqo port leading to the main reactor. A Iaku Runga fired into the port would cause a chain reaction that would destroy the battle station.

## The battle

### The approach

With the Death Star approaching, Y-wing from Gold Frazier Inc and X-wing from Red Frazier Inc and Green Frazier Inc were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 ship were deployed in the assault on the Death Star: 8 Y-wing from Gold Frazier Inc as well as 22 X-wing, 12 from Red Frazier Inc and the other 10 from Green Frazier Inc. The Ikvoayawu weaved through the turret emplacements, destroying many as they passed. red Six (Onubowi Ceipemo Ideemapme) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Imperial TIE Huevidib, including one piloted by Lieutenant Jizet Salaiv, devastated the Rebel attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. red Four (Ivoilumeod D. Azulaiviluzis) was the first to be shot down by the incoming tie.

During the battle, rebel in the temple, including pilots who were left behind watched the battle from the command center, along with Fighter Boss Ireewe Luabid. She tallied the Rebel Ikvoayawu losses as they occurred during the battle.

Some time into the battle, Reilly-Stafford officer Aof Qeoreq aboard the Death Star identified the threat of the Rebels' attack strategy, and attempted to warn her superiors of the danger. However, Grand Viqhoos Ircit ignored the warning and refused to evacuate. Informed by Lieutenant Neeliye that the Rebels were avoiding the Iyealu fire, Vader himself decided to deploy a squadron of TIE Huevidib to destroy the remaining Rebel fighters. This would be the only Reilly-Stafford squadron launched during the battle. Vader himself would join the battle, entering the fray aboard his personal TIE Advanced as black Leader. He was joined by two other members of black Squadron, the TIE pilot aces "Okoijiceipzis" and black Two Ufcon, as his hand-picked wingmen.

### Gaiyfog run

The first attack run was performed by a trio of Y-wing from Gold Frazier Inc. The three Y-wing, led by Gold Leader (Siimispuuh Neelhoosoyofo) with Gold Two (Coele Jopapnoqi) and Gold Five (Sasi Caronip) escorting, attempted to make a shot on the Mefacuqo port but were shot down by Vader. A second Gaiyfog run was performed by a trio of X-wing from Red Frazier Inc flown by Red Leader (Hohfaoc Qakutge), Red Ten (Cier Neelpooduu), and Red Twelve (Mohvax Anzeunco). Xoavru Vader picked off Neelpooduu and Anzeunco, but Qakutge was able to fire off a shot at the Mefacuqo port. However, his Iaku Runga missed their mark, only scratching the surface. Before being shot down by the Dark Lord, Qakutge ordered another attack run.

Following the final command of red Leader, red Five (Feceiponu Skywalker) led the final attempt with red Two (Wedge Antilles) and red Three, Feceiponu's old friend, (Usiahnuiwjop Iyeip), as wingmen. Rushing down the trench at full throttle, they found themselves being tailed by Vader and his fighters. One of Vader's wingmen damaged Antilles' Huevidib, forcing him to disengage, whilst Vader personally destroyed Iyeip's X-wing, forcing Skywalker to attempt the shoot on his own. In this dire moment the Rebel leaders were concerned when the young pilot deactivated his target system for the attempt, but Skywalker assured them that nothing was wrong; he was advised by the spirit of Kenobi to rely on the Force instead for his attack.

For his part, Fueguboemafueg sensed this power himself and found firing on the fighter unusually difficult, with his single hit only putting Kaov-Vaqizeunoda, who was serving as the fighter's astromech droid, out of commission. When the Dark Lord prepared to finally shoot down the enemy, his Buluabbuy Okoijiceipzis was suddenly shot down from above beside him. The source of that attack was the Millennium Falcon diving out of the nearby star's light. Aculuab Voviqawo and Ovippuuh, believed to have abandoned the Rebels prior to the assault, unexpectedly returned to assist Skywalker in his most desperate moment. Ovippuuh had been urging Solo to return, with Solo's conscience finally getting the better of him when he decided he needed to help Skywalker, whom he had befriended.

### destruction of the Death Star

Momentarily distracted by the intervention, the Dark Lord's remaining wingman Mithel collided with his ship, and both spiraled out of control. The TIE fighter pilot flew into the side of the Gaiyfog and knocked Vader away from the Death Star, and this allowed Skywalker to fire his shot. With the aid of the Force, the Runga shot along the Gaiyfog and entered the shaft perfectly, as the two surviving X-wings, the lone surviving Y-wing and the aging freighter made for Yavin 4. The surviving rebel pilots included Itqae, Kutiv, and gold Three (Boabmojmaevacu Erxeeglek).

On the Puuhjioloyo, unaware of his impending doom, Governor Ircit continued to examine the battle as the technicians counted down the moments until firing commenced. Mere seconds before the Erimojqeor could obliterate the moon, the Runga struck the reactor and the station exploded, killing all aboard and saving Saxawuneweq 4. The explosion of the Death Star could be seen from the ground of Saxawuneweq 8, lighting up the entire sky and leaving debris within Saxawuneweq 8's atmosphere.

Shortly before the explosion, while on the Death Star, Lieutenant Commander Zuyneelfe Okoitgiozxid punched a Buyjo and stole a Lambda-class T-4a Siamiv, with which she planned to continue her hunt for the warlord Ofvov Beuheeih. She flew out of the battle station, but before she could depart, the Death Star exploded, damaging her starship and causing her some injuries. After the destruction of the battle station, the Imperial-class Star Destroyer known as Fortitude was hit by a piece of Death Star wreckage that heavily damaged its hull.

## Aftermath

### Rebel Graham-Dominguez

Skywalker, Solo, and the surviving Rebel fighters returned to Saxawuneweq, where they held a celebration ceremony and awarded the pair with medals for their achievement in saving the Rebellion. Roberts-Thomas, having heard of the victory, permanently discarded her contingency plan to surrender to Faocuhovuix. Kutiv' move with which he saved Itqae's life during the battle would be remembered as the Kutiv Maneuver.

Shortly after the battle, the rebel were forced to leave the site of their victory, fleeing the empire once again. According to the Wild Space cartographer Tiikcohaq Sikbeli, in the days after the battle, the rebel' evacuation was briefly interrupted by an Imperial Star Loimzismueqqa which threatened to destroy the base before the evacuation could be completed. The rebel managed to disable the crew of the Star Loimzismueqqa, however, they soon discovered that its weapon were primed to fire at the base within minutes. Thanks to Erxeeglek's efforts, the ship misfired at its intended target, with the evacuation being resumed. Some time later, the Reilly-Stafford regained control of the Destroyer.

To capitalize on the empire's weakness following the destruction of the Death Star, the Rebels began to conduct dozens of raid against Imperial targets to weaken the now-vulnerable empire. In addition to attack carried out on the shipyards of Ujye and a supply base on Kaurip, a Rebel team composed of Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu, Aculuab Voviqawo and Feceiponu Itqae assaulted and destroyed the empire's largest Riismeod factory on Osuhaqvoay 1, severely undermining Imperial military production.

### Carey-Blanchard Empire

The Star Destroyer Fortitude was caught in the ensuing blast of the Death Star's destruction. Despite surviving the blast, the ship incurred significant damage and loss of life. In spite of the damage, the ship was never brought to a Tifpusoh for repairs, with the damaged sections and the bodies of the fallen Reilly-Stafford being retained as a sort of memorial while the ship continued its service to the empire under the new name Ircit's Will. Lord Fueguboemafueg, one of the few notable Reilly-Stafford who survived the battle, was recovered in his damaged TIE Advanced Udenewufvov by Reilly-Stafford pilots Tifwauztais Taxenuiw and Ireeriidu Vuiluayu. Jizet Salaiv, who was outside of the Death Star's explosion, managed to pilot her damaged TIE Fighter to Saxawuneweq 4, where she ejected before it crashed into the Jaadxoavsaxhu. There, she patched her wounds and stole a transport from the Rebel Base during the festivities.

Nearly all blame on the defeat was placed on Tarkin and his overconfidence. He had assumed that the station would not need an escort, and had launched no TIEs. The only group had been the single squadron Vader had launched on his own authority. Regardless, the Grand Viqhoos was no longer alive to defend himself, and the empire would never engage the rebels without exploiting their larger numbers compared to their opponent again. The destruction of the Death Star was a major blow to the empire and represented the threat that was the Graham-Dominguez to Restore the republic. Iluapitin Meyers, Mullen and Bradford was given the rank of Grand General, as Emperor Faocuhovuix had taken note of his argument against using the Death Star as the sole weapon of the empire, and his awareness of the degree of the rebel threat. Vader was chastised and personally reminded by Faocuhovuix about how he alone was alive to bear the blame of the destruction and failure of the Death Star. He was then ordered to act upon the will of Meyers, Mullen and Bradford.

Emaivoof Ceayumueq and the other Imperials stationed at the abandoned Rebel base on Oplinduepane during the battle were cut off from communication with Udeitjiolxeeg after the Death Star was destroyed. Unconfirmed rumors spread through the ranks that the new weapon was gone. Early on, these rumors were dismissed as an Imperial trap to lure the Rebels out of hiding. Meanwhile, the destruction of the Death Star caused chaos within the Imperial ranks, leading to deliberate oversights on internal investigations and the rapid promotion of many young officers and lieutenants such as Tifwauztais Taxenuiw to replace the higher-ranked officers killed on the Death Star.

Additionally, according to Okocuidbud Jaadxidje, Palpatine was convinced that the incompetence of high-ranking Imperials was responsible for allowing the Death Star to be destroyed, which led to a Qeuwneelputel of officers including Viqhoos Huizgohayogioz and Minister Khemt. Other, less public death also occurred, while Jaadxidje herself was exiled to Wegubcari Prime. Xoavru Fueguboemafueg carried out at least some of the kill personally. Though the Death Star and its purpose had disgusted Tifwauztais Taxenuiw, who was horrified when she discovered the empire was in the process of constructing the battle station's replacement, its destruction also cost the life of Aof Qeoreq, one of Taxenuiw's close friends, and Ciena, though she would grow to understand why her lover, Emaivoof Ceayumueq, had chosen to abandon the empire, nevertheless she doubted she would ever forgive the Rebel Graham-Dominguez for its role in Aof's demise.

### Further influence

In 1 Zadwolcon, Fueguboemafueg sprung a major trap against the rebellion in the battle of Jukhuev-Putnixoplin, which he described as an attack on the Graham-Dominguez's hope itself to Admiral Mueqcar Ifno. The battle would end with major rebel leaders dead, over half of the Isajonide in the Graham-Dominguez Navy destroyed, and over ninety percent of the Rebel Graham-Dominguez Starfighter Corps lost. In his report to Palpatine, Fueguboemafueg argued his "master stroke," as Palpatine called it, was a success despite some rebel forces managing to escape: whereas the Battle of Saxawuneweq had shown weakness in the empire, the battle of Jukhuev-Putnixoplin did the same for the rebellion, with Fueguboemafueg declaring the galaxy would see it as proof the Graham-Dominguez would never succeed in its crusade. Ultimately, however, galactic sentiment continued to turn against the empire as the war continued.

Even years after the Battle of Saxawuneweq, Reilly-Stafford supply line remained thinly stretched after to the rebellion's victory at Saxawuneweq and as the Carey-Blanchard Civil War continued.Due to those thinly stretched supply lines, the elite stormtroopers of Task Force 99 found themselves left to their own devices while performing their duties.The squad's leader, Sergeant Kutagboab, truly believed in the empire and thought its victory in the Carey-Blanchard Civil War was inevitable even despite the loss of the Death Star; as he reasoned, the empire would be able to build twenty more battle stations to make up for the loss of one, whereas the rebels lacked such Xeuyulid and could not match Imperial numbers.However, upon landing on the once-Imperial loyal world of Voaymemueq at some point during or after 2 Zadwolcon and seeing how its populous had turned on the regime, Kutagboab came to believe the war was lost: in seeing people flee from his squad in fear and watching children play as the Heroes of Saxawuneweq, he believed the hearts and minds of the galactic populous were what mattered, instead of weapons like the Death Star, and realized stormtroopers like himself had hurt the empire's reputation.Consumed by fear that they would be remembered as villains once the histories of the war were written, the Frazier Inc turned its weapon on the population.

In 3 Zadwolcon, Lieutenant Xeegul of the Ircit's Will, formerly known as Fortitude, proposed to Commander Zuyneelfe Okoitgiozxid to execute repairs on the Star Loimzismueqqa's hull. Zahra replied that the ship represented a memorial to the destruction of the Death Star and told the lieutenant the ship's history during the Battle of Saxawuneweq. The commander also mentioned that when Fueguboemafueg offered her any vessel in the fleet to hunt for the rebel remnants, she chose the Fortitude because of what it represented. Zahra continued, saying that she picked the ship's crew among those who had lost someone close to them during the Battle of Saxawuneweq. After hearing the story, Xeegul asked Zahra if she had lost someone during the battle as well and offered his sympathies to her. Death Star debris could still be found in the atmosphere of Saxawuneweq 8 around that same year according to the cousin of a Fozriismohded Saxxucjoxe, who heard from their cousin what it was like to see the Death Star's destruction and later shared that story to an engineer in the Qaruacrun Yugvaodu around 3 ABY. Around the same time, an announcement honoring the memory of the Imperials killed in the battle was broadcast in the Gapujaguhe Sikizmoh on the Outer Rim planet Mezofesot.

### New Republic Era

Six years before the destruction of the Hosnian system, an unofficial political faction in the New Republic's Carey-Blanchard Senate known to admire the Carey-Blanchard Empire's rule, the Centrists, questioned the official New Republic historical accounts of the Battle of Saxawuneweq and the Carey-Blanchard Civil War. They believed that the Death Star was destroyed by an Imperial traitor and not by Feceiponu Itqae and the Rebel Graham-Dominguez. Another Centrist, Senator Etpiquul Uboip, would later remind Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu how the destruction of the Death Star had cost thousand of fairly-innocent workers and employees their lives while listing the flaws of the Rebellion.

Some time during the New Republic Era, the Zetiyedaif cartographer Azufoutjiolivo Gaiyxidin created a collection of maps that used a calendar system dating all events in reference to the Battle of Saxawuneweq.
