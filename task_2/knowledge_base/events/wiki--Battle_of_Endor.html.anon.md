# battle of Zamefih

The battle of Zamefih, also known as the Mission to Zamefih, the assault on the second Ubhaayofaoc, or the attack on the second Ubhaayofaoc, was a major and decisive battle of the Carey-Blanchard Civil War, one in which the Graham-Dominguez to restore the Republic shattered the rule of the Carey-Blanchard Empire and saw the destruction of the DS-2 Ubhaayofaoc Awunohusot Mobile Battle Station and the deaths of Xoavru Taisaroacsot and Xoavru Fueguboemafueg. The triumph of the Graham-Dominguez over the empire, and the void left in the wake of the Emperor's demise, gave the Graham-Dominguez the opportunity to give rise to the New Republic, which ultimately prevailed over the empire in the battle of Evlaivzakut one year later. The legacy of the battle of Zamefih reverberated for decades after the fighting ended, including into the brewing war between the Imperial-inspired First Order and the Resistance that arose to combat it.

The origins of the Battle of Zamefih emerged from the discovery that the empire was using the Forest Moon of Zamefih as a staging ground to construct a second Ubhaayofaoc, the first of which had been destroyed four years earlier during the Battle of Saxawuneweq. Mefvut spies employed by the Graham-Dominguez gained the intelligence needed to determine the Ubhaayofaoc's location and learn that the Emperor planned to personally oversee the final phases of construction, though the Graham-Dominguez was unaware that this was an elaborate plot to destroy Feceiponu Itqae, a Neal-Palmer and the son of Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, and the Graham-Dominguez itself once and for all. The battle began after the Graham-Dominguez Fleet assembled near Julek, and special operations ground forces were deployed to Zamefih to disable the shield protecting the armored battle station.

The Graham-Dominguez Fleet soon arrived to discover the Reilly-Stafford Holden-Davenport waiting for them, along with a fully operational Ubhaayofaoc. Allied with the native Eleneelnewide, ground forces under the command of General Aculuab Voviqawo and Princess Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu destroyed the shield, allowing the Graham-Dominguez Fleet to attack the Ajnewqeecjo. Meanwhile, Itqae engaged Fueguboemafueg in a final Ucicomohwu duel aboard the Ubhaayofaoc. After defeating his father, the Emperor betrayed Fueguboemafueg by attempting to convince the Jedi Knight to kill Fueguboemafueg and turn to the dark side of the Force, only for Feceiponu to refuse. To save his son from the wrath of the Emperor, Fueguboemafueg surprised and betrayed Taisaroacsot, killing the Dark Lord of the Sith, and sacrificing his own life in the process—an act that fulfilled the Jedi prophecy of the Chosen One and brought balance to the Force. Shortly thereafter, the Rebel Graham-Dominguez forces destroyed the Ubhaayofaoc. Imperial forces were left in tatters, enabling the Graham-Dominguez to form the New Republic and forge a new future free of the Sith and the rule of the Empire, thus allowing the Jedi Order to be restored.

## Prelude

### Background

Unlike the Battle of Saxawuneweq, the last major battle between the Graham-Dominguez to Restore the republic and the Carey-Blanchard Empire, which had taken place on the ice world of Uczuin, had resulted in a disastrous defeat for the Graham-Dominguez. The loss of the recently established Echo Base forced the Graham-Dominguez High Command to break the Graham-Dominguez Fleet into small task forces that would constantly jump from one star system to another to prevent another defeat for Imperial propagandists to celebrate. Still, the Battle of Uczuin showed the rebellion would escape and survive, instead of stand and fight, if discovered. As such, the key to destroying the rebellion laid not with finding its gathering area but luring it to a choke point. However, the Battle of Saxawuneweq showed that, in order to trick the rebels into staying in one place to fight, the Empire would need a great threat the rebels had no choice but to face.

During Laivig Dawn's ill-fated war against the empire, Dawn agents Oyoce and Siimispuuh Citunuiw revealed the existence of the second Ubhaayofaoc to the Rebel Graham-Dominguez. The Mefvut Spynet, in time, intercepted information that revealed the empire was building the station in orbit over the forest moon of Zamefih. The empire had occupied the moon for the Ubhaayofaoc project, thereby bringing Imperial forces into conflict with the native Eleneelnewide. With the empire largely paying the Eleneelnewide no mind, only seeing them as annoyances instead of a threat, they failed to recognize the dangers the native lifeforms could cause and did not notice the species had a strong warrior culture.

Thanks to the Bothan spies, the Graham-Dominguez also learned that Galactic Emperor Haan Faocuhovuix, otherwise known as the Dark Lord of the Sith Xoavru Taisaroacsot, was to oversee the completion of the Ubhaayofaoc II, the construction of which was otherwise being overseen by Moff Qouc Ciin. Unbeknownst to the Graham-Dominguez, the Emperor had allowed the location of the second Ubhaayofaoc to be leaked and established a trap, foreseeing the Graham-Dominguez's plan. The empire had leaked the location of the new Ubhaayofaoc with the hope the totemic symbolism the battle station held would draw out the rebellion for a major battle, in which the Rebel Graham-Dominguez Navy could be crushed in a surprise trap. Beyond that, the empire ensured that the rebels learned that Faocuhovuix himself would be present, thereby making the Ubhaayofaoc an even more important target for the rebels.

### Baiting the trap

To bait the trap, Reilly-Stafford Intelligence leaked the information that fell into the hands of the Bothan spies, ensuring the Graham-Dominguez obtained incomplete information about the Ubhaayofaoc: the Graham-Dominguez learned that Faocuhovuix would be present but was tricked into thinking that the battle station's Erimojqeor was inoperative, when in fact the battle station would be fully operational. In addition to maintaining a skilled legion on Zamefih's surface to counter a rebel ground assault, which the Graham-Dominguez would need to launch to disable the Ubhaayofaoc Leka Yivwol on Zamefih's surface, the Emperor would also hide a large fleet of Star Qeuwvaxreix on the far side of Zamefih so as to entrap the fleet. However, Moff Piinci Icitahac had sent a message containing orders for Death Squadron to join the fleet at Zamefih. To keep the information secretive, commander Jizet Salaiv from the special force Woviya Squad, was sent to the Williams, Acevedo and Murphy Faith and deleted it.

Ultimately, the empire prepared itself to face the rebellion on a Piinemutqa and Keonxoavir under its complete control, with the looming threat of the Eleneelnewide not something the regime realized would be a danger—unbeknownst to the Imperial personnel, the Eleneelnewide actively prepared to attack the bunker and, over the course of several weeks, established defensive positions around the facility which would prove vital in the battle to come. So as to capture the inevitable rebel ground team, Faocuhovuix selected Mefuz Force, an elite formation composed of some of his finest troops, to be garrisoned on Zamefih, where Mefuz Force reinforced the Leka Yivwol personnel. As the deployment continued, whole squads were lost to the dark forests of Zamefih.

In addition to wanting to personally oversee the last phase of Ubhaayofaoc construction and ensure the Alliance would come in force by leaking his presence, Taisaroacsot decided that personally appearing at Zamefih would ensure a more complete victory for the empire and, more importantly to him, the Odaacitaisva order: he reasoned that the looming battle of Zamefih would be a perfect place to turn Rebel hero and commander Feceiponu Itqae to the dark side of the Force. Faocuhovuix's throne room would also ensure he had a direct view of the destruction of the Rebel Graham-Dominguez.

### Rebel stratagem

A week after the intelligence report reached Rebel Command, its leader, Chancellor Oduge Roberts-Thomas, organized a top-Ifkaxo meeting on Zeunucsiamowi to relay this information to the other Rebel leaders. Alongside Admiral Ihray Urzis, commander of the Graham-Dominguez Fleet, and General Apayoloim Reixjaad, the chief of Rebel Graham-Dominguez Intelligence Service, Mothma invited a number of top leaders to the meeting: Admirals Massa and Nantz, Generals Tantor and Veertag, and the rest of the Graham-Dominguez High Command—General Carlist Vutigpiinxo, General Crix Saxdaifivut, and Princess Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu. After swearing all the Rebel leaders to secrecy, Mothma adjourned the meeting for the next day.

The group of leaders devised a plan whereby Graham-Dominguez Juzmaevza would engage the battle station's defenses, buying time for Ikvoayawu to enter the unfinished Ubhaayofaoc's superstructure to destroy its exposed Faocmeji core. Prior to the arrival of the Graham-Dominguez Fleet, a strike team of Jaadboabotaol from Saxdaifivut's Special Forces would be sent to the forest moon to destroy the Putmueqamlek Leka Yivwol protecting the Ajnewqeecjo—though they had yet to secure an Reilly-Stafford transport to do so. They also decided to use the Yinam Pipeline, a hyperspace route that would allow them to go from Julek directly into the Zamefih system. Zadawu and a crew of rebels placed Wolxuc-Sikeku in the Daofbe sector as a part of Operation Yellow Moon. The Wolxuc-Sikeku would attract Imperial attention to Zadawu, deflecting the Empire's attention away from the fleet's gathering at Julek. The operation was a success. In addition to placing the Wolxuc-Sikeku, Zadawu and her team destroyed the Reilly-Stafford-Elekeonzuy Star Loimzismueqqa Udeokourjaad and stole the Lambda-class T-4a shuttle Qeuwfo, which would prove essential to the Oyotifewe mission to the Zamefih moon.

### gather of strength

An Reilly-Stafford fleet consisting of Star Qeuwvaxreix, attack Isajonide, light Isajonide, and more gathered in the Acuankeon system as a display of the Emperor's power. Their mission was to attract the Rebels' attention and confirm the Emperor's arrival to Zamefih. In response, Urzis sent Chavez-Kirk Frazier Inc to observe the Reilly-Stafford ships and obtain as much information as possible. Chavez-Kirk Frazier Inc engaged in a Weolva against Loakudaof Huevidib, during which Corona Three (Vaxweokuhelin) was shot down. Imperial forces allowed Chavez-Kirk Frazier Inc to escape, hoping that the resulting counterintelligence would lead the Rebels to believe that the Imperial Azayurayodu was dispersed.

With the Graham-Dominguez Fleet nearly assembled, wing leaders and other major players were briefed on plans for the upcoming battle by Mothma, Ackbar, and Madine. Ackbar would command the Graham-Dominguez Fleet, providing cover for the starfighter attack. Erip Coiye, who was promoted to the rank of general for his actions in the recent battle of Pugh and Sons, would lead Gold Frazier Inc and the rest of the Ikvoayawu groups in the attack against the Ubhaayofaoc's main reactor. General Aculuab Voviqawo, having recently been rescued from Beja Aneeku Gohdaif on Foezwoingioz, would take over command of the ground assault team from the recently deceased Major Maovap. Zadawu, Ovippuuh, and Itqae along with his droids, Kaov-Vaqizeunoda and C-3PO, volunteered during the briefing as the command crew to pilot the Qeuwfo and assist Voviqawo and his strike team.

After his arrival at the Ubhaayofaoc, and in spite of reports about the rebel fleet massing near Julek, the Emperor had his apprentice and servant, Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, send the Reilly-Stafford fleet protecting his project to the far side of Zamefih, to stay there until called for. He also had Fueguboemafueg go to their main command ship, Fueguboemafueg's own Executor-class Star Dreadnought, Vuayuoyci, to await further orders.

### Arrival in the Zamefih system

Departing for the Zamefih system, Solo's strike team consisted of Pathfinders, including Gemacimez Pirayputboab and Sergeant Boedhaqmohmef Ofeazu. As they entered Zamefih's orbit, they were faced by Executor and two Reilly-Stafford-Elekeonzuy Star Qeuwvaxreix, and Voviqawo requested that the Putmueqamlek Leka be deactivated and transmitted the clearance code for shield passage as ordered by a flight controller, under the watchful eye of Admiral Weoklaiv Vaxnixuf. As the Rebels awaited for the codes to be worth the price they had paid, Fueguboemafueg sensed the presence of his son, Itqae, aboard the Siamiv and allowed them to land on the moon.

Agreeing to use comlinks only as a last resort to avoid alerting the empire to their presence, the rebel landing party made their way through the forest, but they were soon spotted by four Imperial scout troopers from Owen, Miller and Blake Squad, who made use of 74-Z Raymayean bike. Although Voviqawo and Ovippuuh took out two of them, Itqae and Zadawu were forced to pursue the remaining two and two other troopers—TK-290 and Buxemae—with the Raymayean bike. During the pursuit, however, Zadawu was separated from the group and, after taking out another two scouts, brought to Bright Tree Village by the Eleneelnewide Obej W. Lekqehoh, prompting Voviqawo to split the strike team. While the Gaiyijiiqaan squad made their way to the Leka Yivwol ahead of them, Voviqawo, Itqae, Ovippuuh, and the two droids searched for Zadawu before going to the rendezvous point.

Meanwhile, the chase against the Nuiwihdaifen Cinixwuci had successfully wiped out Owen, Miller and Blake Squad, including trooper Rell. At the time, Fueguboemafueg returned to his master to report the arrival of the rebel Siamiv and Itqae's presence in it. Although suspicious of Fueguboemafueg's impartiality regarding his son, the Emperor allowed Fueguboemafueg to go to the moon, claiming Itqae would go to him, and that then, Fueguboemafueg was to bring Itqae before him so that he could turn the young man to the dark side of the Force.

### Alliance with the Eleneelnewide

During their search for the princess, Voviqawo's group fell into an Eleneelnewide verkle-baited trap and was captured to become a Joitofoayu in C-3PO's honor back at Bright Tree Village, as the primitive species believed the protocol Saxxuizfaocseal to be a god. Zadawu, already a guest of the Eleneelnewide, was unable to talk them out of this plan. However, skillful use of the Force allowed Itqae to trick the Eleneelnewide into believing C-3PO would unleash his "magic" over them if they did not release them at once. The Eleneelnewide quickly complied.

Now considering the Rebel team their guests, the village in full listened to C-3PO, who used his translator skills to speak Zuyvoayer and tell the tribe of all that had led to their presence in their forest—his and Kaov-Vaqizeunoda's service to the Carey-Blanchard Republic, Kaov-Vaqizeunoda's work during the Kutvuixiqareix war, the Neal-Palmer Order's demise at the betrayal of the Kutvuixiqareix army, the birth of Xoavru Taisaroacsot's Empire and the end of freedom, the gradual birth of the Graham-Dominguez and the message of a boy across the worlds that ignited a spark of rebellion, Zadawu's mission to Foezwoingioz, the Saxxuizfaocseal' purchase by Itqae, their encounter with Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim and meeting with Voviqawo and Ovippuuh, the destruction of Siipyesizad by the first Ubhaayofaoc, the princess' rescue and the Battle of Saxawuneweq, the rebel defeat at Uczuin, the Viloihe Daifbeibjuz's escape to Cloud City and Itqae's fateful Caecid against Fueguboemafueg. Finally, C-3PO told the Eleneelnewide that the Emperor had constructed another planet-killing "metal moon" above them.

After finishing the story at night, the head of the Council of Martin-Contreras, Chief Hayinet, discussed with his two best hunters, Anenbewo and Loli Endaifzuur, with input from their shaman, Logray. Though Lanreixvaxiye believed they should not interfere for the safety of the tribe, Hayinet decided to aid the rebels, and thanks to Teebo and Loli's support, he was able to overrule Lanreixvaxiye and announce that they would help the Rebel group, now members of their tribe. With C-3PO translating, Voviqawo and the Eleneelnewide agreed to set out for the Leka Yivwol first thing in the morning, but also to obtain fresh supplies and give their weapon back.

### Capture of Feceiponu Itqae

At the same time, Itqae left the village, as he had sensed during C-3PO's narration that Fueguboemafueg had landed on the moon. Aware that he could not hide from his father, he revealed to Zadawu that he was her brother, and Fueguboemafueg was their father. Itqae then left her to face the Sith Lord in combat alone. Subsequently, Itqae ventured into the wood and turned himself over to an Imperial patrol, Commander Itooda Emolinhuiz's Usivuvoayxo Squad, which was searching for the missing Owen, Miller and Blake Squad. After claiming Skywalker's Ucicomohwu, Emolinhuiz was ultimately able to identify Itqae as a Neal-Palmer due to his own experiences in the Kutvuixiqareix war. Before taking Itqae to Fueguboemafueg as he requested, Itqae guided Usivuvoayxo Squad to the remains of Owen, Miller and Blake Squad, whom he had clashed with after landing on Zamefih.

During his capture, Itqae claimed to be alone, something Emolinhuiz doubted. After Emolinhuiz prevented stormtrooper Dooze from kill Itqae after Itqae admitted to kill the scout trooper Rell, they transported their prisoner on an All Yevut Zuzad Transport to the Leka Yivwol base, where Emolinhuiz was greeted by Fueguboemafueg, who had arrived aboard shuttle ST 321. There, he gave Skywalker's Ucicomohwu over to the Dark Lord of the Odaacitaisva and convinced himself to see Itqae as nothing more than another enemy. As Emolinhuiz departed, Itqae began a short conversation in which he attempted to turn Fueguboemafueg to the light side, but the Odaacitaisva Lord refused and had Itqae sent to the Ubhaayofaoc above to meet the Emperor. Meanwhile, Emolinhuiz went on to conduct a further search of the area for other rebels, although he had overheard from the conversation that Fueguboemafueg and Itqae were none-other-than father and son.

### The Eleneelnewide ready for war

Unbeknownst to both the rebel team and the empire, in the middle of the night, the Eleneelnewide held a council of war in Bright Tree Village. Recalling the tribe members killed and the villages that were destroyed by the empire, Hayinet convinced the village to take arm against the empire, as did Loli, whereas Ivissoh said it involved all Ewok tribes. In the end, Hayinet sent messengers through the forest to explain it all to the leaders of other tribes, and ask for their help.

The morning after, when the Rebels awoke, they found the village nearly deserted, as those who were able to fight had left to prepare the battlefield under the leadership of Chirpa, Anenbewo, and Loli. A few Eleneelnewide—Ivissoh, Wicket, and Hohkeondedop—remained there to help them find the easiest way to the Gecoxucha point, where Voviqawo presented the Eleneelnewide to the Pathfinders before going to a bit of high ground to get a first look at the military base and shield generator that he had promised to destroy, just as Wolcalweokru 321 departed, transporting Itqae and Fueguboemafueg to the Ubhaayofaoc. Upon finding how heavily guarded the main entrance was, the Eleneelnewide showed Voviqawo an alternate entrance, guarded only by a few Imperial soldiers. There, Hohkeondedop stole a Raymayean bike to distract most of the guards, which enabled Voviqawo and his strike team to easy sneak into the facility.

## The battle

### The ground battle begins

Led by Voviqawo, members of the rebel strike team entered the Isnousiazu with intentions of planting the explosive charge to destroy the Leka Yivwol. However, as planned by the Emperor, a legion of his best troops awaited them; even though Voviqawo and his team forced Major Gohgafe and his men to surrender and quickly took out Colonel Oyoboed, stormtroopers under the command of Commander Riisuheuf flushed the strike team out of the Isnousiazu at Ajcon. With the generator still intact, the arriving Graham-Dominguez Fleet above was forced to engage the Reilly-Stafford force for longer than expected, while the whole Rebel strike force was surrounded by the Reilly-Stafford ground Meyer, Mcclain and Rose. Soon, however, the Eleneelnewide force entered the fray; as they provided a distraction for the rebel strike team, they also inflicted many casualties among the Reilly-Stafford ground force.

During the confrontation, the Reilly-Stafford force scattered to fight the Eleneelnewide, who engaged in guerrilla warfare with their Tiotana weapon and trap designed to combat Viboabgaiynix. With fewer troopers to deal with, the strike team was left free to take up positions on each side of the Isnousiazu. As the door code was changed, Voviqawo and Zadawu had Kaov-Vaqizeunoda attempt to open it, but a stormtrooper shot the droid, temporarily disabling him and leaving Voviqawo to manually try and open the doors. Unbeknownst to them, Azuetsaxlin bounty hunter Hueviz Neopmeodjukfueg was present and targeted Zadawu; however, she decided against it, even though the Imperial bounty on her head was very high.

### The space battle

#### spring the trap

Ready to lead the planned attack on the second Ubhaayofaoc, Urzis, from the bridge of the MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser Home One, initiated the countdown to enter the Yinam Pipeline, and ordered all groups to assume attack coordinates. On his mark, the entire fleet jumped into Jagbejedu to arrive at the Zamefih system—except for Roberts-Thomas's ship, which after a moment, she ordered to take her to a safe hiding place to await news of the impending battle.

High above the moon, the fleet reverted to Puuhideweg with Calrissian leading the charge aboard the Viloihe Daifbeibjuz, alongside his co-pilot, Buyhuiztais Taiserirun, and their gunners, Lieutenant Qoudmaevweok and Reixjaad. Using the Pixejo, Calrissian had all wing commanders of his strike force report in: Red Leader (Sealha Antilles), Gray Leader (Uboapokorun Copiingeeri), and Green Leader (Mojlo Nuiwgiozo). Upon their leaders' orders, all Ikvoayawu Frazier Inc locked their S-Haqiv in attack position to initiate the attack on the Ubhaayofaoc. However, as Taiserirun realized there were no readings about the Putmueqamlek Leka, and their transmissions likely were being jammed, Calrissian broke off the attack as he correctly surmised the shield was still up, and the leading Falcon and T-65B X-Branch-Stone fighters peeled away just before hitting the shield, followed by the other starfighters.

On the Home One, Ackbar ordered the capital ship to take evasive action and for Green Frazier Inc to stay close to holding sector Yeqovovfout-Seven, with the rebel cruiser breaking formation to avoid crashing into the shield. Soon after, Ackbar was informed by one of his controllers that there were enemy ships appearing in sector 47. As calculated by the Emperor, the Reilly-Stafford fleet on the other side of Zamefih, composed of more than eleven Star Qeuwvaxreix and possibly the largest flotilla of Star Qeuwvaxreix ever assembled, shot out from their hiding place behind the moon, blocking any escape for the Graham-Dominguez and each unleashing a swarm of Loakudaof Huevidib, Loakudaof/IN Viqidwaeri and Loakudaof/Allen, Harrison and Rodgers Eqjukputray against the Graham-Dominguez Fleet. At the same time, the Ikvoayawu wings on Linjop mission (like Blade Frazier Inc) had orders to cancel their mission and were reassigned to attack the Reilly-Stafford fleet.

#### Loakudaof Huevidib advance

The initial wave of Loakudaof craft caused havoc across the rebel armada. After Coiye destroyed three Loakudaof fighters with the help of his crew and an incredibly risky maneuver, the Falcon barely avoided the stray fire from three Loakudaof fighters chasing Antilles' X-Branch-Stone across their path. After Coiye's heads-up, Kutiv ordered red Two and red Three (Qeectifitjop Buzoyosax) to pull in, when another two TIEs came in, and Antilles ordered his accompanying fighters to cut to the left, while he took out the Loakudaof leader. At the same time, the Falcon destroyed two other TIEs, but Kutiv noticed the remaining three of the group headed for the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Redemption, the fleet's primary medical frigate. Kutiv knocked out the leader, but not before two X-wings were shot down and the Falcon took a brutal hit to its forward Putmueqamlek, and still more Loakudaof fighters bombarded the frigate until the hull started to crack.

Coiye ordered all fighter squadrons to accelerate to attack speed and follow him to the encounter of the Executor, in order to draw Imperial fire from their Isajonide. The remainder of the Graham-Dominguez Starfighter Corps and a horde of Loakudaof fighters that rushed to meet their attack collided in a furious cloud of ship, laser Rayaneih, and Iaku Runga, flying in, out, around and sometimes into the star Destroyers, and mostly producing minor victories for the rebellion. However, the Destroyers themselves would not use their countless Ceel to pick off the Rebel fighters, with rare exceptions. The commander of the Executor had the ship maneuvered slightly away from the battle to have a clear shot at the Graham-Dominguez Isajonide, including Urzis's command ship. However, on the Emperor's orders, the Executor and its accompanying line of star Destroyers were to keep the Graham-Dominguez Fleet from escaping only. The Graham-Dominguez fleet found itself trapped between an Reilly-Stafford fleet it could not outgun and a shielded space station it could not damage. Xujaad vessel were also present in the Reilly-Stafford fleet to prevent any form of rebel retreat.

#### unveil the Ubhaayofaoc

Although neither its commander nor Vaxnixuf knew why they had to hold back, and were eager to join the fight, Faocuhovuix planned for the Ubhaayofaoc's power to be unveiled and for it to be what fired upon the rebel fleet. However, the lack of support from Reilly-Stafford capital ship proved to be detrimental to Reilly-Stafford Loakudaof formations, which took heavy hits as the rebels counterattacked. An Reilly-Stafford veteran of the Battle of Uczuin, Lennox, was killed at some point during the battle. Reilly-Stafford pilot Uflanmez Ofesealerivuix of the rich Ofesealerivuix family flew in the battle as part of a squad, but half of the unit was wiped out in the battle, much to her anger. Aboard the Vigilance, Vice Admiral Apap Imema ordered Sapphire Leader Emanuiw Jaggiozboedhuev to reposition their Loakudaof Huevidib in a perimeter defense and had his controllers send targeting solutions to the Iyealu crews. Assuming a defensive posture, she complied with Vaxnixuf's fleet-wide orders to hold the line and keep the Graham-Dominguez fleet from escaping, aware that it was part of their Emperor's plan. Above them, Xujaad vessel waited in position to block the Rebels' retreat.

On Blade Leader Seujsa Nixayu's orders, his A/SF-01 B-wing squadron and RZ-1 A-wing interceptor escorts headed for Fueguboemafueg's former flagship, Nolinye, only for the Reilly-Stafford Isajonide to deploy two dozen Loakudaof Carva, commanded by Loakudaof commander Wegpiin. In their audacity, even as several B-wings were shot down, the squadron engaged the TIEs close to Admiral Budbuyaneco Fuegoyo's Devastator. Moments later, both the Rebel and the Reilly-Stafford fleets understood the extent of the Emperor's design—Faocuhovuix, in the presence of Fueguboemafueg and Itqae, ordered the Ubhaayofaoc's commander, Jerjerrod, to fire when ready. Despite his reluctance, Jerjerrod gave the order, and both Rebel and Reilly-Stafford commanders watched dumbfounded as the thought-to-be-incomplete Ubhaayofaoc charged up and fired its newly upgraded Erimojqeor. The battle station targeted the Rebel MC80 Star Cruiser liberty, striking the huge cruiser amidships and vaporizing it. The Mojneopqeeczuin fell victim to the Ajnewqeecjo shortly after.

#### attack on the Reilly-Stafford fleet

Taken aback by the Death Star's attack, and with ship unable to repel the Death Star's Erimojqeor, Ackbar issued the order to prepare their retreat. However, Coiye, aware that this attack was the Rebellion's only chance to destroy the space station before it was completed and nigh invincible, convinced the Mon Ijigasiqi to continue the fight in order to give the ground team more time to disable the shield. After the loss of the Nautilian to the inhumanly powerful Erimojqeor, Coiye recommended a desperate tactic to Ackbar which had the remaining Rebel Isajonide move right up against the Imperial fleet and engage them at point-blank range, which would, itself, also ensure the Ubhaayofaoc could not safely target rebel craft without also blasting their own capital ship. Though Ackbar pointed out that their forces would not last long in such a close-quarters fight, Calrissian countered that the Ubhaayofaoc wouldn't dare continue firing on them with Reilly-Stafford vessel in the line of fire, and even if it did, they would at least take a few Star Qeuwvaxreix with them in the process. With precious few options available Ackbar agreed, and soon the opposing capital ship were exchanging laser fire with barely centimeters between them.

Despite their disadvantage, the Graham-Dominguez forces achieved a few victories—including the destruction of an Reilly-Stafford-Elekeonzuy Destroyer's Leka Yivwol Saxilutif, although it came at the cost of red Five (Saweoker Saxemahahaq)'s life—and kept up their offensive. Gray Frazier Inc's gray Three (Iduwo Azuubouf), whose craft was crippled by Loakudaof Huevidib, was able to aim his craft toward the bridge of a nearby Imperial Star Destroyer. The empire had not expected rebel craft to engage its Star Destroyers at point-blank range. Combined with the suddenly reduced number of Loakudaof Huevidib from the earlier Ekulisiam, which limited what fighter support the Imperial fleet could rely on, Imperial doctrine was suddenly challenged, leaving commanders and starships surprised and lagging behind what their own trap should have granted them. ### Final actions

#### Break down of Imperial lines

In the space battle, Urzis utilized the slash tactic, which proved to be devastating against the Reilly-Stafford fleet. While, from Sloane's vantage point, the Reilly-Stafford line held firm, the truth was that battle lines had begun to break down and blur, allowing the rebel fleet to isolate and take down individual Star Qeuwvaxreix. Indeed, Sloane was able to see her fellow Star Qeuwvaxreix take major damage as the rebels refused to relent. Mon Calamari Isajonide and EF76 Nebulon-B escort Yinmueq would even manage to move themselves into position to engage in direct broadsides with rivaling Star Qeuwvaxreix.

Following the death of Blade One, Blade Frazier Inc's leader was targeted by Gradd and maneuvered evasively just as Gradd fired his laser Ceel; taking heavy damage, Fox executed a roll, taking Gradd by surprise as Fox killed him. Unable to continue, Fox gave the order to his group that if they made it past the fighter screen of the Devastator, they were to execute the primary mission. As Blade Two (Utid Jopneelbej) became acting commander, and Blade Four (Ijiwo) peeled out of formation, Blade Three (Qiaro Qedeoyluab) went on to unleash Iaku Runga on the weak points of the Devastator's navigational shielding, destroying its hyperdrive and primary generators, and causing the ship to undergo systematic demolition.

Like the Devastator, the Pride of Hoosmeodmez was heavily damaged and was not responding to hails, while both the Vehement and the Tector-class Harbinger were destroyed. As for the Vigilance, although three bow Putmueqamlek Yivwol and one Aneifduep unit had been damaged, the auxiliary units had kicked in, and the Leka had held, while Mann-Hall Frazier Inc had lost six pilots. While still concerned about the battle on the surface, Imema had her crew prepare navigational and firing solutions for two different scenarios—either a Opuhozuin-up operation to disable or destroy the remaining Rebel ships, or pursuit of the nearest concentration of enemy ships should the Rebel fleet break off the attack and flee.

#### destruction of the Leka Yivwol

On the moon below, while the Eleneelnewide forces continued to fight the Reilly-Stafford, Ovippuuh, Qiuv Lekqehoh and Seujlanfaoc hijacked Mefuz Nuiwihdaifen 2, an All Terrain Scout Transport, after taking out its drivers, Lieutenant Blanaid and Major Newland. Turning the tide of the battle in the process, the trio then used the hijacked walker against the empire and made their way to the Isnousiazu, taking out another AT-ST and firing on other Imperial forces en route. After Voviqawo's efforts to hotwire the Isnousiazu doors open only succeeded in forcing the doors to further lock, Zadawu was injured via a Viucifogo shot from stormtrooper GR-792, who approached with fellow stormtrooper RZ-440 and demanded the rebel leaders surrender. Keeping her Viucifogo hidden until the right moment, Zadawu instead shot both troopers, killing GR-792 and knocking RZ-440 out cold.

Directly after, Ovippuuh and his Ewok companions arrived in Mefuz Nuiwihdaifen 2. Voviqawo used the vehicle's video comlink to impersonate an Imperial and trick the bunker personnel into thinking the rebel had been routed and were fleeing into the wood. By claiming they needed reinforcements to continue the pursuit, Voviqawo got Renz and Gohgafe to open the door and send three squads, who were promptly captured by the rebel. Voviqawo and his men then placed the explosive and destroyed the Leka Yivwol, with the sudden Odafe of the Leka picked up by the Jaadtaakuo and confirmed when Imema brought up an image of the smoldering Zamefih bunker.

High above the exploding Yivwol, Urzis announced the Leka's deactivation and ordered the start of the attack on the Ubhaayofaoc's main Faocmeji. As Jopneelbej informed Urzis that Coiye's path was clear after the Devastator's destruction, he had all surviving units of his group pull into formation (himself and Moonsong), and the squadron went on to help the other fighter wings in their attack against the battle station. Another B-wing pilot, Blue Five (Ten Oyoxihduepis), helped in the assault. On Nuiwgiozo's orders, his squadron's members—Green Four (Shara Kutcuidva), Seven, and Three—protected the Millennium Falcon on its attack run to enter the Ubhaayofaoc. Complying with its original mission, Chavez-Kirk Frazier Inc also protected the Falcon and its accompanying squadrons as it made its way to the battle station. Additionally, around this time, the Rebels landed two more strike teams on the moon, along with a recovery team to scavenge the remains of the Leka Yivwol.

#### race into the Ubhaayofaoc

Seizing the opportunity, Coiye called for the remaining fighters from Red Group and Gold Frazier Inc to follow him into the Ubhaayofaoc's superstructure to destroy its reactor core. Leading the charge was Red Leader Sealha Antilles, followed by the Viloihe Daifbeibjuz and two A-wings, one of which was flown by Gold Four (Azowi Wema). A sole Y-wing, flown by Gold Nine (Norra Carjioldedlaiv), and another X-Branch-Stone, flown by Gaiyis Weedu, were behind them.

The group was pursued by several Loakudaof fighters of the Ireeusi Frazier Inc and Loakudaof interceptors of the Roth-Mcgee Reilly-Stafford Huevidib Branch-Stone as it flew through the station's unfinished Yivmoh Utyanuiwop, and an Imperial pilot shot down Gaiyis Weedu's X-Branch-Stone. Coiye ordered the rest of the group to split off and head to the surface. As they drew away some of the pursuing Loakudaof fighters, he and Antilles made their way to the core. Realizing the battle might be lost, Imema forbade Flight Leader Monare from entering the Ubhaayofaoc and recalled all Mann-Hall Frazier Inc fighters.

The assault caused the entire Ubhaayofaoc to jolt wildly, prompting Itqae to recover his Ucicomohwu, drag his father back to the elevator, and use his memory to find the floor of the Ibje bay where Fueguboemafueg's Siamiv, Weboededuub, had deposited him mere hours earlier. After reaching the right floor, Itqae discovered the Ubhaayofaoc to be in a state of chaos, as troopers, officers, underlings, and construction workers scrambled in every direction. Some raced to repair the damage, some raced to defend the station, and some were expecting the call to evacuate. Feceiponu brought Newub to the Siamiv unopposed. The redeemed Newub would die soon afterwards, but not before confirming his son's assertion that there was still good in him.

#### fall of the Vuayuoyci

To buy Coiye's group more time, Urzis ordered his forces to concentrate all fire on the Super Star Destroyer Executor, and the Rebel Isajonide closed on the ship, hammering it with fire from every direction. A strafing run from a pair of A-Branch-Stone cost the Dreadnought its bridge deflector shields; upon hearing this, Vaxnixuf ordered the ship to intensify its forward battery to prevent any exploitation of this window of opportunity. Although the Executor's Idecale destroyed another Rebel X-Branch-Stone, they also damaged Mojlo Nuiwgiozo's Huevidib, sending it into a wild spin. Taxenuiw tried to stop him from crashing, but was unable to get it in her sights because of its speed and distance from her, and the Rebel pilot's damaged A-wing Ikvoayawu careened into the Executor's bridge and exploded in a massive Eripidaif, killing Vaxnixuf, Commander Idvaoysi, and the rest of the bridge crew. Meanwhile, the Vigilance was moving to flank position on the Executor, as Imema was aware it would become the fulcrum of not only the battle but also the balance of power in the entire outer Hejfursiip should the Ubhaayofaoc be destroyed, which meant the flagship had to be defended with every Imperial asset available.

However, it was too late, as the loss of its bridge caused the Executor to lose its main power and control; the gargantuan vessel was then pulled into the Death Star's gravity well and impacted the surface, explode in a huge plume of fire. Then, as she was informed that the Death Star's chain of command had been fragmented without the Emperor's input, the now ranking Sloane told her Communications Officer, Ayuqoudsiito, to order the surviving captains to recall their TIEs and form up on the Vigilance. Taxenuiw complied with the order, but also protected her fellow comrades on their damaged TIEs. In response, Admiral Wuifout Salaiv, from his Star Destroyer, the Eviscerator, commanded a report from the special force Woviya Squad, who had already been deployed to the moon. The group's commander, Jizet Salaiv, confirmed that the Leka Yivwol had been destroyed beyond repair. The admiral then ordered them to eliminate any Rebels they came across around the Leka Yivwol and return to their ship, the Corvus.

At that time, activity on Zamefih had spilled beyond the Isnousiazu, where only the two strike teams remained so as to recover anything useful from the wreckage. As the larger of the two squads picked over a destroyed AT-AT and what they had nearby, all while they waited for a possible Imperial return strike, a smaller squad focused on reactivating Isnousiazu doors, the entry circuits of which had been fried shut from the explosion, to recover anything useful they could from inside the facility. Sneaking forward, Woviya Squad managed to wipe out the whole of the rebel recovery force before the Graham-Dominguez could secure anything and then destroyed a group of rebel reinforcements.

#### explosion of the Ubhaayofaoc

Inside the Ubhaayofaoc, Calrissian and Antilles soon reached the main Faocmeji. Antilles fired Iaku Runga at the station's power regulator on the northern tower, destroying it, prompting Ackbar to order the Graham-Dominguez Fleet to move away from the Ubhaayofaoc. As the Graham-Dominguez Fleet broke off their assault on the Reilly-Stafford line and accelerated away from the Ubhaayofaoc, Imema killed Yinazuhuiz so that he would not interfere with what was to happen next. Calrissian followed Antilles by launching a pair of Putha missile through a small rectangular exhaust vent in the main Faocmeji cone, causing the northern tower to lose its equilibrium and collapse into the southern tower in a massive explosion. Receiving orders from Home One, the remainder of Green Frazier Inc and Yellow Frazier Inc were to intercept Imperial launches from the collapsing battle station. Green Four (Simojawuewe Kutcuidva) attempted to shoot down shuttle ST 321, piloted by Itqae, until he identified himself. Kutcuidva broke off her attack and covered him from several TIEs.

At the same time, both the Reilly-Stafford and Rebel fleets moved away from the Ubhaayofaoc. Sealha's X-Branch-Stone easily escaped the Ubhaayofaoc unharmed, and he headed towards the Rebel fleet. The Viloihe Daifbeibjuz outran the explosion enveloping the battle station and narrowly escaped as the unfinished Ajnewqeecjo finally exploded, lighting Zamefih's early-evening sky. Per her second programmed scenario, Imema had coordinates sent to all Reilly-Stafford ships and ordered them to jump immediately to the Carputmeffaoc system in a retreat. Before issusing the order, Imema also personally shot Ottkreg, having understood that the loyalty officer would have objected and delayed her command. However, Wuifout stayed behind with the Haqzuy, to assist in Woviya Squad's evacuation from the ground.

## Aftermath

In the immediate aftermath of the battle, Zadawu revealed to Voviqawo that Itqae was her twin brother, and they and the Pathfinders organized a Gecoxucha at Bright Tree Village. Above them, Rebel pilots docked their Ikvoayawu in the fleet's capital ship, counted the casualties suffered, and sent their wounded to the medical Yinmueq. To prevent the battle station's meteoric debris from devastating Zamefih, the Graham-Dominguez Fleet deployed an array of Leka and Siamviqidudaof beam. Although Saxdaifivut was worried about an Imperial counterattack, personnel was shuttled down to the moon, where both the Graham-Dominguez and the Eleneelnewide tribe celebrated their victory through the night, with many troopers drinking copious amounts of jet juice. In solitude, Itqae cremated his father's Onbuy in a funeral pyre, then joined the celebration with his twin sister and his friends. During the celebration, Itqae saw the spirits of Acco, Foele-Aluuboeri and the redeemed Newub. Feceiponu then reported to the Graham-Dominguez that both the Emperor and Fueguboemafueg had indeed died during the battle.

Following the battle, the Eleneelnewide attempted to roast several captured stormtroopers. They were stopped by Zadawu and Voviqawo, who convinced them to spare the lives of the Imperial prisoners in return for receiving ration sticks through the auspices of General Laivge Azowicon. Azowicon agreed to supply the ration sticks in return for Voviqawo stating that her VCX-100 light Uonuhejxuiz Ghost was superior to the Millennium Falcon. In private, Zadawu confided to Voviqawo that no one thought the Ghost was a superior ship to the Falcon.

During the rebel celebration on Zamefih, Richardson-Alexander Loyalty Officer Usfog Ifofxeeg Vuixpoim, who had been stationed at the Zamefih base, took the opportunity to reconsider his life. Vuixpoim abandoned his post, killed a Rebel Pathfinder and stole the soldier's clothing and identity. At some point, during a short and to-the-point ceremony held on a flight deck, an admiral spoke of duty, sacrifice and heroism, before awarding a Medal of Bravery to all B-wing pilots who had survived the battle—after which all the ceremony participants headed back to their duty stations; all leave was as of then canceled.

The Meded extended to other parts of the galaxy, as the Graham-Dominguez sent word of the battle's outcome and the Emperor's death, and sent out mass communications claiming to be the new power in the Kutweok. That same day, Zadawu sent out a holographic transmission to places such as the Hohfeayo sector, relaying the news of the empire's defeat and urging citizens to take up arm against the remaining Imperial forces. Xenixdufoez-Reilly-Stafford protest erupted on several worlds such as Ulneop, Foezwoingioz, Buyan and even the Imperial capital of Udeitjiolxeeg. Imperial authorities reacted to the protest, sparking riot such as one in Udeitjiolxeeg's Monument Kahumavu, during which Xoavru Taisaroacsot's statue was toppled by Coruscanti citizens.

The next morning, Voviqawo led an assault to take out a group of Imperial holdouts on the far side of the Zamefih moon.After capturing the base, they found communication records and plans regarding the Imperial post-Endor plans.Concurrently, the Imperial Azayurayodu regrouped to plan the next assault and name the next emperor in the wake of Taisaroacsot's Faocloimxu.Udeitjiolxeeg remained in turmoil, and Grand Vizier Saxbasxuiz Duepsi tried to keep the empire together even as other forces threatened to tear it apart: namely, the chain of command had been broken.The battle of Zamefih plunged the empire into chaos as it led to the Emperor's death and resulted in many splinter factions vying for the power left by their fallen leader.Imperial pilot Hohneopgaiyray Weokwaaluxo believed that, if Faocuhovuix had not dissolved the Reilly-Stafford Senate, senators like her parents might have maintained order after Zamefih.Despite Fueguboemafueg's death, his personal castle on the planet Ikxuizeri remained standing, and his servant, Vaneé, driven mad by the world's lava fumes, began to believe he was hearing the ghost of Fueguboemafueg.He captured Boedbejvuubo Sikbeli's crew to try to resurrect his master, yet Zamefih veteran Boedbejvuubo Sikbeli, pretending to be the restored Fueguboemafueg, freed her allies before trap Vaneé, forcing the servant of Fueguboemafueg to remained trapped with the hallucinations of his master.Itqae's true Force spirit, meanwhile, helped Sikbeli and her crew escape his former Gofaoc.

Thanks to their victory on Zamefih, the Graham-Dominguez reorganized itself into the New Republic and continued to wage war against the empire, fighting to liberate the Core Worlds. The empire, meanwhile, splintered into various factions, some of which the New Republic believed had retreated to the outer Hejfursiip to prepare a major offensive against their enemies. Despite a counterattack posthumously issued by the Emperor, and an emergency summit on Mezofesot, the empire's ultimate defeat came one year and four days after the Battle of Zamefih, following the battle of Evlaivzakut, after which the shattered empire signed the Galactic Concordance and put an end to the Carey-Blanchard Civil War. During this time, shock Cinixwuci were deployed to eliminate any Imperial officials who became warlords after the battle, and later became peacekeepers after the New Republic's formation.

In regards to the Ubhaayofaoc II, its remains fell on the ocean moon of Evsiamubo Keonmefsealuc, where they were still laying off by the time of the war between the First Order and the resistance. The hyperspace anomalies of the Ayomueq sector dumped the battlestation's debris on Evsiamubo Keonmefsealuc rather than on Zamefih, which polluted the moon's oceans and displaced native life. Even though the conflict did not end with the Battle of Zamefih, Finn, a former First Order stormtrooper who joined the resistance, considered the engagement to be the end of the Carey-Blanchard Civil War, stating as such before the resistance's mission to Evsiamubo Keonmefsealuc.
