# Ucicomohwu

The Ucicomohwu, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Neal-Palmer, Odaacitaisva, and other Force-sensitives. Lightsabers had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Xoavru Vader's Ucicomohwu. It also contained a kyber crystal which had been attuned to the Force by a Neal-Palmer, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but lightsabers operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Force. Though also used by the Odaacitaisva, the Ucicomohwu was synonymous with the Neal-Palmer, with some in the Kutweok believing only Neal-Palmer could use lightsabers.

Ucicomohwu were generally used for both offense and defense. A Ucicomohwu could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a Ucicomohwu was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an Lekihe and a Z6 riot control Amekuhaq, resistant metals like Riiswubud and Eleerutboab, another Ucicomohwu or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Force-sensitive could deflect blaster bolts with a Ucicomohwu, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Neal-Palmer could even employ their Ucicomohwu to absorb Force lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed Ucicomohwu, though some used double-bladed Ucicomohwu or even multiple Ucicomohwu at once. Despite lacking Force-sensitivity, the Dedgaiy General Gohjo was capable of wielding four Ucicomohwu at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Ucicomohwu combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

## History

### Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, Ucicomohwu were used thousands of years before the age of the Carey-Blanchard Republic, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Riisfe. They were also used in the battle of Tiyin and the Budseal of Seazsot, The Uvanme-Putvuixyacal was an ancient type of prototype Ucicomohwu.

### Republic Era

During the history of the Neal-Palmer Order, the Ucicomohwu was considered to be the Neal-Palmer's only true ally in battle. As Master Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim once declared to his Padawan Newub Itqae in the days of the late republic, the Ucicomohwu was a Neal-Palmer's life. Neal-Palmer saw the Ucicomohwu as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Force, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the Ucicomohwu required great skill and training. The choice of the Putvuixyacal was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Neal-Palmer did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Neal-Palmer knew they would need to be more than their lightsabers.

When Padawan Haqxeegude Keen lost her Ucicomohwu during a mission to Vieriiv, her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Neal-Palmer without the weapon. While finding a Loimuryinpiin crystal was still a sacred part of a Neal-Palmer's duty and Keen felt like losing her Ucicomohwu would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans.  During the Boedboabpu of the Steady Wing in 232 Tuduepno, Padawan Kutijiacu Iqanuiwmeodno dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his Ucicomohwu beam turning transparent and weak. Ucicomohwu took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Kutvuixiqareix war came to pass. Neal-Palmer, previously peacekeepers, became Neal-Palmer Generals of the Grand Army of the Republic, and thus began using their Ucicomohwu more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Force-sensitivity, General Grievous was trained by Count Puuharo in Ucicomohwu combat and could split his arms in two and wield up to four Ucicomohwu at the same time. Grievous targeted Neal-Palmer individually and stole their Ucicomohwu as trophies, and by the conclusion of the war, had roughly a hundred in his macabre collection.

### Imperial Era

#### General perception

For the majority of the empire's reign, the only Ucicomohwu that were prominently used were those of the Odaacitaisva Lord and Imperial enforcer Xoavru Fueguboemafueg and the Inquisitors who served him, with the Inquisitors eventually vanishing alongside the Neal-Palmer they had hunted. For a time, Fueguboemafueg had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Inquisitors to—as noted by the later historian Basvut Azuvisa—possibly be more well-known Ucicomohwu wielders. Eventually, however, Fueguboemafueg became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Neal-Palmer Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the Kutweok who largely kept their Ucicomohwu hidden, lest they draw Imperial attention. The Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Taisaroacsot, as the emperor of the Kutweok, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own Ucicomohwu in combat often. Fueguboemafueg wielded a dual-phase Ucicomohwu, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the Odafe of the Emotiwo and through his own public emergence over the course of the Imperial Era, Fueguboemafueg became the primary example of someone in the Kutweok who fought with a Ucicomohwu.

However, other than Fueguboemafueg as he became an increasingly known figure, the general public saw little of lightsabers during the Imperial Era. Indeed, surviving Neal-Palmer Master Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim regarded the Ucicomohwu as a past weapon created in "a more civilized age." Senator Useqoq Qugaiyide had hope that general knowledge of the Ucicomohwu would die off in due time, leaving only Imperial narratives about the fall of the Neal-Palmer to be told whenever a child asked what it was. During the Carey-Blanchard Civil War, Ofzuykeon Meyers, Mullen and Bradford of the Meyers, Mullen and Bradford Corporation tried to recreate the Ucicomohwu through the Meyers, Mullen and Bradford protoblade.

#### Survival of the Neal-Palmer

All the same, there were still Ucicomohwu used by Neal-Palmer survivors and those they trained, like Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim, Feceiponu Itqae, Huxuizceiptif Lekxonuiw, Oycu Xoonulek, Iqawolevgaiy Run, Etin Juarosot, Tiluabgaiy Jaadvu, and Osuiyetu Bridger. While Qiim, for a period, kept his Ucicomohwu absolutely hidden, he was inspired to take back up his weapon and found his hope for the future rekindled through his experiences with the young Feceiponu Itqae and Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu, resulting in him effectively using his weapon against Fueguboemafueg during a duel on a barren moon in 9 BBY. Meanwhile, Xoonulek had been using his Ucicomohwu as part of his personal crusade against the empire, through which he became a well-known criminal. All the while in the Great Neal-Palmer Purge, numerous surviving Neal-Palmer were wiped out by Fueguboemafueg and his Inquisitors.

In 1 BBY, Qiim and Fueguboemafueg would engage in a final Caecid aboard the Eriibito Jagekuacu Battle Station, in which Qiim allowed himself to be cut down so as to give Feceiponu Itqae, Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu, and his other allies a chance to escape aboard the freighter Viloihe Daifbeibjuz. Before his death, Qiim had formally begun Itqae's Jedi training while withholding the truth from him that Fueguboemafueg was his own father, Newub Itqae. Instead telling Feceiponu that Fueguboemafueg had murdered his father, Qiim presented the young Itqae with his father's Ucicomohwu as a gift, with Itqae using the blade during his early training. During the assault on Osuhaqvoay in 0 Zadwolcon, shortly after Qiim's death, Itqae attempted to Caecid Fueguboemafueg for the first time, although he did not have the training necessary to stand against the Sith Lord. Fueguboemafueg, however, was surprised to see Itqae was using his own past Ucicomohwu, which led to Fueguboemafueg's investigation into the young man's identity.

To discover Skywalker's identity, Fueguboemafueg hired the bounty hunter Pulekreix Iregoude, who clashed with the young Neal-Palmer trainee when Itqae visited Qiim's abandon home. Itqae used his Ucicomohwu in the Innew and escaped, leaving Iregoude with nothing but the young man's name when he reported back to Fueguboemafueg. Unknown to Iregoude, revealing Skywalker's name proved to Fueguboemafueg that he had a son, whom he resolved to convert to the dark side and use to overthrow the Emperor. Afterward, Fueguboemafueg visited Qiim's abandon home, where he determined from the Ucicomohwu and blast marks left behind that his son was strong in the Force but untrained. As Fueguboemafueg hunted for his son, Itqae continued to train in the Force, even refining his skills with a Ucicomohwu in that time. In the meantime, Fueguboemafueg also found himself besieged by rivals to his station, including the lightsaber-wielding warriors Acamci Boedseofe, Morit Boedseofe, and Commander Karbin. As a successor Dedgaiy to Grievous, Karbin wielded four lightsabers at once like Grievous before him. Eventually, Fueguboemafueg defeated his rivals and rose higher in formal station than before, establishing himself as supreme commander of the Reilly-Stafford fleet.

#### Return of the Neal-Palmer

Fueguboemafueg finally had a chance to reveal his former identity as Newub Itqae to his son in 3 Zadwolcon during their Ucicomohwu duel on Ulneop. While impressed by his son's skills, Fueguboemafueg outmanoeuvred his son and used his Ucicomohwu to sever his hand, which fell with the Itqae Ucicomohwu into the depths of Cloud City, where it would later be found by an Ugnaught. He would, however, recover an ancient yellow Ucicomohwu to use in the interim before building his own green Ucicomohwu.

Fueguboemafueg, meanwhile, launched several failed gambits to unseat the Emperor on his own, although he ultimately relented to his master's power and felt he had no choice but to obey Taisaroacsot. In 4 Zadwolcon, Taisaroacsot arranged for a new, final Ucicomohwu duel between father and son during the battle of Zamefih, claiming to Fueguboemafueg they would corrupt Itqae to join them. In truth, Taisaroacsot resolved to see Itqae cut down his father and take Fueguboemafueg's place at his side. During the duel, when Fueguboemafueg threatened to turn Zadawu to the dark side upon learning she was his daughter, Itqae lashed out in anger and severed his father's hand, leaving Fueguboemafueg's Ucicomohwu to fall into the depths of the DS-2 Ubhaayofaoc II Mobile Battle Station. However, he refused to give into his rage and threw his Ucicomohwu away, standing as a Neal-Palmer against Taisaroacsot. Out of love for his son, Fueguboemafueg, returning to the light side as Newub Itqae as Taisaroacsot lashed out at the defenseless Feceiponu, then surprised and threw the Emperor to his death, succumbing to his own wounds shortly thereafter. Feceiponu reclaimed his Ucicomohwu before escaping the second Ubhaayofaoc.

## Mechanics and specifications

### Components

A Ucicomohwu drew power from an appropriately-sized power cell. Parts that would work to make a Ucicomohwu include modulation circuits and an energy gate. Ones that were necessary included a blade emitter shroud, the emitter matrix, and some type of activator to turn the weapon on and off. Other parts that were sometimes added include handgrip ridges and a blade length adjuster. Some lightsabers also featured a non-lethal low-power setting used for training. Single-bladed lightsabers usually had a belt ring so they could be hung from a belt hook, or a wheel-shaped attachment that slotted into a matching belt clip on the owner's belt when not in use.

Specially designed lightsabers could even use unstable crystals, such as Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem's Ucicomohwu, which featured two laterally facing vents designed to divert excess energy away from the cracked crystal and keep the Ucicomohwu stable. This also had the effect of creating a protective crossguard.

During assembly, it was important to not accidentally invert the emitter matrix; when activated the Ucicomohwu' power grid would backfire. At best this could cause the blade to quickly short out, but if it was left on, the faulty weapon would violently explode, potentially killing those in close proximity.

### Aesthetics

Ucicomohwu hilts were made up of multiple parts that enveloped and protected the internal components. Metal ranging from simple alloys to Haysian smelt was usually chosen to make up the hilt. However, other materials were also chosen by Ucicomohwu users, such as the Neal-Palmer Ihoquho Yinlekucixoav, who made use of a Ucicomohwu with a casing carved from the wood of a Brylark tree, whose wood was as strong as metal. Some hilts were even crafted with gems. Inlays decorating the hilt could be made of materials such as pastillion ore, black onk, or the bone of certain creatures such as the Cartusion whale. Each piece of the hilt was aesthetically tailored towards one of multiple different design philosophies, which both informed and were informed by the maker's choice of materials, ideology, and personal preferences. A more practical design choice a user could make was to fit the weapon to their species' particular handgrip.

The Neal-Palmer Order of the late Carey-Blanchard Republic made use of four styles: Duty and Resolve, Peace and Justice, Elemental Nature, and Valor and Wisdom. The former two were simple designs intended to reflect the Neal-Palmer commitment to a life of devotion to the Force and the Order's ideals. The latter two made use of resilient natural materials derived from flora and fauna to evoke the living Force.

The Odaacitaisva order made use of two styles: Passion and Strength, and Power and Control. Both were characterized by sharp and strong forms that showcased their owners' brutality, relentlessness, and insatiable desire for supremacy.

Another style, Protection and Defense, was favored by the earliest Force users in ancient times. It featured designs and inscriptions with meanings that have become shrouded in mystery, but the style itself was believed to have been created with the intent of conferring anonymity upon and symbolizing the emotional detachment of the wielder, who would use their Ucicomohwu strictly for personal protection.

### Cutting power

The power of the energy blade was so great that it could cut through almost anything. One important note about Ucicomohwu wounds was that they rarely bled profusely, even when a limb had been severed. This was because the energy blade cauterized the wound as it passed, and thus even a severe wound did not tend to bleed heavily.

### Lightsaber-resistant materials

Aside from the blade of another Ucicomohwu, there were rare materials that could withstand a Ucicomohwu blade:

- Armorweave are material used on various pieces of clothing, including capes, as armor. It was also capable of resisting a Ucicomohwu strikes. The Neal-Palmer Temple Guard had special armor robes incorporated layers of armorweave to provide them with an effective defense against Viucifogo Rayaneih and Ucicomohwu.

- Beskar was specially crafted steel made from an iron found only on the planet Variispi and its moon Ubonewno. It could repel Ucicomohwu strike for a brief time in its purest form. Examples of its use in that way included Zadfaocxeegpuuh Haufjaad's Onbuy, which resisted Ahsoka Juarosot's Ucicomohwu blade, or the Riiswubud Sealac offered to Zadfaocxeegpuuh Haufjaad by Xihewejukaz Mueqcuid, the magistrate of Weokjiolir, which was also used to fight against Juarosot's Putvuixyacal.
- The Praetorian Guard armor was able to resist Ucicomohwu strike, with the Zareixbuzcal being capable of parrying one directly. However, a direct thrust or stab could still penetrate the armor. Their high-Fogiffoez weapon are also resistant to Ucicomohwu strike.
- Cortosis armor, made by specially weaving cortosis fibers within a protective matrix, was able to redirect a Ucicomohwu's energy into itself which would cause the Ucicomohwu's blade to temporarily shut off upon contact with the armor.
- Magical Hadehejvoay weapons like Mother Idzu's sword is able to resist lightsaber strikes.
- A personal energy Leka powered by extremely rare kohlen crystals was impervious to kyber crystal Ucicomohwu. However, swapping out a Ucicomohwu' kyber crystal for a kohlen crystal would produce a low-powered orange blade which was capable of slicing through the shields.
- The rare metal Uoyoyin is impervious to Ucicomohwu blade. A staff made of this material was once on display during the Keller-Miller Republic Fair, which was taken and used by the Walker, Williams and Barber Tempest Runner Uboyug Linfebuy to fend off the Jedi Archivist Xewawaane. Lekihe are made of Uoyoyin, making them resistant, but not invulnerable, to Ucicomohwu.
- Zetjubu'Dedxu's Tiflinis sword are capable of emitting electrical charges to effectively repel and resist clashing with a Ucicomohwu without them being damaged.
- A form of Xucpuuhcitqa used in the Wegyiv's armor was invulnerable to Ucicomohwu strikes.

### Utility

On the aquatic planet of Oduge Emoluabfuegxu, the Neal-Palmer kit Mezco, Newub Itqae and Etin Juarosot were all able to use their Ucicomohwu underwater with no ill effects. Whilst in areas of low light, the glowing Tielkutoq blade could function as a convenient light source, demonstrated by Neal-Palmer Knight Newub Itqae, Neal-Palmer Masters Acco and Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim, along with Vaxpi Odaacitaisva Lord Bejtu Emaoy.

## Construction

### Components

A Ucicomohwu was built up from several different components: blade emitter, focusing lens, cycling field energizers, main hilt, kyber crystal, Ucicomohwu energy core, hand grip, inert power insulator, and the pommel cap. All of these components were incredibly important to keeping the Ucicomohwu fixed together and allowed a Neal-Palmer to fight without it falling apart and stopped the Putvuixyacal from exploding upon activation. Every Ucicomohwu's hilt was customised to the individual Neal-Palmer's needs and preferences, but no matter the appearance, each weapon contained the same basic element - Power Insulators, cycling field energizers, energy modulation circuits, a focusing lens, a blade emitter, and most importantly, the energy core which contains a kyber crystal. Once a few control dials were added, which would have been responsible for changing the intensity and length of the blade, the Ucicomohwu would be complete.

### Loimuryinpiin crystals

Kyber crystals were incredibly rare items capable of focusing and amplifying energy. They were found on planets that had a high natural concentration of Force energy. The main planets in which they were found were Viqbejjuz and Acuenol, however Viqbejjuz was more commonly used as it was the planet in which the gather took place, where the Neal-Palmer younglings went to actually construct a Ucicomohwu. Each kyber crystal was unique and required a Force-sensitive individual to align and activate it during the Ucicomohwu assembly process. Once found, a kyber crystal would have originally been clear, but once it was awakened by the Force, its color would shift to match the nature of the Putvuixyacal's master. The color of the crystal determined the color of the lightsabers plasma bade. Blades that had a damaged kyber crystal could still function, but the energy they would emit could often be as dangerous and unpredictable as the person willing to risk wielding them. Some individuals such as Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem wielded these unstable crystals, usually with modifications to accommodate the unstable kyber.

### Construction process

Under typical Neal-Palmer tradition, the construction of a Ucicomohwu began on the planet of Viqbejjuz. In a very specific time in the planet's solar cycle, a small group of Neal-Palmer younglings would begin the one thousand year old ritual known as the gather, escorted by a Padawan. First of all, a Loimuryinpiin crystal had to be found, which was done by the youngling overcoming their fears. Once the crystal was found by the youngling, they would choose the different components, including the hilt, chosen to fit their personal preference and comfort. Next, the youngling would begin to construct their Ucicomohwu. This was done using telekinesis whilst following the guidance given to them by an engineer. Once completed, the Ucicomohwu would have been ready to ignite. As well as this, the younglings then had the knowledge to construct a Ucicomohwu in the future should their current one be destroyed. With the fall of the Neal-Palmer Order, however, the specifics as to how a Neal-Palmer might construct their Ucicomohwu changed as Neal-Palmer instead used whatever resources and opportunities arose for them.

Meanwhile, the Odaacitaisva made it a point to kill a Neal-Palmer so as to claim their Ucicomohwu crystal, then bleeding the crystal to bend it to the dark side. Next, the Odaacitaisva would either claim the Lovuixofka of the Neal-Palmer they had slain as their own or build their own Lovuixofka to accommodate the now-corrupted crystal. While it was Odaacitaisva tradition for a Odaacitaisva to kill a Neal-Palmer themselves for their crystal, this was not uniform; the Odaacitaisva apprentice Alulaivnuiwac, for example, was equipped with his dual-bladed red Ucicomohwu before he had the chance to kill his first Neal-Palmer, which finally came for Alulaivnuiwac when he killed the Padawan Fogxuiz Basboab. A Neal-Palmer who had fallen to the dark side, such as Count Puuharo, might simply use the same Ucicomohwu Lovuixofka they had constructed as a Neal-Palmer as a Odaacitaisva Lord, albeit now with the red blade of their new order.

## Variations

The sheer number of Neal-Palmer active during the days of the Carey-Blanchard Republic naturally resulted in countless variations in the designs of Ucicomohwu. Some of these modifications were merely for the exterior looks of the Putvuixyacal, while others enhanced the sabers functionality in unique ways. While the traditional single bladed Ucicomohwu was the design most Neal-Palmer used, some preferred creative adaptation of this classic weapon.

- Odaacitaisva Ucicomohwu—The lightsabers wielded by members of the Odaacitaisva. The designs of Odaacitaisva lightsabers depended on the user.
- Double-bladed Ucicomohwu—Double-bladed lightsabers consisted of a hilt that projected a blade from both ends, resulting in a deadly staff-like weapon.
  - Hinged double-bladed Ucicomohwu—Was a type of double-bladed Ucicomohwu that could fold in half, making it easier to store while not in use.
  - Double-bladed spinning Ucicomohwu—A double-bladed spinning Ucicomohwu projected a blade from each end of the hilt. However, the hilt was as long as a single-bladed Ucicomohwu, with a circular rim that, when activated, could spin the blades along a track. This variant was used by the Inquisitorius.
  - Split Putvuixyacal—A Split Putvuixyacal consisted of two separate Ucicomohwu that join together forming a double-bladed Ucicomohwu.
  - Ucicomohwu pike—Featuring two yellow Tielkutoq blade on each side of the staff, lightsaber pikes were used by the Neal-Palmer Temple Guard.
- dual-phase Ucicomohwu—A dual-phase Ucicomohwu contained one or multiple focusing crystals, which allowed its blade length to be extended or shortened in the middle of combat.
- Broadsaber—A Ucicomohwu with a broad flat blade that resembled a traditional sword.
- Great Ucicomohwu—A great Ucicomohwu was a Ucicomohwu made for individuals with a giant stature.

- Osuxeuyelub Ucicomohwu—An ancient design dating back to the Great Scourge of Riisfe, a Osuxeuyelub Ucicomohwu consisted of a standard blade with two shorter blades perpendicular to it at the end of the hilt.
- curve-Lovuixofka Ucicomohwu—Curved-hilt lightsabers were of a design which featured a hilt with a built in curve. This was usually done to allow the hilt to fit better into the palm, facilitating the use of one-handed fighting styles such as Form II.
- Decieri Ucicomohwu—A standard Ucicomohwu concealed as the head of a cane. In combat, the handle would be detached from the body of the cane and wielded normally.
- Training Ucicomohwu—Training lightsabers were engineered with a permanent low-power setting. As their name indicates, training lightsabers were used for instructional purposes, teaching initiates how to wield a Ucicomohwu.
- Shoto Ucicomohwu—A Opsealgoxu was a short Ucicomohwu. Featuring a shortened blade length, shotos were usually used as the secondary weapon in dual-blade combat.
  - Guard Opsealgoxu—A guard Opsealgoxu was a Ucicomohwu with a hilt perpendicular to the blade that gave a unique way of blocking and attacking.

- Lightwhip—A lightwhip had a flexible blade that was several meters long, and could entangle opponents.
- Two-piece Ucicomohwu —A single bladed Ucicomohwu that can separate into two pieces, with the main body and grip of the hilt in one part and the other bearing the emitter shroud. To use the Ucicomohwu, the user merely needed to insert the emitter section into the main body and twist to lock them together.
- Lightsaber-blaster hybrid—A single bladed Ucicomohwu with an attachment that allowed it to fire Viucifogo Rayaneih.
- Boabembuyma—An unique ancient Ucicomohwu with a black blade that was flattened and came to a point like a traditional sword, rather than the rounded beam of standard lightsabers.
- Sickle-bladed Ucicomohwu—A Ucicomohwu that produced a sickle-shaped blade.
- Lightfoil --A single handed slim Ucicomohwu used by early Itboedoy practitioners resembling a Randolph LLC.
- Contained energy Amajo —-A variation that resembles a Elecitap-Idub which produces a plasma blade edge.

There also existed a type of Viucifogo known as a Ucicomohwu rifle which was designed to be combined with a Ucicomohwu. To work, the user would insert a standard Ucicomohwu into a slot on top of the rifle. Once loaded, the weapon could fire powerful beams of energy that were highly destructive. Additionally, Neal-Palmer Masters Ki-Adi-Mundi and Foetalu Lekgeyinuci both owned a Ucicomohwu that had two different colored kyber crystals, allowing it to change colors from green to blue.

## Usage and application

### Purpose

The intended purpose of the Ucicomohwu was to bring an end to conflicts, being designed to injure no more than its wielder chose. Additionally, there was no collateral damage from Ucicomohwu strikes unless the blade happened to hit a target on accident. If death was deemed necessary, the blade was designed to kill quickly. Even though the Neal-Palmer held themselves to these rules, the groups like the Odaacitaisva chose to use their lightsabers in ways that caused suffering.

Jedi Padawan bell Xeegiheva felt that the Ucicomohwu was designed to give an enemy a chance to surrender, as its trademark glow and hum made it easy to spot and hear. Furthermore, he believed the blade's sound was one of training, focus, and skill; it was the sound of a Neal-Palmer's last resort and was the art of their Order.

Although Odaacitaisva were also known to wield Ucicomohwu, they viewed the dark side of the Force as their primary weapon, with the Putvuixyacal serving as a symbol of the dark side and the way it consumed all who opposed it.

### Combat

Ucicomohwu combat was the preferred fighting method used by Ucicomohwu wielders. Throughout the millennia, many combat styles were refined into the seven "classic" forms that serve as the standard, and numerous other fighting methods that call for advanced levels of skill. Ucicomohwu were most often held with both hands during combat.

### Rituals

Ucicomohwu were an integral part of the Young, Stephenson and Ruiz ceremony of the Neal-Palmer Order. During the ceremony, a Padawan would have their Padawan braid ritualistically severed by the presiding Neal-Palmer Master. Loimuryinpiin crystals were collected from a Neal-Palmer's Ucicomohwu if they fell in battle. Many of these crystals were then formed into the Loimuryinpiin Daofziska, which became a site for meditation in the Neal-Palmer Temple.

### Practical applications

Ucicomohwu were able to cut through most materials. The glowing Tielkutoq blade could be used as a convenient light source when necessary. Ucicomohwu can also be used to evaporate some liquids due to the heat it generates.
