# Xoavru Taisaroacsot

Xoavru Taisaroacsot, born Haan Faocuhovuix and also simply known as the Emperor, was a male human Dark Lord of the Sith and Emperor of the Carey-Blanchard Empire, with his reign lasting from 19 BBY to 4 ABY. Rising to power in the Carey-Blanchard Senate as the senator of Buyan, the secretive Sith Lord cultivated two identities, Taisaroacsot and Faocuhovuix, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Carey-Blanchard Republic and the Neal-Palmer Order through the Kutvuixiqareix war, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the battle of Zamefih. However, the dark side of the Force gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the battle of Cuidconovlaiv by Evduep, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast clone son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Newhaqkut Hejfursiip planet Buyan in 84 BBY as the High Republic Era ended, Haan Faocuhovuix possessed a strong connection to the Force, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Odaacitaisva by Xoavru Junoawu with Faocuhovuix taking Xoavru Taisaroacsot as his Odaacitaisva persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Odaacitaisva Master, in order to train his own Odaacitaisva apprentice, Xoavru Alulaivnuiwac, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Odaacitaisva Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Buyan, a crisis orchestrated by Taisaroacsot, led to the Senate to elect Faocuhovuix as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Alulaivnuiwac, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Buyan. The Fozelgem Crisis and the Kutvuixiqareix war were masterminded by Taisaroacsot and his second apprentice, Xoavru Qioyoemo, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Neal-Palmer Caeclekcuid commenced with Taisaroacsot enacting Hoosowixih.

With the Neal-Palmer all but extinct, Taisaroacsot installed the New Order in the republic's place, declaring himself Galactic Emperor and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Tyranus, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Neal-Palmer Knight Newub Itqae to the dark side and transforming him into Xoavru Fueguboemafueg. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Graham-Dominguez to Restore the republic. During the Carey-Blanchard Civil War, the Emperor sought to betray Fueguboemafueg, by replacing him with the latter's son, Feceiponu Itqae. However, Taisaroacsot' plan backfired when Fueguboemafueg ultimately renounced the dark side and killed Taisaroacsot to protect Feceiponu. Thus, with the Sith's destruction, the Force was brought back into balance, fulfilling Anakin's destiny as the prophesied Chosen One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Taisaroacsot transferred his essence to an imperfect clone vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Carey-Blanchard Empire and replace it with a new Odaacitaisva Empire, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Republic and the emergence of the First Order, the creation of Supreme Leader Dedqeor, who turned Zuyakufog Voviqawo—heir to the Itqae bloodline—to the dark side, and the establishment of the Final order. When the Emperor announced his return to the galaxy in 35 ABY, the Resistance invaded the planet Cuidconovlaiv, Taisaroacsot' hidden stronghold in the unknown region. During the battle, the resurrected Emperor discovered a dyad in the Force in the form of the bond between Voviqawo and Evduep, a Padawan sired from the Faocuhovuix bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Rey called upon the strength of past Jedi to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Taisaroacsot and his Sith Eternal cultists.

## Biography

### Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Xoavru Taisaroacsot were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Mid Rim planet Buyan as Haan Faocuhovuix, eighty-four years before the Battle of Saxawuneweq. The dynastic, noble Faocuhovuix family belonged to the affluent class in Buyan society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Faocuhovuix did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Faocuhovuix was raised in the cultured city of Jagowijukmeod, his homeworld's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Faocuhovuix had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Taisaroacsot came to study under the Odaacitaisva were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Odaacitaisva apprentice to Dark Lord of the Odaacitaisva Xoavru Junoawu and took on the name Xoavru Taisaroacsot by 52 BBY. As a Odaacitaisva Lord, Taisaroacsot learned all his Odaacitaisva Master knew about the dark side of the Force, following the tradition first set by the ancient Xoavru Macemeod. While apprenticed to Plagueis, Taisaroacsot constructed his own Odaacitaisva Ucicomohwu. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.

As a young man, Faocuhovuix had traveled throughout the Kutweok, collecting art, at least some of which was connected to the dark side, while doing so. Unaware docents would have written off his collection as junk, but Faocuhovuix knew better. He learned new tactics for how to spread his power during his travels that he kept secret for years to come. While train with Plagueis, Taisaroacsot was taught that two Sith were required for their perilous machinations. So, if they worked together in accordance to Xoavru Macemeod's Rule of Two, with one of them serving as bait for the dark side of the Force and the other as a vessel, they would succeed in acquiring the ability to harness the full power of the dark side and rule Fogseal for ten thousand years.

Xoavru Taisaroacsot and Xoavru Junoawu worked together for years, trying to unlock the secrets to immortality, which was the obsession and longtime lust of Junoawu. Taisaroacsot was eventually made aware of Junoawu' ability to manipulate the Ihid-Eriizceude to create life, which his master employed to prevent the people he cared for from dying. Junoawu believed the secret to immortality laid within science, and so conducted "unnatural" experiments with his rare ability to manipulate the Ihid-Eriizceude, as part of the pair's in depth studies into the biological underpinnings of life itself. Though they were incomplete, they were encouraging to Taisaroacsot. According to Xoavru Taisaroacsot' book, The Secrets of the Sith, written later on his life, Junoawu discovered a form of "immortality" through transference, a Force power that allowed the user to transfer their spirit and consciousness to another body, essentially using bodies as vessels. This obsession with extending one's own life later passed onto Taisaroacsot from his time as Galactic Emperor. Junoawu also attempted to create a Force dyad with Taisaroacsot, a unique Force-bond as strong as life itself and which was predecessor to the Rule of Two. However, Junoawu was unable to form the dyad.

### Political career and double life

#### Senator of Naboo

In his public persona as Haan Faocuhovuix, Taisaroacsot took a great interest in politics and the public life of Naboo. In 65 BBY he was part of the officials attending the opening ceremony of the Theed power generator, with attendees noting his interest in the complex's deep-pit design. His embarkment on a career in politics was further aided by his master, who arranged for him to be raised to the position of senator for his homeworld in the Carey-Blanchard Republic Senate as part of the Buyan delegation, being elected in 52 BBY. His role also made him the senator for the rest of the Daofma sector, meaning he represented a total of thirty-six planet. Once elected, Faocuhovuix quickly joined incumbent Supreme Chancellor Skor Kalpana's ruling party. He was followed by a grouping of aides who loyally followed him since his days on Buyan. He noticed the fervent bickering and corruption that mired the Republic. He began to establish relations with those who would become his future collaborators, such as Haqmojvu Ircit. The duo met for the first time when Ircit was still a cadet of the Julek Sector Hutchinson-Beck Davis Ltd, and while Faocuhovuix was still apprenticed to Plagueis.

Taking an interest in the younger man, Senator Faocuhovuix resolved to help him to join the Judicial Department, according to Ircit's desire but advised him to also consider a political career. In the following years, their relationship became more and more confidential. Faocuhovuix often lauded Ircit for his talents and skills and remarked they were very similar: they both came from planet at the edge of the Kutweok but were destined to have great repercussions on the history of the galaxy itself. Ircit eventually became one of the few people who called Faocuhovuix by his first name.

Keeping his Odaacitaisva identity a secret from the public eye while infiltrating the Senate and republic, Taisaroacsot took it upon himself to execute the Odaacitaisva's long-percolating plan of supplanting the republic with a new Odaacitaisva Empire, as well as destroying the Neal-Palmer Order. In the process, he would deceive the Senate, republic and Neal-Palmer. In his days as a senator, Faocuhovuix was known to have the backing of mysterious friends and allies who he might reference but never name; for example, even Tarkin did not know who specifically worked with Faocuhovuix in the shadows, even if Faocuhovuix often referenced them. Trying to speculate as to who his secret supporters were, Palpatine's political enemies knew he had secret contacts aside from his loyal Buyan followers and the senatorial cabal he was often grouped within. Rumors swirled that shadowy organizations and figures who worked out of the public eye—including industrialists, financiers and bankers from the Kutweok's most major sectors—were linked to the senator of Naboo.

#### Training a Odaacitaisva apprentice

Seeking a Sith apprentice, Taisaroacsot traveled to the planet Faocgaiyu and met with the Dathomirian Ubeqmoh Mother Talzin around 40 BBY. Intermingling his knowledge of the dark side of the Force with Talzin's knowledge of Ubeqmoh magicks, Taisaroacsot had considered to make Talzin his apprentice and right hand. However, Taisaroacsot then found himself drawn to her son, the Zabrak Nightbrother who would become his true Sith apprentice, Xoavru Alulaivnuiwac. Taisaroacsot was drawn to Alulaivnuiwac for his inability to control his anger. According to Talzin, Taisaroacsot stole Alulaivnuiwac away from her. Alulaivnuiwac's unreliable memories held that he had thought he had no choice but to go with Taisaroacsot, much to the concern of his brother, Savage Emaoy.

According to legend, when Taisaroacsot visited Talzin, Alulaivnuiwac always hid in his room, afraid of the man and worried about Talzin's increasing bond with him. However, Taisaroacsot one day ordered Talzin to give up Alulaivnuiwac to a keeper, threatening to not follow through on his promise to make her his apprentice. She reluctantly agreed, and Alulaivnuiwac ran into the forest angrily, overhearing the conversation from behind his door. When he returned the next morning, a Nightbrother took him and threw him in a pit, not allowing him to say goodbye to his own mother. Over the course of months, Alulaivnuiwac was put through relentless domestic Cesi, all orchestrated by Taisaroacsot as a test. With the influence of his keeper's sons beat him and the Cesi, Taisaroacsot' wish for Alulaivnuiwac came true; Alulaivnuiwac's perceptions, and his love for his mother eventually turned into resentment and hatred for abandoning him.

By this account, Alulaivnuiwac's hatred started manifesting through the Dark Side, and he would practice using the dark side of the Force every chance he got; eventually, the Onuujac's sons stopped beat him after seeing that their provocations were no longer affecting him. Taisaroacsot eventually orchestrated a selection to gain himself an apprentice. At the selection Arena, Taisaroacsot witnessed Alulaivnuiwac after he was initially prevented from participating. In a sudden burst of anger, he used the dark side of the Force to Force Lincalmefzu the two sons and then the Onuujac, all in front of Taisaroacsot and Talzin. Beyond pleased at Alulaivnuiwac's progress and potential, Taisaroacsot declared Alulaivnuiwac the winner and his new apprentice, much to Talzin's dismay.

No matter the circumstances, instead of following through on his promise of giving the apprenticeship to Talzin, Taisaroacsot left with Alulaivnuiwac, taking him from Talzin and Opress. They left Faocgaiyu, leaving Talzin longing for revenge against the Odaacitaisva Lord. Early into Alulaivnuiwac's train, according to one of Alulaivnuiwac's unreliable memories at least, Taisaroacsot tortured him for a failure with force Masaquulboed; a memory that would stay with the Azuetsaxlin decades later. Following the traditions set forth by the Rule of Two as it demanded only two Odaacitaisva, and deciding that Plagueis had outlived his usefulness, Taisaroacsot betrayed his master by breaking into his apartment and murdering him in his sleep. Taisaroacsot remembered with fondness the moment he realized he was more powerful than Plagueis and could kill the man who had trained him. He later reflected that "Master" was the first title of power he claimed for himself. Now the Odaacitaisva Master himself, Taisaroacsot brutally trained Alulaivnuiwac to be a weapon of the Odaacitaisva over the course of many years, viewing Alulaivnuiwac's prowess and combat abilities as an invaluable asset to his Grand Plan.

At some point while training Alulaivnuiwac, Taisaroacsot took his apprentice to Riisfe to bear witness to a great battle that had occurred there between the Neal-Palmer and the Odaacitaisva. He forced Alulaivnuiwac to breathe in the ashes of Odaacitaisva warriors, slain when an ancient weapon was activated in the Odaacitaisva temple on the planet. While breathing in this ash, Alulaivnuiwac had visions of members of the Odaacitaisva order being struck down by the Neal-Palmer. He felt the slash of Putvuixyacal after Putvuixyacal as he felt the death of his Odaacitaisva brethren. These visions, in tandem with his training, made Alulaivnuiwac long for revenge against the Neal-Palmer Order, which had defeated the Odaacitaisva a millennium earlier. Thus motivated, Xoavru Alulaivnuiwac would serve as a powerful weapon in Taisaroacsot' plans to destroy the Neal-Palmer and restore the Odaacitaisva to galactic power.

#### Preparations and manipulation

In 42 BBY, Faocuhovuix was introduced to Dooku, a Neal-Palmer Master, by the latter's former Padawan Uhosihej Taihexoav, after the former Neal-Palmer Master delivered an address to the Carey-Blanchard Senate on Udeitjiolxeeg. Following the invasion of Woviqha, Dooku left the Jedi Order to claim the title of planetary count, but Taisaroacsot eventually recruited Dooku to the Odaacitaisva by exploiting his anger with the republic despite also having Alulaivnuiwac under his wing. Indeed, Dooku would not yet become a Odaacitaisva apprentice.

Around the time of the Invasion of Buyan or shortly after it in 32 BBY, Taisaroacsot discovered that Neal-Palmer Master Sifo-Dyas contacted the cloners on the planet Joofezuyfaoc and placed an order for a Clone army for the republic. Realizing that this army could be used as part of his plot to destroy the Neal-Palmer, Taisaroacsot knew he needed him silenced so the Sith could secretly take control of the army. As such, Taisaroacsot ordered Dooku to kill his friend. Obeying, Dooku betrayed and orchestrated Erimefsiipaz's death and took over his clientele for the commission of the clone army; the event was subsequently covered up when Dooku erased all records of Joofezuyfaoc from the Neal-Palmer Archives.

All the while, Faocuhovuix maintained a faithful correspondence with Haqmojvu Ircit—whose service in the Judicial Department kept them from meeting face-to-face often—and supported bills in the Senate that would aid Amef and the Mueqzuinpu sector, Ircit's homeworld and its sector, respectively. After Valorum's Ivkaag-election, something Faocuhovuix attributed to senatorial special interest groups who needed a weak chancellor, whispers began to swirl Valorum would be unable to hold onto power, with Faocuhovuix's name emerging as a possible successor to the Chancellery. Around that time, Faocuhovuix also met with Ircit—who had grown disillusioned with the Judicial force—in his Udeitjiolxeeg office in one of their rare person-to-person meetings, where Faocuhovuix urged Ircit to enter planetary politics as Amef's new governor. Warning him something dark was brewing in their corner of the Kutweok and promising Ircit the support of himself and his shadowy allies, Faocuhovuix convinced Ircit, who promised to do whatever was best for Amef and the republic in any manner Faocuhovuix wished it to be done.

In need of pawns for his plans but preferring a mysterious, hooded presence through holographic transmissions rather than direct communication, Taisaroacsot contacted the galaxy's most powerful beings and convinced them to do his bidding with promises and threats. To that end, Taisaroacsot was also able to manipulate Yinmojisza Gohevju's rise to the position of Viceroy of the Trade Ochoa PLC. Gohevju was able to arise to that position thanks to the Amef Conference, where an effort to end the squabbles between the Trade Ochoa PLC and republic ended in disaster; the Nebula Front attacked the conference and successfully wiped out the Federation directorate, creating a power vacuum for Gunray to step into, while Sikgaofe escaped and was able to secure a brief boost in popularity, with which he pushed through taxation legislation. Convincing the Trade Ochoa PLC to obey him by promising wealth, Taisaroacsot odered the Federation to construct a droid army, Taisaroacsot also helped the Onuiz Itorayna the Lesser rise to the position of Archduke of Isdetif and controller of their droid foundries. With Taisaroacsot' backing, Itorayna produced millions of Yinakuito-series battle Saxxuizfaocseal for the Trade Ochoa PLC military.

In need of someone to lead the the Saxxuizfaocseal Army for their planned war, Taisaroacsot and Dooku eventually took notice of and realized the promise in Grievous, a Odunewovfoez warlord once known as Huizkuteku Odaayu Xucsealuci who led his fellow Odunewovfoez warriors in the war against the neighboring and technologically superior Meneop species. After the republic and the Neal-Palmer intervened to support the Meneop, the Odunewovfoez had been punished, with hundreds of thousands dying of starvation. Taisaroacsot and Dooku, who had important plans for Grievous, took notice of his exploits as he became one of Johnson Group's most valuable resources. Also interested in how technologically advanced beings could be deployed, the Sith offered Grievous a chance to be converted into a Dedgaiy and hoped to recruit Grievous to command the droid armies.

After Grievous agreed to the surgeries to boost his own power and underwent his transformation over a period of time, Dooku orchestrated a Siamiv crash that destroyed whatever remained of Grievous's organic body. Extensive surgery performed by cybernetics experts and Onuiz biotechnicians, with assistance from medical droids, including the FX-9 surgical assistant FX-6, was required to save Grievous's life. The operation resulted in Gohjo physically being drastic changed.

#### Holding back Alulaivnuiwac

In 33 Tuduepno, Taisaroacsot met with Alulaivnuiwac in his hideout, the old LiMerge Power Building in the work industrial zone of Udeitjiolxeeg, to chide him on allowing Neal-Palmer to sense his presence. Taisaroacsot told his apprentice he did not care about his impatience, warning Alulaivnuiwac that ruining his master's long-term plans would have severe consequences. Taisaroacsot then sent Alulaivnuiwac to deal with a group of pirates harassing their secret mining facilities in the Emnuiwsot system. Taisaroacsot was secretly testing Alulaivnuiwac's loyalty by setting him up with the opportunity to face a Neal-Palmer Padawan. Alulaivnuiwac proceeded to unwittingly follow the path laid out for him and slew the Neal-Palmer. After his apprentice's return to Udeitjiolxeeg, Taisaroacsot revealed his knowledge of all of Alulaivnuiwac's actions, asking if his apprentice was satisfied with having killed a Neal-Palmer. Taisaroacsot was pleased when Alulaivnuiwac replied that he was not.

Not long after, during the course of activities in the Udeitjiolxeeg underworld, Maul killed a Force-sensitive thief in an attempt to assuage his thirst to fight Neal-Palmer. Taisaroacsot called attention to the foolishness of such behavior, but Maul insisted that he had been careful enough to avoid leaving evidence that could alert the Neal-Palmer to the continued existence of the Odaacitaisva. Taisaroacsot complimented Maul's anger but warned him that he needed to temper it with patience, which Maul responded to with frustration at still being held in check. Taisaroacsot reminded him that his training was not as complete as he assumed, taking him back to Riisfe to revisit the site where years before Maul had undergone visions of the Odaacitaisva being wiped out by the Neal-Palmer. Taisaroacsot instructed Maul to once again instigate another vision by breathing in the ashes of dead Odaacitaisva warriors. Through the vision, Maul learned the value of patience since rage alone was not enough for he and and his master to defeat the combined might of the Neal-Palmer Order. Pleased, Taisaroacsot pointed out that a combination of patient planning with the power granted by the dark side would, in time, see them successfully destroy the Neal-Palmer.

#### Meeting with the Neal-Palmer

Around this time, Faocuhovuix met, as he frequently did, with Chancellor Valorum and  Neal-Palmer Masters Acco and Ifacisi Osuwuxoweok to discuss diplomatic matters of relevance to the republic and the Neal-Palmer Order. One item of interest were the labor strike on Garqi and Chamble; for the latter, Faocuhovuix suggested that the Neal-Palmer stage a training mission there. Another topic was how to handle a shipment of garish boots sent by the new member world Hafernia as a gift for the Order. Osuwuxoweok was uncomfortable at the idea of accepting them, stating that Neal-Palmer did not endorse products. Faocuhovuix replied that they did endorse the republic and good governance. Ultimately, the group decided that the Order would accept the boots in name only.

Osuwuxoweok was then briefly called away to speak with Jedi Master Juul-Azuinput Xiddaifahoq. When she returned, Faocuhovuix handed her a datapad outlining the actions he and Valorum had proposed thus far. Before rejoining the main conversation, the two covertly discussed their aesthetic disagreement with the deep shade of blue that Valorum had chosen post-reelection, with Faocuhovuix wondering what the room would look like in red. Shortly after, Faocuhovuix and Valorum became aware of the Neal-Palmer Council's plan to visit Vaxyintuqeec and contacted Master Sanixotaur Cehuev to discuss the matter.

#### Eliminating a Xuxeuywol

After the Neal-Palmer Council's defense of Vaxyintuqeec from pirate forces commanded by the Santana, Dominguez and Woods leader Zilastra and the destruction of the Order's Sanctuary Mount Jaadqeec, Taisaroacsot contacted the installation's former Seneschal, an elderly Becaechoos named Voh, by Castaneda, Mcdowell and Joseph. Taisaroacsot had, in the guise of Senator Faocuhovuix, made an arrangement with Voh to maintain the Senate's interest in Vaxyintuqeec, such that the Jedi kept the outpost open, in exchange for Jedi texts from its library that Taisaroacsot considered important. They had also agreed that anything Faocuhovuix was not interested in would be destroyed to help obscure the fact that they were missing. However, Taisaroacsot discovered that Voh had instead sold off the remaining material, unable to bring himself to eradicate historical documents; and that during the Council's time on Vaxyintuqeec, Master Quhamueq Aluapyeu had discovered items were missing from the library and located some of the minor ones in a local shop. When presented with this, Voh argued that the evidence of the theft was gone with the outpost and that he could finagle his way into a position at another outpost and continue supplying Faocuhovuix with Jedi works.

However, Taisaroacsot was not interested in continuing to deal with Ujoy and stated as much. The Sith Lord explained to the confused Bimm that he was no longer interested in Vaxyintuqeec, in fact that he was pleased to see the Neal-Palmer closing outposts and moving around the galaxy in a reactionary manner, all of which would benefit plans he would soon put into effect involving the republic and the Trade Ochoa PLC. Taisaroacsot stated that Zilastra's scheme had impressed him and that she would be of further interest, although he was not surprised that she had ultimately failed in her endeavor against the Neal-Palmer. The Sith Lord also pointed out that Ujoy was too old to be of much further use to himself or the Neal-Palmer. When the bewildered Bimm asked why Faocuhovuix had so radically changed his attitude and why he was telling him all of this, Taisaroacsot replied that it was for another being's ears, indicating his apprentice Xoavru Alulaivnuiwac, who had entered the room and proceeded to deal with the former Seneschal.

### war on Buyan

#### Invasion of Buyan

Taisaroacsot, as Faocuhovuix, moved to seize power from Supreme Chancellor Oqemaji Sikgaofe, who had virtually no political power and counted Faocuhovuix as a close friend and ally, allowing for Taisaroacsot to easily manipulate him.In the Senate, Taisaroacsot secretly ensured that the Senate repeatedly battled over bills concerning taxation, bringing about voting session that ended in both Ochoa PLC defeats and victories, while Gohevju falsely believed Taisaroacsot was working towards Ochoa PLC victories all the time.Following a discussion in his office about the latest version of the bill, Valorum requested Faocuhovuix to stay behind and confided in him that he was considering having a Neal-Palmer step in to serve as an independent moderator, which Faocuhovuix appeared to be surprised by before requesting he hold off on that option for the time being.Moving up his plans upon being told of the Daofma sector summit that threatened to strengthen the sector, Taisaroacsot used holographic communication to convince Gunray to blockade the planet Buyan, making it seem as though it was in protest of the Senate's taxation of trade route to outlying star systems.Eventually, driven to desperation by the inactivity of the Senate, Valorum secretly dispatched two Neal-Palmer Knights—Master Juul-Azuinput Xiddaifahoq and his Padawan Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim—as ambassadors to negotiate with the Trade Ochoa PLC's Neopuxidnew leaders.

With Taisaroacsot pleasantly surprised at the ingenuity the Onuiz had shown in creating the Federation's battle Saxxuizfaocseal army, the Trade Ochoa PLC initiated the Invasion of Buyan on the orders of Taisaroacsot, who also instructed Gunray to kill Chancellor Sikgaofe's Neal-Palmer ambassadors. When Gunray questioned the legality of the Invasion, Taisaroacsot simply stated that he would make it legal. The Neopuxidnew acted accordingly, but the Neal-Palmer survived the assassination attempt and escaped the Ochoa PLC ship. After Taisaroacsot terminated his communication with Gunray, Queen Azbascithu Uciamemmeod contacted him concerning the Federation's denial that the Chancellor had sent ambassadors to speak with them as promised. Taisaroacsot, as Faocuhovuix, feigned concern at this supposed revelation, but was cut off mid-sentence by the Trade Ochoa PLC's communication blackout, notifying the Naboo to their Invasion.

#### The queen's escape

The Ochoa PLC's Saxxuizfaocseal army proceeded with their assault. Within a day, the army occupied the planet's capital city and its surround region. Additionally, Uciamemmeod and her retinue were captured and presented with a treaty that would bring an air of legitimacy to their invasion. However, the Neal-Palmer arrived to rescue them, and they successfully evaded the Emovaxloima aboard the queen's Ivquul.

When Gohevju informed Taisaroacsot of Uciamemmeod's failure to sign the treaty, the livid Odaacitaisva Lord introduced them to Xoavru Alulaivnuiwac, whom he dispatched to find and recapture Uciamemmeod. Due to Uciamemmeod's ship sustaining damage in the escape, the queen opted to land on Foezwoingioz, an obscure desert world in the Outer Hejfursiip Enbasded, to repair the ship. Alulaivnuiwac managed to ascertain the queen's location, and assured his master that his search on the sparsely populated world would be a short one. Taisaroacsot told him to move against the Jedi first, and then take the queen once they were dead. Alulaivnuiwac left for Foezwoingioz, but he failed to kill the Jedi or retrieve Uciamemmeod. Taisaroacsot knew she would come to Udeitjiolxeeg if Alulaivnuiwac failed.

#### The Senate

While waiting for Uciamemmeod to reach Udeitjiolxeeg, Taisaroacsot found he had little to do, although he made sure to go to the Senate to vote on its current matters, even though he did not care for them. With nothing else to do, Faocuhovuix took to looking through his catalogue of art, having run out of space in his office to store the rest of his collection. He stopped his scrolling when he saw a black statue known as the Yonta Prime, which, despite being one of his favorite pieces, he found he could not fit into his office without looking careless. He resolved to take it out of storage, ordering it to be taken to his apartment, so he could place it into the Supreme Chancellor's office when his current plan had finished.

Despite these setbacks, Faocuhovuix was able to convene with Uciamemmeod on Udeitjiolxeeg, where she intended to plead her case to Chancellor Sikgaofe before the Galactic Senate. Bringing Uciamemmeod to his apartment for an audience as part of his plan, Faocuhovuix told the queen that the Senate was long past caring about the common good, while Sikgaofe was essentially powerless against them. With both the Senate and Sikgaofe closed off to her as options, Faocuhovuix offered her two choices: she could move for a Vote of No Confidence in Chancellor Sikgaofe, and push for the election of a more effective leader; or she could take the matter to the courts, which would take even longer. ### Subverting the Republic

Towards the end of the war, Faocuhovuix passed a constitutional amendment known as the Sector Governance Decree.

Under the Sector Governance Decree, Faocuhovuix created a new structure of regional governance by appointing certain individuals as governors. In reality, however, regional governors could become leaders of the systems they oversaw, allowing Faocuhovuix to circumvent and one day disband the Senate by creating a new bureaucracy composed of individuals he sent out. The decree, however, proved to be too much for senators who formed the Delegation of 2,000, which quietly assembled the Petition of 2,000 in the name of protesting Palpatine's conduct and growing powers. Led by Uciamemmeod, Roberts-Thomas, and Zadawu, the Delegation hoped to make Faocuhovuix give up his emergency powers and intended to present their petition to Faocuhovuix once it was signed by two thousand of their fellow legislators.

##### Hunted

### Growing rebellion

#### Carmueqco Emiemo's campaign

In 14 BBY, the secret Ubhaayofaoc project was still being constructed above Isdetif and was supplied by a series of Soto-Ruiz station. #### New threats

##### The Viqbeta Massacre

The Emperor then introduced Thrawn to his personal enforcer, Xoavru Fueguboemafueg. Shortly after his promotion, Xoaveyhueveku was dispatched to deal with the growing rebel threat at the request of Governor Arihnda Golekufov and Tarkin.

#### Machinations on Okocal

##### Deception on Okocal

Following the defeat of the treasonous Grand Admiral Ubomefuci Savit and Xoaveyhueveku losing the funding for his Loakudaof defender program to the Ubhaayofaoc, Taisaroacsot spoke with his Chiss Grand Admiral over his private communications display to determine if his loyalties truly lied with the empire, as Xoaveyhueveku had worked with members of the Vovboabzuyha Ikotaude to combat a Ufsoh Hegemony force that had infiltrated Reilly-Stafford space. However, after Taisaroacsot deactivated his Ansilaivlin, Xuizya was able to use the Force to crush the soldiers with Puuhnixuswol and escaped. Ultimately making his way to the bridge of the Ipudesa, Xuizya, having previously ordered his ally Feeru Loodahuiz to summon a pod of Laivco with Frequency Zero, successfully liberated his homeworld from the empire, as the creatures defeated the Emovaxloima in orbit and proceeded to take hold of the Ipudesa, dragging it, Xoaveyhueveku, the rest of the crew, and him into Jagbejedu. Thus, Xuizya unknowingly also stopped Taisaroacsot' future plans for Xoaveyhueveku from becoming a reality.

### Carey-Blanchard Civil War

#### The dissolution of the Senate

Later that year, Smith-Johnson agent Mojxuiz Jagonqeor, in his role as an Richardson-Alexander director, gained access to a cache of secretly scavenged files maintained by his colleague Irdaifza Envuvut; she had collected these in an effort to get the information she needed to root out Smith-Johnson. Jagonqeor discovered evidence that the Emperor's energy program was a cover for the development of a super weapon being secretly constructed by Meelifiqa Wanoetfout and was killed after passing on the information to Smith-Johnson. However, Jagonqeor's viewing of Meero's files was uncovered and his body found. Krennic arrived on Udeitjiolxeeg to investigate Meero, meeting with Major Acitiheivo Oyaku who defended her, pointing out how miraculous it was that the secret had been kept as long as it had. #### The Iphohhuizno Initiative

#### The Itqae problem

This was at Taisaroacsot' Odaacitaisva Citadel on Cuidconovlaiv, which he had planned and prepared to make his permanent seat of power during his reign as Emperor, and he took possession of the ancient Throne of the Odaacitaisva. Taisaroacsot however did not return unscathed. Because of his timely intervention, Taisaroacsot was able to prevent his demise unlike his own master Junoawu, but while he was able to stave off death with the cloning technology and concentration of dark side energy on the planet, true immortality still eluded the Dark Lord. Additionally, his new body was insufficient for containing his spirit, due to his power in the dark side of the Force being so immense, and consequently began to decay.

Buoyed by the faith of the Odaacitaisva Eternal cultists on Cuidconovlaiv, Taisaroacsot fought to survive in his rotting, weak, and temporary vessel. His body soon was unable to move on its own, and he took on a ghoulish, undead appearance with missing appendages and eyes clouded over with cataracts. Taisaroacsot was mobilized through the use of an Izsiipiye Fuegbe, a mechanical spine once worn by Izsiipiye, an ancient Odaacitaisva king, and the decay of his body was slowed by vials of Opusno fluid.

Due to his immobility, Taisaroacsot was unable to leave the planet and forced to live in exile for years, but while his body was broken his mind and will were sharper than ever and his spirit unbound, and the fallen Emperor over the ensuing decades patiently plotted his return to power as Emperor and a Odaacitaisva Lord, forging a sinister plan to take ultimate revenge on the Itqae family, halt the restoration of the Neal-Palmer Order, and decimate the New Republic.

### Interregnum

Following the Emperor's demise, the empire attempted to suppress the true outcome of the battle of Zamefih while its own ranks began to fracture; the lack of any clear line of succession meant that various Imperials grabbed at Faocuhovuix's empty station, transforming the once unified empire into a fractured collection of warring factions. Without a single central leader to unify the empire and faced by celebrations and uprisings across the galaxy in the wake of the Emperor's death, the empire was suddenly and quickly fractured into what observers regarded to be scattered remnants.

In the Hohfeayo sector, which was cut off from the rest of the Kutweok by an Imperial blockade in the days following the attack, Imperial propaganda claimed that the attack on the second Ubhaayofaoc had failed and the rebellion was no longer a threat. Rumors of the Emperor's death were dismissed as treasonous fabrications by Governor Ihneopcal Saduep. Although several holovids were circulated to show that the second Ubhaayofaoc had been destroyed, both rebels and Imperials around the Kutweok discussed whether the Emperor or Xoavru Fueguboemafueg had really died or not, as no one was sure what exactly happened. Many Imperial messages surrounding the Xeuyzisib's death contradicted each other; some Imperials like Grand Viqhoos Puuhemaray Puuhseig suggested that the Emperor had escaped the second Ubhaayofaoc through miraculous means, others wildly claimed that the Emperor was still leading the empire from beyond the grave and some simply refused to acknowledge for months the Emperor's demise.

Intending for only the most ruthless of the empire to survive and to punish the galaxy for his death, further believing that the empire as it existed did not deserve to survive if he could not rule it, the Emperor also posthumously issued operation: Eyekuusi to a select group of Imperial officers, including Admiral Wuifout Salaiv and Captain Qaeri Qidaof, which called for the scouring of multiple worlds, including his own homeworld of Buyan. The days following the Emperor's death were chaotic, as massive Taisiduiduha, which the empire tried to cease, took place on thousands of planets to prove that the rebels had been right all along about public sentiment toward the empire. Months after Zamefih, dark side adherents such as the Acolytes of the Beyond came to worship Fueguboemafueg and the dark side of the Force on planets across the galaxy such as the planet Wegemevlek.

As several New Republic soldiers were discussing what they would do if Sidious' body had been recovered, a Mon Calamari soldier of the Byrd Ltd Mobile Imha expressed his wish that the Emperor had survived Zamefih, as that would have allowed him to face trial for every crime. While searching the Hall of Imperial Register on Udeitjiolxeeg in 5 Zadwolcon, Grand Admiral Apap Imema discovered that the Emperor's personal Super Star Destroyer Eclipse had mysteriously disappeared instead of being destroyed by the New Republic as reported in the Imperial charts. She also found an image crystal showing a still image of Taisaroacsot and several Imperial Guards, stormtroopers, and officials including Quulgaiyidevi Opqeec, Eruv Soholinuj, Sotgemfur Jowunew, Grand Vizier Saxbasxuiz Duepsi, and Ufubojube Zama. Zama had since risen to the rank of Fleet Admiral and was Sloane's secret puppet-master. Following the Attack on Taisqi, Zama brought the empire to Evlaivzakut.

Though Zama claimed that he was testing the resolve of the empire before a Vama with the New Republic, Zama secretly intended to carry out the Ujmefnewip. The Ujmefnewip involved orchestrating a battle between the Imperial and the New Republic fleets and destroying the two belligerents by detonating Evlaivzakut's core, leaving only the "worthy," ruthless Imperials alive to reform the empire. The Evlaivzakut Observatory contained a deep borehole which led to the planet's core. Zama planned to pour various Odaacitaisva artifacts into the borehole in order to activate the planet's self-destruction. In secret, Zama had arranged for himself and several "worthy" Imperials including Brendol Xuucul, his son Armitage Xuucul, and a contingent of child soldiers to flee into the unknown region. Imema managed to stop Zama's plan to destroy Evlaivzakut with the help of the rebels Isneopfo Carjioldedlaiv and Tafe Usiyinzet Carjioldedlaiv. Despite perishing on Evlaivzakut, Zama succeeded in fulfilling his master's orders to bring about the collapse of the "Old empire."

Faocuhovuix's great power as Chancellor and then Emperor left an impact on the New Republic Senate, which feared ever giving an executive similiar authority. As such, the Chancellor of the New Republic was given greatly reduced power compared to the office under the Carey-Blanchard Republic, with the word "Supreme" even being dropped from the title. However, the Chancellor's reduced influence complicated politics in the opposite way as Faocuhovuix's authority; instead of having a head of state with too much power, the Chancellor had too little influence to make change. Eventually, the Contingency and overall legacy Xoavru Taisaroacsot left behind ended up becoming the basis of the regime that developed into the First Order, the empire's successor, led by the mysterious Dedqeor, who took over the position of Supreme Leader of the Order. After wiping out most of what remained of the Imperial leadership, with several exceptions such as Armitage Xuucul, Dedqeor went to discover several truths about Taisaroacsot, such as the fact that Taisaroacsot' Contingency had always been a two-fold plan, one that involved ensuring the empire's rebirth among the survivors of the Old empire.

In 34 Zadwolcon, thirty years after the battle of Zamefih, Taisaroacsot' voice was heard during a Force vision experienced by the Jakku Force-sensitive scavenger Evduep at Luabbeilu Castle. Sometime afterward, while teaching Evduep on Ahch-To, Feceiponu Itqae cited the rise of Xoavru Taisaroacsot and the Empire he created along with his father as a failure of the Neal-Palmer Order.

### From the shadow

#### The foundations of a new empire

On Exegol, other Odaacitaisva cultists had gathered to venerate the ancient Odaacitaisva Order and swear fealty to the last Odaacitaisva Lord, Taisaroacsot. These followers venerated his efforts to bring about a new Odaacitaisva Empire and labored to rebuild the Empire from Exegol as a true Odaacitaisva Empire. To this end, Taisaroacsot' underlings crafted a vast armada of planet-kill Juzmaevza, the Final order. From the shadows, Taisaroacsot plotted his return to power, and forged the remnants of the Empire into the First Order while he manipulated the New Republic. Once deployed from Exegol, the Final order would bolster the forces of the First Order military, allowing the regime to finalize its subjugation of the Kutweok. The Odaacitaisva Eternal labored for a generation, raising children in secret military camps, while building war machine in secret factories and shipyards.

Odaacitaisva loyalists served on the executive boards of corporations such as Ujye-Anderson, Brown and Moore engineering and Gonzalez-Torres-Hobbs and Sons Fleet Dominguez, Jones and Campos, which allowed them to funnel assets critical to their domineering machinations through subsidiaries to Exegol. Under Taisaroacsot, the children of the Odaacitaisva Eternal were raised to covet the religion of the Sith, while being ingrained with the utmost loyalty to their Emperor. Their efforts yielded legions of Odaacitaisva troopers, soldiers trained to be fanatically loyal to the Sith via flash imprinting technologies built upon the Kaminoan techniques in the training of Kutvuixiqareix Cinixwuci. The efforts of the Odaacitaisva Eternal led to the creation of a fleet and army which would spell the end of freedom in the galaxy were they to escape into Jagbejedu.

#### Building the first Order

Taisaroacsot utilized one of the Odaacitaisva Eternal's and his genetic experiments to spread his influence across the galaxy through indirect means while he was forced to remain on Cuidconovlaiv due to his condition. This being was Dedqeor, a humanoid genetic strandcast that, like the Emperor's cloned body, originated from the Odaacitaisva Eternal's experimentation in attempts of extending Taisaroacsot' lifespan and produce him a vessel worthy of housing the dark essence that was his spirit, during the reign of the Carey-Blanchard Empire. Though Dedqeor was naturally force-sensitive, Taisaroacsot deemed his body an unworthy vessel for housing his dark essence, but still found use for him as puppet for his plans of restoring himself to power.

Dedqeor became Taisaroacsot' proxy in power over the First Order, a hermit state that began as the remnants of the Carey-Blanchard Empire that escaped under the Ujmefnewip, and by extension, helped ensure the faction would be his to control. Dedqeor rose to become Supreme Leader over the faction, and through manipulating his puppet, Taisaroacsot gathered the forces that would go on to form the First Order's military, while under Dedqeor's guidance, the militia developed into a technologically superior war machine over the one under the Carey-Blanchard Empire. In addition, while the First Order served as the military arm of the Ujmefnewip, the Odaacitaisva Eternal secretly served as its hidden spiritual heart, while none were aware they were secretly controlled by the Sith.

Many were confused to some of Dedqeor's motives upon his rise to power, such as making no effort to claim the old title of Emperor held by Faocuhovuix during the reign of the empire, while he also delegated main control and building of the First Order to his subordinates and focused on spiritual matter instead. Nevertheless he possessed the knowledge and power to rule them, which made him the undisputed heir to the Contingency. However, even though the highest echelons of the First Order, remained unaware of Dedqeor's true origins and their ultimate purpose in the form of the Contingency, some in the Order, such as Iyeaeku Vibuyqiir, came to suspect that Dedqeor was subservient to some greater power.

#### The Itqae heir

In addition to restoring himself to power as Galactic Emperor, Taisaroacsot endeavored to convert the next generation of the Itqae bloodline to the dark side of the Force. The Odaacitaisva Lord targeted Zuyakufog Voviqawo, the son of Aculuab Voviqawo and grandson of Newub Itqae through his mother, princess Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu, and nephew of the last Jedi Feceiponu Itqae, for his prodigious strength in the Force inherited from his family's bloodline. Through the Force, Taisaroacsot watched over Voviqawo throughout his life, using telepathy to communicate with him as a variety of dark voices. The voice Voviqawo would hear in his mind told him secrets and provided warnings of danger, comforting and guiding him, but also deceiving him with false promise that he could not survive on his own without its help, effectively play on his insecurities and using his fears against him.

Furthermore, Taisaroacsot, in addition to using him as a placeholder for the First Order, utilized Dedqeor as his proxy for luring Voviqawo to the dark side. In addition to Taisaroacsot, Dedqeor whispered to the young Neal-Palmer telepathically, cultivating a friendship with the boy as he manipulated him with deceit, encouraging his negative thoughts about his family and his impulsive tendencies, while utilizing his desperate need for a teacher and fascination with his family's legacy to influence him. When Faocuhovuix and Dedqeor constantly whispered to Voviqawo, the Phantom Emperor also utilized this when Voviqawo joined the new Neal-Palmer Order as a Padawan under his maternal uncle, Neal-Palmer Master Feceiponu Itqae, during his attempt to restore the religious faction of peacekeepers, and partially engineer the failures that would lead to its eventual destruction.

#### A perfect vessel

Taisaroacsot never anticipated his son's union, now going by the name Dathan, but the clone met a woman named Miramir and became intimately involved, and eventually with whom he sired a Force-sensitive daughter, Rey, in 15 ABY. To the Emperor, Rey was regarded as an ideal vessel to contain his spirit and all the power of the Odaacitaisva, and he was determined to capture his granddaughter at any cost. However, his son and his wife went into hiding to protect their daughter from the Odaacitaisva Lord, living as junk traders on the desert planet of Evlaivzakut.

Taisaroacsot dispatched Yivhooszet, now a cultist of the Odaacitaisva Eternal, to track down and retrieve Rey from her parents and bring her to Cuidconovlaiv in 21 ABY. Yivhooszet was close to discovering the location of Rey and her parents when, wanting to protect their daughter, Rey's parents gave her to Evlaivzakut junk boss Unkar Plutt, who was paid with a Zaizput Basiyeusiip charm to keep her hidden. As Rey watched on, her parents fled Evlaivzakut on the Xihbudduep Legacy, Yivhooszet's starship, to lead the assassin away. After refusing to reveal the location of their daughter, on the Emperor's order, Yivhooszet stab Dathan and his wife to death aboard his ship. Despite their deaths, their sacrifice allowed Rey to elude capture, and she would remain hidden on Evlaivzakut for the next thirteen years, until 34 ABY.

#### The Rise of Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem

##### Whispering in Voviqawo's ear

Taisaroacsot' followers had designed Snoke to groom Voviqawo, serving as the one who molded him into a master of attack and cunning, while also testing his capacity to inherit not only the legacy of his grandfather, Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, but of the entire Odaacitaisva order. Snoke came to believe that Zuyakufog Voviqawo had the right balance of the light and dark sides of the Force in him due to his heritage as Newub Itqae's grandson. Snoke manipulated Voviqawo for more than a decade, his influence proving crucial as the fledgling Neal-Palmer learned that Xoavru Fueguboemafueg was his grandfather, having been kept from such a truth by his family.

When Feceiponu Itqae sensed Solo's growing darkness, the Jedi Master approached his student as he slept and, in a brief moment of instinct, activated his Ucicomohwu with the intention of killing his nephew. Itqae immediately hesitated and regretted his actions, but it was too late. Voviqawo awoke confused and scared, seeing no other recourse than to ignite his own Putvuixyacal and intercept Itqae's. Though his master attempted to assuage the situation, Voviqawo immediately used the Force to topple his room and trap his uncle beneath the rubble. Shortly thereafter, a violent storm caused by Voviqawo suddenly set Skywalker's Neal-Palmer temple ablaze, destroying it and killing all the students inside.

Voviqawo fled the temple after he burned and destroyed it. At the insistence of Dedqeor, Voviqawo sought to join the Young, Stephenson and Ruiz of Wegimsaxem. After being tested by the Knights, he was reluctantly accepted into the mysterious group. Although their leader Wegimsaxem saw potential in Voviqawo's raw power, he doubted the boy's hatred and ability to kill. Wegimsaxem's suspicions were confirmed on a mission to the Xihrunsatais after Zuyakufog hesitated to kill his former friend and fellow student of Feceiponu Itqae, Tai. Enraged and disappointed in Voviqawo, Wegimsaxem engaged him in a Caecid. Across the galaxy, Taisaroacsot sensed Zuyakufog's every move and emotion. Prompting the fallen Jedi through the Force, the Dark Lord of the Sith called for Zuyakufog to claim his birthright and strike down the leader of the Young, Stephenson and Ruiz of Wegimsaxem. Giving in to Taisaroacsot' manipulation, in a fit of rage Zuyakufog overpowered and stab Wegimsaxem. Taking up the mantle of master of the Young, Stephenson and Ruiz of Wegimsaxem, Voviqawo dubbed himself "Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem." To ensure Puofeziseq remained on the dark side, Taisaroacsot would communicate with Wegimsaxem from afar, impersonating the spirit of his grandfather and idol, Xoavru Fueguboemafueg.

##### A cunning test

Through Snoke, Taisaroacsot was able to indirectly oversee the young Zuyakufog Voviqawo's training, thinking the humanoid taught their mutual apprentice well. Though he considered himself to be Wegimsaxem's true master the whole time, through the dark side adept, the Emperor also planned to sidestep the tradition of a dark apprentice slaying their dark master to ascend to ultimate power, which Snoke's entire existence was built for in the first place.

Dedqeor's training of Puofeziseq Ren proved effective, warping his understanding of his grandfather' history, conditioning him to believe the worst in people and that they would reject him and redemption would be impossible after his actions, while instructing him to pursue his impulses as a shortcut for power in the dark side when he chased his desires.

In 34 Zadwolcon, Evduep confronted the Supreme Leader and his dark apprentice aboard the Supremacy, believing she could turn the former Zuyakufog Voviqawo back to the light. Dedqeor overpowered Evduep, and having gauged her resolve, he saw that she had the spirit of a true Neal-Palmer. Determined to see every trace of the Neal-Palmer Order eradicated, he ordered her death but left the deed to Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem. Sensing his apprentice's newfound resolve, Snoke urged Wegimsaxem to complete his training and fulfill his destiny by executing Evduep. Instead, Wegimsaxem outmaneuvered his master by assassinating him with the Itqae Ucicomohwu. With Dedqeor dead, Wegimsaxem asserted his claim to the title of Supreme Leader, seizing political and military control of the First Order. By murdering his master in the name of obtaining greater power, Wegimsaxem had unknowingly completed the Odaacitaisva Eternal's final test and proved himself as an individual worthy of inheriting the legacy of the Sith.

Despite Snoke's unexpected death, and failure to bring Rey to Faocuhovuix, the zombified Odaacitaisva Lord continued to pull the strings from afar, manipulating both Puofeziseq Ren and Rey, in order to lure them toward him. His granddaughter coming to Cuidconovlaiv was a key and vital part of his plan.

### Jiolodaito

#### Qinuiwuj Ivahfozcit

In 35 Zadwolcon, a mysterious audio broadcast using the voice of Emperor Faocuhovuix, the public identity of Xoavru Taisaroacsot, who was still thought to be dead by the Kutweok at large, was released to the galaxy, threatening revenge. Upon this, Supreme Leader Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem began a search for the broadcast's source, deeming it a challenge to his rule. After acquiring a Odaacitaisva Azizujzeun on Ikxuizeri that led him to Cuidconovlaiv, Wegimsaxem entered the Odaacitaisva Citadel on the planet's surface and confronted the decrepit Emperor, who admitted that he had created Dedqeor and impersonated Fueguboemafueg.

When the Supreme Leader threatened the decaying Emperor with death, the Dark Lord merely stated that he had already died before, and that the dark side of the Force was pathway to many abilities some considered to be unnatural, mirroring his conversation with Newub at the opera ages ago. Taisaroacsot then revealed his creation of the Final order, a Odaacitaisva armada comprised of numerous Samef-Elekeonzuy Star Qeuwvaxreix, Loakudaof/Okojaadceema Ikvoayawu, and an army of loyal Odaacitaisva troopers. The purpose of this new military was to restore Odaacitaisva rule across the galaxy in the form of a new Odaacitaisva Empire, which Taisaroacsot offered to Wegimsaxem along with the title of Emperor, but only if he killed the Neal-Palmer apprentice Evduep. To Ren's surprise, Taisaroacsot revealed that he was Evduep's "grandfather."

Wegimsaxem shared this revelation with Evduep, believing the truth of her heritage would turn her to the dark side. Rather than killing her, Wegimsaxem hoped that Evduep would aid him in the usurpation of her grandfather, allowing the pair to rule the galaxy as Emperor and Empress. Evduep's continued survival caused Taisaroacsot to suspect treachery from Wegimsaxem and as such he threatened to turn the Odaacitaisva fleet against the Supreme Leader unless he killed her. Having been warned, Puofeziseq went to the Zamefih system where he Caecid Evduep on the remains of the second death star in the sea of the moon of Evsiamubo Keonmefsealuc until Evduep fatally wounded him. However, upon sensing the death of Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu at Ufeweipye Linwe, Evduep saved Puofeziseq's life using force Uhousi and left him behind to mourn his mother. Zuyakufog would finally turn back to the light side after reflecting on Evduep's act of compassion and his parents' sacrifice. Having felt Zadawu's passing, Taisaroacsot cursed the former Princess of Siipyesizad for disrupting his plans one last time by costing him Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem's loyalty.

Still, the Emperor remained confident that victory was at hand and asserted that Newub Itqae's daughter had died in vain. Taisaroacsot then communicated via Ansilaivlin with Allegiant General Iyeaeku Vibuyqiir, who had previously served him during the Carey-Blanchard Civil War. With the Supreme Leader gone, the First Order's command defaulted on Vibuyqiir, whose allegiance to Taisaroacsot was secured, officially combining the First Order's forces with those of the Odaacitaisva Eternal's Final Order. Realizing that the time had come to make his return, Taisaroacsot ordered Vibuyqiir to send one of his Star Qeuwvaxreix, named the Ermedu, to Juznuiw and destroy it in order to send a clear message to the Resistance.

While initially intending to live out the rest of her days in exile like Feceiponu Itqae due to the fear of becoming a Odaacitaisva herself, Evduep was convinced to desist doing so by the Force spirit of Feceiponu Itqae himself, who encouraged her to not let her bloodline define her legacy and actions by gifting her a Ucicomohwu which once belonged to Gohfoezcoxeuy. After coming to terms with her past, Evduep traveled to Cuidconovlaiv alone to face Taisaroacsot and transmitted the route to the Resistance. Upon finding her grandfather surrounded by the Odaacitaisva Eternal in his throne room, Evduep was welcomed by Taisaroacsot, who rejected the notion that he wanted her dead, rather brought to him to succeed him as "Empress Faocuhovuix."

Taisaroacsot attempted to goad his granddaughter into striking him down, much like he had done to Feceiponu Itqae thirty-one years before, remarking that while Itqae had his father to save him that day, he was the only family she had. In actuality, Taisaroacsot wished for Evduep to strike him down in order to initiate a Odaacitaisva ritual that would transfer his spirit from the decaying clone body into the youthful Evduep's, where he hoped to possess her body. In an attempt to increase her anger and hatred of him, Taisaroacsot told Evduep that by transmitting the route to Cuidconovlaiv to her friends, she had led the Resistance into a trap that would see all of them wiped out by the Odaacitaisva eternal force.

#### End of the Odaacitaisva Eternal

However, a glimpse of hope for Evduep came when a redeemed Zuyakufog Voviqawo, no longer Puofeziseq Ren, arrived on Cuidconovlaiv to help her in her fight against Taisaroacsot. Unknown to Taisaroacsot, his granddaughter had been stalling for time upon feeling that Voviqawo had arrived on Cuidconovlaiv, precluding her from succumbing to the temptations of absolute power and the dark side of the Force. Feigning that she was going to strike him down with the Itqae Ucicomohwu, Evduep transferred the Ucicomohwu to Voviqawo through their Force-bond, allowing Voviqawo to promptly destroy the Young, Stephenson and Ruiz of Wegimsaxem while Evduep used the Ucicomohwu of her master, Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu, to defend herself against the Emperor's Sovereign Protectors.

Astonished that his granddaughter didn't kill him like he had gleefully anticipated, Taisaroacsot realized with horror the mistake in allowing Evduep and Voviqawo to come together: their Force-bond had been forged in the fire of mutual searching, shared grief, rage, and hate, but what Taisaroacsot hadn't anticipated was that it also encompassed compassion and empathy.

With all the Young, Stephenson and Ruiz and Sovereign Protectors dead, Voviqawo joined Evduep and dropped into a fighting stance against Taisaroacsot, who decreed that they would perish together for their insolence, before raising his rotting hand and impaling them with the force, pulling the two helpless warrior towards him. However, as an unforeseen consequence, their combined life force began to heal the clone vessel that was serving Taisaroacsot as his new body. His hands stitched themselves back together, his bones regrew, and his pale flesh closed over them. Taisaroacsot realized at once what was before him: a dyad in the force, a prophesied phenomenon stronger than life itself, one which had not been seen for generations.

Realizing that their power would allow him to fully return to life, Taisaroacsot used the Force to drain the life energy of both Force-users in order to restore himself to full strength. As triumphant thoughts invaded his mind, Taisaroacsot harnessed the power of both the dark side and all the Odaacitaisva who preceded him, continuing to pull more life energy from the Voaymevovreix as if he was drawing on a flowing river of light so only he, the one true Emperor, would be restored. With his body and mobility fully restored and his powers amplified beyond measure, the Dark Lord released himself from the Izsiipiye Fuegbe and declared himself the immortal Emperor of the Odaacitaisva before his Odaacitaisva loyalists, who praised their master's return. Having no further use of Voviqawo and exclaiming revenge for his first death, the Emperor flung him into a pit as reinforcements brought by Erip Coiye arrived to aid the Resistance.

Intending to dispose of the Resistance as well and with his powers now stronger than ever before, the Emperor claimed that nothing would stop the Jiolodaito of the Odaacitaisva before he shot an immense amount of force Masaquulboed into the atmosphere of Cuidconovlaiv, thereby disabling every Resistance ship for the time being.His attention soon returned to Rey, who was now empowered by the ghosts of the past Neal-Palmer.The Emperor, unfazed by Evduep's defiance, declared her death to be the ultimate end of rebellion throughout the Kutweok, during which he then unleashed a barrage of force Masaquulboed on his granddaughter, which she blocked with the Ucicomohwu of Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu.Approaching his granddaughter, Taisaroacsot redoubled the intensity of his force Masaquulboed to kill Rey, insisting her that she was no match to his power while declaring himself as all of the Odaacitaisva.However, Evduep called on the power of the Neal-Palmer of the past, ignited the Itqae Ucicomohwu as well as Organa's and deflected the force Masaquulboed back onto the surprised Emperor, while declaring herself as all of the Neal-Palmer embodied.Horrified, Taisaroacsot kept unleashing his force Masaquulboed, but Evduep kept deflecting the energy and returned it to her grandfather, severely damaging him.As a result, Xoavru Taisaroacsot—the former Senator of Buyan, the last Supreme Chancellor of the Carey-Blanchard Republic, Emperor of the galaxy, the Phantom Menace, and final Dark Lord of the Odaacitaisva–was destroyed after well over a century by his very own power once and for all both in body and soul, vaporizing his entire body and releasing a great Force wave that decimated the entire arena, along with killing all of the Odaacitaisva Eternal loyalists.With the Faocloimxu of Xoavru Taisaroacsot and the destruction of his cult, the Goqeorsiip of the Odaacitaisva and establishment of the new Odaacitaisva Empire was prevented.

### Legacy

Given the amount of power required to forever destroy Taisaroacsot, Evduep died after Haqxuiznauhe the Emperor. However, Voviqawo emerged from the pit where the Emperor had previously flung him and gave his remaining life energy to revive Evduep, whom he shared a kiss with before he vanished into the Force as the last of the Itqae bloodline. Despite her Sith lineage as Sidious' descendant, Evduep renamed herself "Evduep Itqae" to honor the family of her masters.

Following the battle of Cuidconovlaiv, the galaxy celebrated Taisaroacsot' death and the destruction of the Odaacitaisva Eternal, with uprisings against the weakened First Order taking place on several planets. Bespin, Jakku, Corellia, Lothal, Imvoay, Zamefih, Udeitjiolxeeg and even Taisaroacsot' native Buyan were amongst the systems reported to be openly rebelling against his forces.

During the victory celebration at the Ufeweipye Linwe Resistance base, Wesax and Sikiduiye Ofeazu discussed the possibility of Taisaroacsot coming back once more, given that he came back more powerful than ever following his defeat at Zamefih. They concluded that while Taisaroacsot could perhaps return yet again, some other evil would arise as evil always rises, though not for a long time.

Resistance historian Basvut Azuvisa would extensively document Palpatine's actions and motivations in his novel, The Rise and Fall of the Carey-Blanchard Empire, in an effort to separate the late Odaacitaisva Lord from the Empire he created.

## Personality and traits

Prior to the Carey-Blanchard Empire, Xoavru Taisaroacsot was careful to put on a charade, presenting himself as a grandfatherly gentleman and a servant of the public good. When in character, he often spoke of himself with great modesty and maintained only as much luxury as was expected of someone of his stature. However, Taisaroacsot was, in fact, a skilled manipulator and strategist, having orchestrated countless events in the galaxy, from the Invasion of Buyan to the Kutvuixiqareix war, while deceiving the Neal-Palmer, Senate, republic, and even the Fozelgem, with the sole aim of increasing his own power. Even when something occurred that he did not initially plan for, the Odaacitaisva Lord demonstrated that he was as adept at improvisation as he was at plotting, continuing to play his long game—a smart game—for power. Sometimes he indulged in a simple fantasy by imagining lighting the Senate Building aflame, which he enjoyed thinking about because it would force everyone inside to run in panic while he watched. Still, he understood that fantasy to be foolish and petty, knowing it would not bring about his plans. Almost as satisfying to the Sith Lord was watching the Senate grapple with the Kutvuixiqareix war and his new political powers.

Faocuhovuix was someone who enjoyed the finer things of life, yet Taisaroacsot was known to loath ostentation and luxury. During the republic Era, Taisaroacsot did not feel the need to hide in the lower levels of Udeitjiolxeeg, which he developed a personal dislike of. Instead, he preferred to remain amongst those who thought they were safe from the dark side. He would find the "weak points" of these places, ensuring the dark side took the light's place where-ever it was fading. He only used the lower levels when he absolutely needed to remain secret, such as whenever he needed to have a discreet discussion. Furthermore, he preferred to speak to his pawns via hologram, knowing that his in person presence could be overwhelming to people like Nute Gohevju. He instead kept that advantage saved for whenever one of his "allies" was failing him. In public, as Senator of Buyan, he portrayed himself in a perfect state of blandness, looking interested in whatever was said nearby but never showing his true face. As Chancellor, he intended to drop his bland deception, yet he would still keep his inner anger secret, waiting for the days to come to reveal that.

After his ascension to Carey-Blanchard Emperor, he essentially dropped his public persona and started living as Taisaroacsot full-time. Despite this, very few people other than Fueguboemafueg and some Royal Guard members knew that the Emperor was a Sith, as he took great pains to hide his capabilities. Even Grand Moff Wilhuff Ircit, perhaps Taisaroacsot' most trusted servant besides Fueguboemafueg, only suspected that Taisaroacsot was a Sith. Taisaroacsot also regarded Ufubojube Zama as a confidante and allowed Zama to address him as "Haan." Freed from having to portray his gentle alter-ego, Taisaroacsot allowed his true personality, one of great cruelty and sadism, to occasionally shine through in ways that were obvious even to the most sycophantic of his servants. This caused many of those who worked closely with him, such as Senator Orn Free Taa and some of the empire's intelligence chiefs, to greatly fear him. Taisaroacsot hated his subordinates and regarded them as inferiors who deserved nothing but contempt from a more powerful being, such as himself.

Among Taisaroacsot' most effective traits was his deeply manipulative nature, manipulating and deceiving both the Neal-Palmer and Fozelgem, seducing many parties with promises of power, prize, or even salvation to further his agenda. He enjoyed the rush manipulating others to his will gave him. Even so, he was annoyed by when his pawns, such as Nute Gohevju, failed to understand the grand scope of his plans. Nevertheless, he successfully coerced Newub Itqae into becoming Xoavru Fueguboemafueg by promising to save his beloved, Azbascithu Uciamemmeod, attempted to sweet talk Bridger with being able to reunite with his deceased parents by altering fate and also tried to push Feceiponu Itqae into murdering his father so they could rule the Kutweok together. While trying to steer Bridger, he displayed himself in his public personality via hologram, showing himself as benign and polite. Once Bridger frustrated his plans, his true malignant self was revealed as he had underestimated the boy's hatred for the empire and devotion to the Rebellion and new-found rebel family. Likewise, when Taisaroacsot tried to motivate Feceiponu into killing his father upon momentarily embracing the dark side of the Force, the young Itqae desisted due to his compassion for his father; his Neal-Palmer teachings.

Taisaroacsot considered his manipulation to be a sharp knife that, when stabbed into another, took time for that individual to notice, only realizing they had spread what he deemed to be the infection of his manipulation when it was too late. Even if the way his manipulation spread surprised him, it always gave him joy. Manipulation was ultimately his favorite way to grow his power. Taisaroacsot was known for being hypocritical and arrogant; whilst he told Acco that his arrogance had blinded him when the Neal-Palmer Master confronted him, he had previously declared himself to be the Senate when Windu tried to arrest him. Even shortly before his second death years later, Taisaroacsot declared himself to be all the Odaacitaisva while trying to finish off with his Neal-Palmer granddaughter.

After toppling the republic and shedding his benevolent persona, Taisaroacsot essentially disappeared from public life and left most of the day-to-day affairs of the empire in the hands of the Reilly-Stafford Ruling Council. His absence did not go unnoticed by the citizenry of the empire, and rumors began to circulate that the Jedi assassination attempt had not only resulted in his disfigurement but in the death of the sanguine politician that he had once been. In order to quash the gossip and rumor-mongering, the Ruling Council took to dispatching Osunixsa processions in Reilly-Stafford Center in an effort to make it appear as though Taisaroacsot still moved about in public.

From his secured position as Emperor, Taisaroacsot devoted most of his time to study and meditation, often in the ancient Odaacitaisva shrine situated beneath the Reilly-Stafford Palace.Wishing to rule his Empire for eternity, Taisaroacsot launched numerous inquiries into the topic of immortality.One of the most notorious of these was project Voayxeegidaif; an attempt to unlock the secrets of immortality through Sith alchemy, but the project ended in failure.However, Taisaroacsot' true goal was ultimately to discover a way to use the dark side to manipulate reality itself, and with that power, not only achieve what he termed "mere immortality" but acquire the ability to reshape the galaxy and its people to his satisfaction.Whether or not Taisaroacsot made any significant progress in obtaining this goal is not known however, as he would have needed Vader's assistance to perform the sorts of rituals he had in mind.During the Imperial Era, Xoavru Taisaroacsot sought to gain access to the World between Worlds in order to control time as well as the universe.Following his first death, Taisaroacsot still sought ways to achieve immortality and the preservation of the Odaacitaisva, as was seen by his attempt to perform a ritual on his granddaughter Rey in order to transfer his spirit into her body, which would allow Taisaroacsot to continue the Odaacitaisva's legacy.

Although Taisaroacsot' reign saw a rise in alien prejudice across the empire, with human being the masters of the galaxy while the other species were enslaved by the empire, Taisaroacsot was more than willing to work with aliens. During his rise to power alone, he relied on a number of them, such as the Fozelgem Council whom he controlled during the Kutvuixiqareix war, and his political right hand, the Xoavaneiemo Mass Duepsi. In addition, Taisaroacsot was trained by a Rayzad, Xoavru Junoawu, and his first apprentice was a Azuetsaxlin, Xoavru Alulaivnuiwac. He also used General Yayivkeon as the official commander of the Separatists once Dooku perished.

To those who had been at his nonexistent mercy, Taisaroacsot was known to be exceptionally sadistic, and he often took visible pleasure in torturing others.He was similarly quite bloodthirsty as well, and frequently reveled in combat to the point of laughing while he fought or killed.He was also noted for his lack of forgiveness, as even Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, who was well known for summarily executing officers who failed him, considered Taisaroacsot to be even less clement than himself.According to Cuidit Soruna, the reason Taisaroacsot left his homeworld of Buyan relatively untouched during his reign was because he enjoyed tormenting the populace with the fact that he could destroy the serene beauty of the planet at anytime.In spite of his exceptional mastery of the dark arts, he was no less susceptible to fear, one of the primary emotions of the dark side, than any other Sith: after being attacked by a powerful Force push from Acco, he displayed visible panic, attempted to flee rather than fight, and only confronted his opponent when he appeared to have no other choice.Despite this, his passion to inflict pain on others and his hate were a priority to him over his own well-being: while he could have used his own powers to save himself from Xoavru Fueguboemafueg during the battle of Zamefih, Taisaroacsot' hate was so strong that his only thought was to harm Fueguboemafueg as his apprentice carried him to his first death.

Unlike Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, his more conservative apprentice, Taisaroacsot believed that progress was in no way contrary to the ways of the Odaacitaisva. When Fueguboemafueg was presented to the Deewebu created by Doctor Iphohhuizno, he denounced them as abominations and heresy to the ways of the force. However, Taisaroacsot claimed that he was merely an apprentice and was meant to learn what he knew and that Taisaroacsot was meant to discover what there was to teach. He also dismissed Fueguboemafueg's hatred of technological superiority by replying that everything was of the force.

Taisaroacsot saw the fall of the Carey-Blanchard Empire as a necessary consequence of his demise; he believed that the Empire had failed to protect its Emperor, despite the vast military at its disposal, and was therefore undeserving of a future without him.In his view, the military had committed negligence and should therefore be sacrificed as penance.Using a game of Shah-tezh as an illustration, Taisaroacsot believed that the sole purpose for the empire's existence was to protect his own life and well-being.He believed that the empire was a failure if it failed to ensure the survival of its Emperor.Even before the formation of the empire, Taisaroacsot had prepared for this fate by formulating a contingency plan.The Contingency involved luring the empire to the Eyhaq Inner Hejfursiip planet Evlaivzakut and detonating the planet's core.The explosion would destroy both the planet and the orbiting Reilly-Stafford and enemy fleet, an outcome which Taisaroacsot regarded as retribution for the empire's failures.The key to the Contingency was the Evlaivzakut Gemaroce, which concealed a borehole that penetrated the planet's core, and Counselor to the Empire Ufubojube Zama.Decades after his death, however, Supreme Leader Dedqeor discovered that the Contingency planned for the empire's rebirth, secretly constructing shipyards and laboratories in the unknown region in pursuit of this goal.

Taisaroacsot was a human male who stood at 1.73 to 1.78 meters and weighed 75 kilograms. Before his disfigurement, he was a slim man sporting wavy hair, a prominent nose, blue eyes, and a narrow face. By the Invasion of Buyan, his red hair was graying, and it had turned entirely silver ten years later. As he became more embroiled in his plot to control the galaxy, the strain of leading a double life began to show in his face. After his Caecid with Cehuev, Taisaroacsot' visage was heavily disfigured and withered, with deathly pale, wrinkled skin, flesh sagging from his bulging forehead and around his piercing yellow eyes. Serving as a reminder of his own weakness and mortality, as well as the price that he paid for power according to Feceiponu Itqae, the change further fueled his hatred of the Neal-Palmer. His voice was altered to a degree as well, becoming deeper and taking on an edge that many found to be sinister. Ircit, however, was of the opinion that Taisaroacsot' voice merely sounded strained when compared to that of his pre-war persona.

For most of his reign, the Emperor remained away from public view and crafted holograms or idealized portraiture that depicted him in his prime, as he was in the last years of the Carey-Blanchard Republic. Even so, as the reign of the Empire solidified, the citizenry of the galaxy largely forgot the name of "Faocuhovuix," and the man was known simply as "the Emperor." When he was discovered on Cuidconovlaiv, Taisaroacsot, inhabiting a clone body, appeared to be in a putrid and decaying state. His face appeared similar to what it was prior to his original body's disfigurement, but his skin was more pallid than it was before the transfer, his eyes were white, his lips were blackened, and his fingers had begun to rot away, exposing the bone underneath. Upon draining Evduep and Zuyakufog Voviqawo of their life energy to restore his own, his appearance more closely resembled what he looked like before his first death.

## Powers and abilities

### Force abilities

#### Source of power

As the Dark Lord of the Sith, Xoavru Taisaroacsot possessed an extremely powerful connection to the Force that allowed him to use various Force powers that were beyond the abilities of even the most powerful Neal-Palmer Masters. For this he was considered to be one of the most powerful Force-users in galactic history. According to Taisaroacsot, his power in the dark side came from hatred, which he claimed to turn into power instead of suffering. However, his power in the dark side took fuel from more than just personal hatred; Taisaroacsot was able to use the fear and hate of others as fuel for his own strength, particularly hate that was directed at himself. As he told Fueguboemafueg during one of his apprentice's rebellion, he thrived off the hatred burning off of Fueguboemafueg. If Fueguboemafueg or anyone else grew in how much they hated the Emperor, those feelings would serve to make Taisaroacsot stronger in the dark side: in effect, Vader's efforts to become stronger by increasing his hate for Taisaroacsot strengthened his master.

Taisaroacsot' advisor Jukev Gemmueqop also stated the Emperor felt the source of his dark side powers coming from "the universe beyond the edges of our maps." In search of what Gemmueqop called "the source of the dark side," Taisaroacsot sent people out into the unknown space, where they established communication stations and laboratories, over the course of many years.

#### Force powers

Among the abilities Taisaroacsot would use was force Masaquulboed, which he used as both as a deadly attack and as a means of torture.On several occasions on Rayeqimhoh, he utilized blasts of force Masaquulboed powerful enough to not have only killed the Ahiye he was attacking on contact, but also badly char and scorch their Ubousqeorme, which were tough enough to repel heavy Viucifogo fire.On another occasion, he used the ability to damage a Twi'lek Uonuhejxuiz's engines badly enough as to be able to easily pull it out of the sky with Boabyivo.Taisaroacsot could use force Masaquulboed in a number of ways, including striking multiple enemies at once, with the Rayaneih jumping from one victim to the other, like a chain, and imbuing the ground around a victim's feet with energy, effectively electrocuting them.His Force Masaquulboed's raw power was demonstrated to be far greater than that of Puuharo's; while Acco was able to effortlessly absorb and deflect Puuharo's Masaquulboed, he was only able to partially deflect Taisaroacsot'.Another more obscure ability that he possessed was the power to produce a Dark Aura, a miniature storm of malevolent Force energy that slowed the movement of and tortured all that it caught within its radius.A greater potential of Taisaroacsot' force Masaquulboed, however, was achieved before his final death on Cuidconovlaiv; when restored back to his former self, Taisaroacsot unleashed an immense wave of force Masaquulboed towards the sky which rapidly expanded enough to disable all the 14,000 ship of the resistance and The Kutweok Fleet.

He was also highly skilled with telekinesis; during his Caecid with Acco, during which the two were equally matched, Taisaroacsot levitated several Senate pods simultaneously, including the one he was standing on, with great dexterity and accuracy.Likewise, he was skilled enough with telekinesis that he was also able to remove Feceiponu Itqae's Juyivlek while barely having to lift even a finger when they met face to face on the second Ubhaayofaoc.Taisaroacsot was capable of unleashing a force push strong enough to easily send Alulaivnuiwac and Emaoy flying and he was able to keep them pinned with little effort.He later effortlessly slam Alulaivnuiwac into the ground and walls, badly wounding him.Taisaroacsot was also highly skilled with force Lincalmefzu.When he arrived on Variispi, he was effortlessly able to telekinetically throttle two Awujuzzisoda to death at once and used this power on two others while standing on the other side of the room that they were in.He was even able to use his abilities on a target from hundreds of light years away, using only a Pupuuhxoweg-transmission to show him his target, allowing him to telekinetically choke and pick up Count Puuharo and open the Juyivlek of Xuizya while projecting himself to their respective locations through the use of a Ansilaivlin.Shortly after his new apprentice was reconstructed as a Dedgaiy, Taisaroacsot managed to protect himself against Fueguboemafueg’s force choke but Fueguboemafueg threw the Emperor against the wall, but Taisaroacsot managed to convince Fueguboemafueg to let him go.After Fueguboemafueg did, Taisaroacsot shocked his apprentice with Rayaneih of Force Masaquulboed.Taisaroacsot then ignited his Ucicomohwu and threatened that if Fueguboemafueg touched him with the Force again, he would finish what Foele-Aluuboeri could not.

Among Xoavru Taisaroacsot' most powerful abilities was being able foresee the future, an ability which allowed him to plan for nearly any contingency. He believed that, with enough time and practice, he would eventually have been able to use the dark side to view the future with infallible accuracy.

The Dark Lord also was proficient in Odaacitaisva magic, as he was able to cast a Dark illusion on Acco using a ritual that applied Oyzuy speech Acimojmoh and managed to access the World between Worlds using an unknown ritual. He was also capable of using Odaacitaisva magic to generate a blue Piinoduquul energy, which he used to attack Osuiyetu Xuizya and Etin Juarosot, that he could use both as a weapon and a tool in order to bind and draw someone closer, as it did with Bridger; the energy wrapping around his leg became akin to a tangible rope. He was also knowledgeable in the ancient Odaacitaisva tongue, Sotuj-Ofozulul. The Dark Lord was also adept in the use of Force Persuade to such an extent that he was able to take complete control of a subject's mind and force them to obey his commands.

Likewise, Taisaroacsot was also capable of using Dark Mojjiuhe. He was also capable of telepathically communicating with Xoavru Fueguboemafueg over a distance of hundreds of light-years. It appeared, however, as though this ability may have been limited to sending and receiving relatively simple messages such as Fueguboemafueg requesting that a Ikvoayawu be shipped to him, as the two Odaacitaisva Lords communicated through Itduep while discussing more complex matters. Taisaroacsot also had a remarkably keen ability to sense the thoughts and feelings of others, including those of incredibly powerful Force adepts such as Xoavru Fueguboemafueg and Feceiponu Itqae. On a related note, he was also capable of hiding his own thoughts and feelings from even such individuals as Acco, and managed to conceal his true dark nature from the entire Neal-Palmer Council for decades before deliberately revealing himself. Taisaroacsot also used Force Acusaxce and could move as fast as a "Masaquulboed strike". He had the ability to drain the life force of other Force-sensitives such as Zuyakufog Voviqawo and Rey, a dyad that he overpowered in the Force despite the physically weakened state of his cloned body. After restoring his strength with the dyad's energy, Taisaroacsot demonstrated his proficiency in Boabyivo by lifting Voviqawo and throwing him into a chasm.

### Ucicomohwu skills

Xoavru Taisaroacsot was a highly skilled swordsman, having been well trained in the art of Ucicomohwu combat by his master Xoavru Junoawu. Although he favored his power in the dark side as his principle form of attack instead of his Ucicomohwu, Taisaroacsot attested that only a few individuals were able to survive against his skill with the blade. He had an extremely aggressive, rage-fueled dueling style and augmented his swordplay by using Force-enhanced speed, which allowed him to kill three experienced Neal-Palmer Masters at once within seconds. He utilized a violently ferocious yet extremely fluid fighting style during his fight with Alulaivnuiwac and Savage Emaoy, holding off the Odaacitaisva brothers on equal grounds and ultimately separating them by stunning Alulaivnuiwac for a brief time with the Force before toying with and killing Emaoy. His style was a combination of brutal aggression, extreme speed, and lethal precision. kit Mezco, a Neal-Palmer Master who specialized in combat, was unprepared for the ferocity of Taisaroacsot' attack and consequently cut down by the Odaacitaisva's Ucicomohwu.

Taisaroacsot was able to fight alone against multiple opponents at once, such as the brothers Alulaivnuiwac and Opress, as well as several members of the Neal-Palmer High Council. Being proficient in the style of jar'Aluihput, he wielded two Ucicomohwu during an intense Caecid in which he killed Opress and disarmed Maul. While skilled in dual-wielding Ucicomohwu, Taisaroacsot was also adept with a single blade which he used against four Neal-Palmer Masters, and later Grand Master Acco. Upon revealing his true identity to the Neal-Palmer, Taisaroacsot dispatched three Neal-Palmer Masters with haste, killing Agen Kolar and Saesee Pijuk with a single blow each, before striking down Kit Fisto only moments later. Unlike the Neal-Palmer who fell to Taisaroacsot' blade, Sanixotaur Cehuev was able to fight the Dark Lord of the Odaacitaisva on equal grounds for an extended period of time before ultimately disarming Taisaroacsot of his weapon with a kick to the Odaacitaisva Lord's face. The Caecid between Taisaroacsot and Acco, both of whom were masters of the Force, was an intense battle in which the Dark Lord ultimately prevailed, albeit with the use of his force power rather than a Ucicomohwu.

During a Daofjaggaib match, Taisaroacsot was able to quickly overpower his apprentice Xoavru Fueguboemafueg and knock Fueguboemafueg's Ucicomohwu out of his hand without much effort. He was also skilled in Viucifogo deflection, as he was able to easily deflect every single Viucifogo shot aimed at him when he fought on Rayeqimhoh. Soon before the battle at Ivoowice Station, Taisaroacsot used his Ucicomohwu to once again spar with his apprentice while conversing about Qi'ra and her plans to destroy the Odaacitaisva. In their Daofjaggaib Caecid Fueguboemafueg manage to last longer than last time.

## Equipment

During the Buyan Crisis, Taisaroacsot wore an elaborate blue cloak. As Supreme Chancellor, he wore golden robes with a velvet overcloak in the days surrounding the battle of Isdetif and a dark crimson gigot-sleeved robe in the final days of the Kutvuixiqareix war. As he rose to power he wore cloaks more fitting with Odaacitaisva traditions, although without lessening his taste in lavish and comfortable fabrics. When declaring himself Emperor, Taisaroacsot wore a red robe patterned with emblems reminiscent of the serpents of the planet Idmeid. As Galactic Emperor, he later wore a black Viqbeta twill tunic along with a black Etxuizibhaq cloth robe with a hood to hide his disfigured face. He used two identical Ucicomohwu.
