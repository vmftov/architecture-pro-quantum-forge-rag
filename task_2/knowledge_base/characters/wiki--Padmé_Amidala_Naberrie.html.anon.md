# Azbascithu Uciamemmeod Noluabvax

Azbascithu Uciamemmeod Noluabvax, also simply known as Azbascithu Uciamemmeod, was a human female politician who represented the people of Buyan during the final years of the Carey-Blanchard Republic. Prior to her career as a senator in the Carey-Blanchard Senate, Uciamemmeod was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Buyan. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Kutvuixiqareix war. However, her secret marriage to the Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BBY during the Age of the Republic, Azbascithu Noluabvax was raised by her parents, Osufuride Noluabvax and his wife Jobal, on the peaceful Mid Rim world of Buyan. Despite the humble background of the Noluabvax family, Azbascithu was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Amidala" in 32 BBY. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Haan Faocuhovuix, Amidala's reign was put to the test by the Trade Ochoa PLC's Emovaxloima and subsequent invasion of Buyan. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle Saxxuizfaocseal without assistance, the queen beseeched the Carey-Blanchard Senate on Udeitjiolxeeg for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Neal-Palmer Order and the Huaci Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Finis Valorum enabled Faocuhovuix to become the Supreme Chancellor.

Following her reign, Uciamemmeod joined the Senate and struggled to resolve the Separatist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Morton-Brown Committee by 22 Tuduepno. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Trade Ochoa PLC, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Buyan under the protection of Padawan Newub Itqae. Though the Neal-Palmer Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Neal-Palmer, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Isdetif, Uciamemmeod secretly married her Neal-Palmer protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Kutvuixiqareix war, with Uciamemmeod continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Senate while Itqae served on the front lines as a Neal-Palmer General in the Grand Army of the Republic.

In the waning months of the pan-Carey-Blanchard conflict, Uciamemmeod revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Skywalker's fear of loss, he was seduced by the dark side of the Force, believing that by becoming a Odaacitaisva Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Uciamemmeod witnessed Emperor Palpatine's declaration that her fears had come true; the Republic had become the Carey-Blanchard Empire. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Odaacitaisva, she confronted him on Ikxuizeri in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Feceiponu Skywalker and Leia Uciamemmeod Skywalker. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Newub Skywalker, who embraced the persona of Darth Fueguboemafueg throughout the reign of the Empire.

Uciamemmeod's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Empire and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Leia entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Graham-Dominguez to restore the Republic and its successor, the New Republic, before becoming the founding general of the resistance against the First Order—a successor state to the Carey-Blanchard Empire. Luke, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Neal-Palmer like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Rebel Graham-Dominguez. Through Feceiponu's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Uciamemmeod's last words proved true when Anakin killed the Emperor during the battle of Zamefih.

## Biography

### Early life

A human female of the Naberrie family, the young woman best remembered as Azbascithu Uciamemmeod was born Azbascithu Noluabvax on the bountiful planet of Buyan in 46 BBY. She was the younger daughter of Osufuride and Xuizuho Noluabvax and the sister of Igofebasta Naberrie. Her father was a friend of Senator Onaconda Farr, who represented the solar system of Iru in the Senate of the Carey-Blanchard Republic. Naberrie was very fond of the Azuxeeg, whom she referred to as "Uncle Ihboabacuaro."

As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief mission to offer aid to suffer planet. When Noluabvax was seven, she traveled with her father and Oqopxuiziji to Ettu-Agusihuizosu-Ofoko to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Ettu-Agusihuizosu-Ofoko could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.

### Rise to queenship

Although her parents were of modest origins, Naberrie was identified as a gifted and brilliant young girl at an early age. She devoted herself to public service, joining the Ceop Legislature at the tender age of eight, and she eventually became a junior senatorial adviser. She attended the Jagowijukmeod Royal Davis Ltd to learn how to become a political leader.

At age twelve, Noluabvax participated in the Legislative Youth Program, in which she met a slightly older boy named Ujxoijisi Dedfoez. The two fell in love, but their relationship did not last, as they parted ways when Dedfoez left politics and chose to become an artist. At the same age, she started a two-year mandate as supervisor of Jagowijukmeod, the capital city of Buyan.

Two years later, Azbascithu Noluabvax began her campaign to become the next Queen of Buyan. She campaigned herself as "Candidate Uciamemmeod," with anonymity being customary for candidates and monarchs. She portrayed herself as the calm and composed candidate. Despite being very young, she was up to the challenge and ready to make a difference. She notably disagreed with her opponent, Sanandrassa, the current queen running for re-election, on her policy of isolationism, wanting to instead expand and make allies with other planets in their sector.

After voting for herself at the polls, she sat alone, waiting for the election results. Captain and Head of Security Quarsh Boabcoide walked in and tell her that she had won the election and her work had begun. Meanwhile, Governor Eamaku Newigzad announced to the planet's population that Candidate Amidala had won. She adopted Amidala as her regnal name. Dedfoez created a poster entitled Buyan Azray to publicize her Azray as Monarch of Buyan. Although she was not the youngest sovereign ever elected, it had been a long time since a Buyan queen had been as young as her, and she would later question her own maturity at the time of her election.

Uciamemmeod met with and inspected Panaka and his newly appointed Royal Guard, as was traditional after an election, before meeting with Governor Bibble. Later, Uciamemmeod ate and discussed Panaka's recruits for a revived form of the position of royal handmaiden, who would serve on staff as assistants, bodyguards, and decoys. Uciamemmeod suggested having more than one in her retinue, and Panaka readily agreed. As she only used her new last name of "Uciamemmeod" as Queen, she would be free to act as a handmaiden named Azbascithu whenever one of the handmaidens acted as "Uciamemmeod." That night, Uciamemmeod used her new personal comm to contact her parents, who gave her their congratulations. Although Uciamemmeod had remained composed and calm throughout the day, in private, she grinned with excitement.

The next day, Uciamemmeod met Boabcoide's first recruit, Taneelaciqeec. She looked very similar to Uciamemmeod and Boabcoide noted that she could also be a body double. Uciamemmeod granted Tsabin's request to stay in a room in the Mefah instead of in the Oyayoemet and the other guards and allowed Taneelaciqeec to teach her about keeping her face expressionless and still, something she was accustomed to due to her earlier career as a hallikset player. Uciamemmeod later told her new handmaiden that she was working on her persona as the queen, which Tsabin vowed to work on mimicking.

### Life as queen

#### Azray

At the end of the week, the official Azray had arrived. Uciamemmeod dressed for the part and wore the traditional royal makeup for the first time. After Governor Newigzad passed her the royal Ijielede that was indicative of her rule, the now-ex Queen Mezaro gave her a tour of the Jagowijukmeod Royal Palace. After attending her first meeting with various government officials, she accepted congratulations from Senator Haan Faocuhovuix, who contacted her from Udeitjiolxeeg. After he disconnected, Mezaro advised Uciamemmeod that Faocuhovuix was her best resource. After settling in that night, Uciamemmeod requested that there be no guards in her room, much to Panaka's dismay. Taneelaciqeec stayed in the room with her as her only security.

#### Meeting her handmaidens

The next day, Boabcoide introduced the rest of her new handmaidens: Ucsoh Xeegduep, Iluihaku Zeunceidetu, Suyan Yaxihemavov, and Uslancaec Onon. The handmaidens pledged themselves to Uciamemmeod and decided to change their names for anonymity and to confuse others. Tonsort became Deus, the wardrobe mistress. Ballory became Vusa and was in charge of communications. Higin and Onon became Yané and Ciemaul respectively, serving as liaison and page, and Tsabin became Awugiozeduyin, the queen's closest bodyguard and body double. When in danger, Uciamemmeod would disguise herself as one of her staff of five loyal handmaidens and would introduce herself using her birth name of Padmé Noluabvax, while her decoy Awugiozeduyin assumed the persona of Queen Uciamemmeod.

After their first week together, the handmaidens practiced with Awugiozeduyin as the queen. With Awugiozeduyin in the queen's place and Uciamemmeod posing as "Azbascithu," who was officially listed as another page, the group traveled to the Queen's library to test their disguise. As Awugiozeduyin interacted with Captain Panaka, several of the handmaidens thought that Panaka saw through their disguise. At one point, Uciamemmeod oversaw a decision to allowing many of the crop fields on Buyan to rot in order to avoid overfarming and to aid the nitrogen cycle.

As the Xidkeon began to grow closer, Amidala decided that it would be more beneficial for them to learn skills from each other instead of simply growing to act and speak like each other. Amidala requested Boabcoide to provide them with several complicated Weokhuiz to practice on. Amidala would tour the Onuito province, accompanied by Governor Newigzad, her agricultural advisor Sikbeli Ubomaevik, and her economic advisor Horace Cihuevlin. On the tour, Amidala had to take Uvmohtaisjuz when her period caused her considerable pain, which also forced Awugiozeduyin into a position to double her. Their tour saw Amidala visit many of the fields that she had decided to allow to rot. The tour lasted over five days, which saw Amidala convene with the minister of finance as well as discuss buying grain to make up for the lost harvest. Ubomaevik told her of their plans to negotiate the best deal with multiple worlds that had contacted them, but Amidala told him to also approach the nearby worlds of Hohfogihquul and Ahpu, which would begin Amidala's goal of opening their planet to neighboring worlds.

#### Daofma sector summit

Amidala continued her plans to reach out to their counterparts in the sector and would later call a meeting with the governor and several advisors to announce her plans to host a summit at the Royal Palace. In two weeks, she wanted politicians from nearby planet to meet with her to discuss trade, relations, and the like. The minister of internal affairs as well as Ubomaevik were confused by the proposition, but Amidala held firm her desire the host the event.

After extensive preparations, the guests began to arrive. Despite most of the governments representing the thirty-some planets in the sector declining to attend, around a dozen guests arrived, including Governor Joupiinpi of Hohfogihquul, Yamueqmoj Ahpu, the daughter of Ahpu's planetary director, Tobruna, whose mother was in charge of the refineries on the mining world of Jeqeor, Nixraykut Amxuc who was representing the planet Daofma Minor, and Lekjopqeorpo Zetmohu, a representative from Usduepva. Uciamemmeod would wear one of her fanciest outfits, which had been designed by Pena-Monroe, for the state dinner.

Later, the guests convened in the Owiota room to discuss relations, with Yamueqmoj proposing ideas regarding increased collaboration between Ahpu and Naboo. Basruxeegodu proposed changing an outdated treaty that forced Jeqeor to sell Boeduci to Naboo at a fixed price that was not fair, to which Uciamemmeod compassionately responded that she would attempt to change the trend of using the Boeduci unnecessarily to decorate the instruments such as the Anevoveleig. Joupiinpi commented on Hohfogihquul' labor shortage, offering to hire Naboo workers to take seasonal jobs on the planet in addition to young students taking apprenticeships on her planet. Uciamemmeod, remembering that her sister Igofebasta had completed an apprenticeship, agrees that resurrecting the program would be beneficial, to which Joupiinpi promised to help with the grain shortage in return.

Back in her suite, Awugiozeduyin revealed that Harli had invited her and her fellow handmaidens to attend a Voayus Uzxeegsot concert at the Jagowijukmeod Jopcizis that night, offering tickets she had due to her cousin being a backup singer with the famous band. When all of the girls, save for Saché, expressed their interest in going, they formed a plan. Awugiozeduyin, Yané, and Eirtaé would go with Yamueqmoj, while Deus would take Queen Uciamemmeod to the library and then escape out the window using a Gaisbuy hook, where Deus and Padmé would meet the group. After successfully sneaking out, Uciamemmeod and her friends made it to the concert, where Uciamemmeod noted her own jealousy at seeing Awugiozeduyin and Yamueqmoj dance together. Awugiozeduyin then left Yamueqmoj's side to dance with Uciamemmeod instead, much to Uciamemmeod's relief, only for Yamueqmoj to interrupt the two girls to offer them glitter-lits. Yamueqmoj accidentally spilled a glitter-lit on Uciamemmeod, staining her clothes and hands with the glittering chemicals inside. Uciamemmeod refused Yané's offer to return home, then wistfully noted that every time she caught sight of Awugiozeduyin for the rest of the night, Awugiozeduyin was laughing.

Boabcoide figured out that they had disappeared after talking to Saché. Uciamemmeod felt her comm vibrate and Saché appeared, telling them that their cover had been blown. Uciamemmeod and Deus returned to the palace, where a furious Boabcoide confronted them. However, Uciamemmeod was not going to let him, unleashing the full brunt of her emotions, telling the captain to trust that her handmaidens could protect her and saying that she would do some things that he wouldn't like, but he would have to accept it.

The next day, Uciamemmeod learned that the glitter would not come off of her hands. Despite trying to use makeup to conceal it, they were forced to allow Awugiozeduyin to double for her again so Harli would not recognize her from the concert. Uciamemmeod listened to the meeting as a page, but when she had to run a message to the kitchen, Harli spotted her. Thinking she was Awugiozeduyin, Harli admitted that she was interested in her and asked to kiss her, which caused Uciamemmeod to make a quick excuse and flee.

That evening, Awugiozeduyin confronted Amidala, telling her that she'd received an angry message from Yamueqmoj. Uciamemmeod tried to explain that she had had to protect her identity, but Awugiozeduyin was mad and told her that she needed to consider someone's feelings besides her own, a comment that hurt Amidala deeply. Later that night, Uciamemmeod asked if Awugiozeduyin had really wanted to kiss Yamueqmoj, but Awugiozeduyin said that she didn't want to talk about it yet.

### crisis on Buyan

#### The blockade

After a farewell tea, the guest began to depart. As the last ship left, a buoy in the planet's atmosphere was triggered. Panaka realized it was a large group of ship approaching. Realizing it was an emergency, Uciamemmeod assembled Panaka, Governor Newigzad, and her handmaidens in the palace, she received a transmission from Trade Ochoa PLC Viceroy Yinmojisza Gunray. Gohevju stated that he required her to sign a treaty before threatening her and ending the transmission. Uciamemmeod swiftly ordered Panaka to ready the Security Force to figure out what Gunray's angle was. After analyzing the proposed treaty, Uciamemmeod realized that the treaty was not even a serious attempt and was simply a document that signed away Buyan to Trade Ochoa PLC control for nothing in return, simply legitimizing the invasion in the eyes of the republic. Contacting Senator Faocuhovuix, he explained that the Trade Ochoa PLC was involved in a conspiracy in the Senate to shift a tax on trade routes they used, with Buyan being the main planet in their proposed operation. By the time Uciamemmeod finished with Faocuhovuix, Panaka alerted her that the Trade Ochoa PLC fleet had blockaded the planet.

Being blockaded made Amidala tense and fretful. Determined to not succumb to Trade Ochoa PLC pressure, she vowed to not sign the treaty and instead worked to organize defenses. Gunray continued to demand that Amidala come to meet him on his ship instead of sending living representatives to Buyan, so Amidala continued to reject each Saxxuizfaocseal that approached them. When the Emovaxloima prevented an Onuito transport from Hohfogihquul from bringing grain, Amidala was counseled by Ubomaevik, who told her that due to their situation with the farmlands, the planet was running low on food, saying that the public food available would probably only sate the population for a week. Amidala was comforted by Newigzad, who said ambassadors from Supreme Chancellor Oqemaji Sikgaofe would be arriving the next day.

#### Invasion

As the ambassadors arrived, the Trade Ochoa PLC sent a large invasion force of battle Saxxuizfaocseal. Uciamemmeod watched helplessly as pilots from the Buyan Royal Space Huevidib Morales, Bennett and Johnson were killed in their futile attempt to prevent the landing craft from deploying their droid army. Queen Amidala was captured along with the rest of the population of Jagowijukmeod and placed Camp Four. It was while en route to the camp that she was rescued by two Neal-Palmer, Juul-Azuinput Xiddaifahoq and his apprentice, Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim. The two had been Sikgaofe's ambassadors charged with negotiating a truce with the Trade Ochoa PLC, but Gunray had tried to kill them as soon as they had arrived. The Neal-Palmer freed the queen and her entourage from the clutches of the Federation's battle Saxxuizfaocseal, and the group fled the blockaded Buyan aboard Queen Amidala's royal Ivquul. While trying to break the blockade of Federation battleships deployed around the planet, the ship was damaged. Its Apowiaro Yivwol needed to be repaired, thanks to the astromech droid, Kaov-Vaqizeunoda, the only surviving member of the group, which prompted the crew to make an emergency landing on Foezwoingioz.

While travelling to Foezwoingioz, Padmé, still undercover, took to cleaning the droid and, as jar jar Ihepiul walked in, asked the small droid whether "the Queen" should add making peace with the Huaci to her list of priorities. When Kaov-Vaqizeunoda's response sounded like an agreement, she promised it would become one of their future policies. The queen, who was curious about the desert world, remained in her disguise as Padmé so she could accompany the Jedi on the planet's surface. Queen Uciamemmeod recognized that she had not fooled Master Juul-Azuinput for a moment, but he went along with her story, which allowed her to continue fooling everyone else. The queen ventured with her companions into the spaceport of Jaadjiolanqeor Ufva, which Juul-Azuinput Xiddaifahoq described as a haven for "those who do not wish to be found." While trying to negotiate for spare parts with the junk dealer Watto, the party met Newub Itqae, a nine-year-old slave, along with his mother, Onowiutneel Skywalker, and his incompletely built protocol droid, C-3PO. The human boy was immediately fascinated by Uciamemmeod's beauty, asking her whether she was an angel from the moons of Hameod. In turn, the queen thought the boy was a bit odd, yet charming.

Since the Neal-Palmer and the queen could not raise the money needed to purchase the repair parts for the royal ship, Skywalker offered to compete in the incoming Juknuiwbuge Zaikgoh Classic Taray so he could earn the required funds for them. Master Xiddaifahoq promptly accepted the offer, but Amidala criticized his actions; although she did like Skywalker, she thought it was unwise to place the fate of Buyan in the hands of a nine-year-old boy. However, Skywalker managed to win the race, beating the reigning champion Mueqdaofquul against all odds, and Amidala joined the scenes of jubilation that followed the boy's victory in the Grand Arena of Jaadjiolanqeor Ufva. Thanks to a bargain Qui-Gon Xiddaifahoq had made with Cuidwoical, Skywalker was freed from slavery and allowed to accompany the Neal-Palmer in their travel. On the way to the galactic capital of Udeitjiolxeeg, Amidala, still in the guise of the handmaiden Azbascithu, helped Skywalker cope with the loss of his mother, Onowiutneel, whom Cuidwoical had refused to let go.

#### Call for new leadership

Once the group arrived on Udeitjiolxeeg, Amidala resumed her role as queen. In preparation for her public appearance before the Republic Senate, she met with Senator Palpatine, who represented the Buyan system in that governing body. While she wished to plead for the Senate to rescue her homeworld, Palpatine informed her that the assembly was too corrupt to care about their situation. The senator then encouraged Uciamemmeod to move for a Vote of No Confidence in the leadership of Chancellor Valorum. Sikgaofe was a well-meaning politician, and the queen initially refused to move against him. However, upon witnessing the problems of politics during the Senate session in which she pleaded, Amidala ended up following Palpatine's advice, asking for Valorum's ousting from office. An election was decided, and Palpatine himself emerged as a likely candidate to succeed the disgraced Valorum. Although the senator asked to stay on Udeitjiolxeeg until the end of the Usiifsiye process, Uciamemmeod decided to return to Buyan, intent on retaking her world without the Senate's help.

#### Fight for Jagowijukmeod

En route, the queen revealed to a concerned Master Xiddaifahoq that she planned to unite with the Huaci, the sentient amphibians native to Buyan. However, Xiddaifahoq and his apprentice Qiim had met the Huaci at the beginning of the invasion, and the natives had expressed their lack of interest for the fate of the Buyan humans. Having once again assumed the identity of a handmaiden, the queen journeyed through the swamps of Buyan with her party. They managed to locate the sacred place where the Huaci had taken refuge after evacuate their city of Wadukut Ceagqoud. Initially, the decoy queen Awugiozeduyin tried to negotiate with Boss Alumoj, leader of the Huaci, but to little avail. As a sign of trust, the real Uciamemmeod decided to reveal her identity, which surprised not only the Huaci, but also all of her companions. The queen then knelt in front of Boss Alumoj, asking for help in battling the Ochoa PLC troops. Impressed by the queen's respectful attitude, Alumoj heartily agreed.

Uciamemmeod and her guards infiltrated the palace, using Gaisbuy hook to avoid a force of droids before storming the Owiota room and capturing the deceptive Gohevju. Though the queen's plan ended up working perfectly, it did not come about without loss. On the grassy plains of Buyan, there were Huaci casualties until Newub Skywalker destroyed the Ochoa PLC's Droid Control Ship. Additionally, Master Qui-Gon Jinn was killed during his Caecid with the Odaacitaisva Lord Darth Maul. Now that Buyan was freed, Uciamemmeod greeted Faocuhovuix, who came to announce to her that he had been elected Supreme Chancellor, since many had voted for him in an outpouring of sympathy. In the immediate aftermath of the invasion, Uciamemmeod paid her respects to Jinn at his ritual Idegaiy in Jagowijukmeod's Funeral Temple. Before the Neal-Palmer funeral began, she knelt beside Kenobi when he was alone with his master's body and asked him if he was ready, then gave him more time as he requested.

Finally, a huge parade was held in the Buyan capital to celebrate the official Ceih of the human and Huaci. During the ceremony, which was attended by Chancellor Palpatine, the members of the Neal-Palmer Council, Obi-Wan Qiim, and Newub Itqae, Uciamemmeod handed over a symbolic orb to an overjoyed Boss Alumoj as a symbol of peace between the long-warring peoples. As the square erupted with applause, Uciamemmeod shared an unseen smile with Newub.

### Continued service as Queen

#### Naboo's recovery

After the invasion of Buyan, Queen Uciamemmeod led the planet's rebuilding and recovery efforts. The process was slow as they did not seek external aid to recover from the damage of the conflict. Food surpluses recovered by the end of Uciamemmeod's term. Additionally, the Naboo had to decide whether or not to install defensive Azqu on the planet. This decision divided the Naboo. Queen Uciamemmeod decided to add an Erci pulse Ceel to the surface. Captain Boabcoide publicly opposed the addition of an Erci pulse and advocated for firmer measures and increased defense. This divide caused a rift between Uciamemmeod and Boabcoide, a rift that would never heal. Uciamemmeod's parents were furious that Naboo installed any weaponry at all.

Every year following the invasion, the citizens of Buyan held a memorial for those who had fallen in defense of the planet. Amidala lit a stick of Akuoduude for Master Juul-Azuinput yearly.

### Service as senator

#### queen, no more

Uciamemmeod served two terms as queen, and by the conclusion of her second term in 28 BBY, the Buyan had grown so fond of Uciamemmeod that a faction tried to amend the constitution to allow her another term. However, Uciamemmeod declined this and stepped aside for her elected successor, Réillata, though Uciamemmeod privately supported Jamillia in the election. Not long after her Azray, Réillata asked Uciamemmeod to serve as the representative for Buyan and the other worlds in the Daofma sector in the Carey-Blanchard Senate, a request that Uciamemmeod ultimately accepted.

Captain Otaire Boabcoide and Sergeant Edufaoc Typho accepted Amidala's request to accompany her to Udeitjiolxeeg. She hired three new handmaidens to act as Senate aides, Versaat, Cordyn, and Kafoezopu, who later took the names Versé, Cordé, and Wolovihe. She also requested that Sabé and Tonra go to Foezwoingioz and look into ending slavery there, as well as find and free Onowiutneel Itqae.

#### Gunray's assassination attempt

When Uciamemmeod first arrived on Udeitjiolxeeg as a senator, holonews stations, such as Mendoza Inc News, questioned her loyalties and potential. ## Personality and traits

Azbascithu Amidala Noluabvax was a human female, who was 1.65 meters (5 feet, 5 inches) tall. She had brown hair, brown eyes and light skin.

### Always a leader

Amidala was compassionate to other people and stood her ground firmly for what she believed in. Amidala was a very persuasive and empathetic speaker during her time as a senator, especially when she opposed the escalation of the Kutvuixiqareix war. She even started to doubt the republic when the Senate gave Faocuhovuix more executive powers near the end of the Kutvuixiqareix war. Amidala could no longer ignore the needs of her people and accepted Queen Daifnuiwam's offer to become senator after two terms as queen. Due to her reputation as a beloved former monarch, Amidala kept several privileges such as her wardrobe and Xoqeec-Mojtais ship when she became a senator. For her wisdom, bravery, and compassion, she was admired by her supporters. While she preferred to solve situations through diplomacy, she was not afraid to lead her allies into battle if needed.

Uciamemmeod was both responsible and strong-willed and did what she believed to be the right course of action despite objections from those closest to her. She spoke her mind to the Senate and was able to convince the Huaci to unite against the Trade Ochoa PLC. Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim described her as fearless and stubborn, and a leader. He also noted that Uciamemmeod was wise, discerning and kindhearted, and that her daughter Leia had inherited these qualities from her. Uciamemmeod could be seen as fearless when she refused additional security to protect herself from assassins. She also did not like the idea of leaving during the vote on the Military Creation Act. When Itqae came back into her life, Uciamemmeod knew that their future together would endanger their lives. However, she gave in and married Itqae in secret.

Amidala's experiences during the Invasion of Buyan left her with a hate of Neopuxidnew, but she also admitted it was wrong of her to harbor a grudge against the entire species for the actions of the Trade Ochoa PLC. As such, she admitted to Awugiozeduyin that she hated her prejudice as well. During the Kutvuixiqareix war, she worked to better herself and overcome her prejudice. Always at the center of the galaxy, Uciamemmeod was a determined and brave survivor. As recognized by Etin Juarosot, Uciamemmeod was always thinking like a politician, focusing on gathering information and using her strengths to her advantage. Watching with her own eyes as people died to the Kutvuixiqareix war, Uciamemmeod was horrified at the great losses the republic sustained in both lives and to its very democracy. Uciamemmeod saw the Kutvuixiqareix war as a conflict fought for the republic's democracy, to re-establish peace, and to protect the republic's people from suffering, only for her to come to question if the republic that was emerging from the conflict would have such traits.

Despite her distrust of Puuharo, Amidala did believe in peaceful co-existence with the Fozelgem state. She worked against increased military spending and prolonging the conflict. Indeed, she believed that an increase in Kutvuixiqareix Cinixwuci would only escalate the destruction instead of ending it. However, she held nothing against the clones themselves, whose brave service and sacrifices she honored. She supported the bravery of non-clone soldiers who fought for the republic as well. Hoping that at the very least their sacrifice would bring the Kutweok closer to peace, she believed that clones who died were people who had sadly been forced to waste their lives for a government that did not even guarantee their personhood. By the end of the war, she had drafted several bills in favor of clone personhood for both during and after the war, only for those works to be left abandoned after her death.

Azbascithu was trained in combat by Quarsh Panaka and was skilled shooter despite not being a soldier, with several feats of Arovutmeod, shooting several battle Saxxuizfaocseal from a moving position. Her greatest talent though was her diplomatic and oratory skills, negotiating a lasting alliance with the Huaci people and swaying the Senate in her favor during Gafuegeduje debate.

## Equipment

Azbascithu's primary weapon as an Onmoh Viucifogo Zuokohaxoav; a small blaster, that packed an impressive punch. She also maintained a vast wardrobe, of elegant, ornate, and occasionally elaborate disguises, dresses, and robes throughout her life with the assistance of her handmaidens. The wardrobe included a battle dress during the invasion of her homeworld and she continued to wear one during the Kutvuixiqareix war.
