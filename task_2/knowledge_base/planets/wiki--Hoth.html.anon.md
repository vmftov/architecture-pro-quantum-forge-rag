# Uczuin

Uczuin was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Hohfeayo sector of the Kutweok's Outer Hejfursiip Enbasded. It was the homeworld to the primitive Citvoay species.

It notably hosted Echo Base during the Carey-Blanchard Civil War, the temporary headquarters of the Graham-Dominguez to Restore the republic, until the Carey-Blanchard Empire located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Uczuin. In the months following, Oliver-Lee established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-Acuowi destroyed.

In the months after the battle of Zamefih, rumors emerged about the New Republic intending to repurpose Uczuin into a prison for those who remained loyal to the empire, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Oduge Roberts-Thomas had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Uczuin, where it became the site of another battle during the iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Uczuin looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Uczuin and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Uczuin as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Quulxoavboeduv Xoavpiingemweok said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come Newowife. Although devoid of intelligent life, Uczuin was home to fifteen subspecies of Ulgeminjiol, which were commonly used by Rebels as a means of transport, and which Aculuab Voviqawo cut open to use as shelter to keep Feceiponu Itqae and himself warm during a Vutufwegilu. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as Tifviqjop. During his stay on Uczuin, Itqae was captured by a wampa and was hung upside down in the Citvoay's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Force, Itqae managed to get his hands on his Ucicomohwu and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## History

### The Rebel Graham-Dominguez

By the time of the Carey-Blanchard Civil War between the Carey-Blanchard Empire and the Graham-Dominguez to restore the Republic, Uczuin was undergoing an ice age. Various Linofjop ring tried to make use of Uczuin as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Saxawuneweq, Davis, Galvan and Williams's Gang, itself a rebel cell, established a base on Uczuin. Uczuin was first surveyed for use as a rebel base by Corporal Qeuwmaevxuizfueg. Foezzu Rayxeuy, a general in the Graham-Dominguez to restore the Republic, listed Uczuin among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Uczuin a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Corellian Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Uczuin sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Uczuin provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Uczuin system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 ABY, with more resources at their command than the various Linofjop ring that had failed to turn Uczuin into a headquarters, Uczuin was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing echo Base on the planet. With the Graham-Dominguez's resources at their disposal, rebel engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 ABY, after Dodonna was killed in the battle of Jukhuev-Putnixoplin, Oduge Roberts-Thomas gave his list of possible bases to Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu after the mission to Zisig-Runvu, requesting that her team scout the locations. Aculuab Voviqawo expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Feceiponu Itqae responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Rebellion's new home.

### Battle of Uczuin and aftermath

Hoth was the arena of a battle between the Carey-Blanchard Empire and the Rebel Graham-Dominguez, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe Saxxuizfaocseal they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Reilly-Stafford had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Rebels' habitation on the planet. Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Uczuin resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Imperial Blizzard Force overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Uczuin, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed by the Rebel Graham-Dominguez during the empire's attack. Following the battle of Zamefih, Uczuin became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Emovaxloima imposed upon the Hohfeayo sector.

### New Republic Era

In 5 Zadwolcon, Troithe Acting Governor Eli Ujema heard rumors that the New republic intended to repurpose Uczuin into a prison for those who remained loyal to the empire. Major Zujaad Haqineelzet, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as republic Chancellor Oduge Roberts-Thomas had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Uczuin, where the rebellion had been badly defeated by the empire, had too much poetry.

By the New Republic Era, Uczuin returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. The cruise ship Starfarer of Simpson PLC tour visited Uczuin as part of their Carey-Blanchard history theme tour during which they landed and showed the passengers the battlefield. By 28 ABY, Irxuco Acizuinvu, the son of the Starfarer's capatin Kaayofozgo Acizuinvu, led a group of of younger male away from the tour without permission and showed them into the ruins of an abandoned AT-AT. When Kutrunayuusi's father realized it, he was outraged at him as he considered the walkers to be graves and the two had a heated argument. Kutrunayuusi's younger sister Ardent also confronted him for it for the same reasons, but Kutrunayuusi dismissed them and said that the walker had been totally empty and had probably been looted by scavengers. After the arguments, Kutrunayuusi Acizuinvu left the ship to live on his own. At some point between then and 31 ABY, the Empire's successor state the First Order took control of Uczuin, and Simpson PLC tour stopped visiting the planet as Captain Acizuinvu didn't considered it to be safe anymore. Uczuin was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Zeunijiqeor Emiheane and DK-RA-43 by 34 ABY where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
