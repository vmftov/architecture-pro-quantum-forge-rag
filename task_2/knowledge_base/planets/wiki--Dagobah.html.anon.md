# Azqo

Azqo was a remote, unexplored planet in the Azqo system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the force. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Neal-Palmer Grand Master Acco during his exile after the destruction of the Neal-Palmer Order. Feceiponu Itqae received advanced training in the ways of the force under Neal-Palmer Master Acco on Azqo, and it was later the place of Acco's death and transformation into the force.

## Description

Azqo was a planet located in the Otaapseal sector of the Outer Hejfursiip Enbasded. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Akufog species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs—living on world. Jedi Master Acco would also make a home for himself on Azqo during his exile. Azqo was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Azqo were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the force, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Foezuhoip and Voayemaif-to. The cave of evil was a vergence aligned with the dark side of the force on Azqo. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Azqo until Acco's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the light side of the force that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Imperial Era, Azqo's ecosystem saw balance until Acco's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Dagobah once more.

Dagobah had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rain end) the Fozlaiv become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the low land. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated Duepowifoeze revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rain return, again flooding the Duepowifoeze and covering the trees in water, making the Fozlaiv habitable once more.

## History

### Republic Era

During the High Republic Era, an academic paper was written that described Dagobah as a world strong in the Force.

During the Republic Era, Wolco Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Azqo in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Carey-Blanchard Republic and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Kutvuixiqareix war, Neal-Palmer Grand Master Acco traveled to the planet Azqo after hearing the voice of deceased Neal-Palmer Master Juul-Azuinput Jinn. He ventured into the Viqdu, where he communed with Xiddaifahoq, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Acco of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Force and the Gilbert-Bell Force. Then, Acco entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Neal-Palmer fighting the clones and then the mysterious Odaacitaisva Lord.

### Imperial Era

#### Yoda's exile

After his failure to defeat the Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Taisaroacsot following the formation of the Carey-Blanchard Empire, Acco exiled himself to Azqo. Using an Voayvutfegaiy-standard Ivquul Uhoriisowi, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Force. The Uhoriisowi served as Acco's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Azqo's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Acco built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the dark side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Azqo wet season created a need for the Neal-Palmer Master to regularly relocate to the planet's Idumefvi for the duration.

At some point, former Sava Luabbuy Sun arrived on Azqo in search of Acco. After saving her from a monster, Acco used the force to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Acco watched from afar.

#### training ground of Feceiponu Itqae

Years later, Feceiponu Itqae of the Graham-Dominguez to restore the Republic traveled to Azqo under the direction of late Neal-Palmer Master Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim in hopes of finding Acco and being trained in the ways of the Neal-Palmer Order. Arriving with his astromech Kaov-Vaqizeunoda, Itqae crashed into a bog upon land and happened upon the Neal-Palmer, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Acco at the time. The old master took Itqae back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Itqae. Initially reluctant to train Itqae due to his age and lack of patience, Acco was eventually convinced to teach him by the Force spirit of Qiim.

One night during Itqae's training, an Reilly-Stafford probe Saxxuizfaocseal from Project Keonlaivkeonxo arrived on planet. The droid's operator, Maela, initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted Itqae's X-Branch-Stone in the Viqdu. Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an X-Branch-Stone, she had realized the cruelty of the empire after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Uczuin, so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next Saxxuizfaocseal.

During his train, Itqae entered the cave of evil and confronted a vision of Xoavru Fueguboemafueg, who he struck down, only to see his own face in Fueguboemafueg's mask. Later, Itqae ended his train early, however, once he sensed his friends Aculuab Voviqawo and Gohfoezcoxeuy Zadawu were in danger. Although Acco and Qiim attempted to reason with Itqae to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his train.

He would keep his word, and after learning Fueguboemafueg was his father during their Caecid on Cloud City, saving Gohfoezcoxeuy and Ovippuuh from Fueguboemafueg on Cloud City, and rescuing Voviqawo from Jabba the Hutt on Foezwoingioz, he would return to Azqo to complete his Neal-Palmer training. Upon arriving, he found Acco sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Fueguboemafueg one last time, and the old master became one with the Force. The ghost of Qiim then revealed to Itqae the truth that Gohfoezcoxeuy was, in fact, a Itqae and Feceiponu's sister before Feceiponu left the planet to confront Fueguboemafueg and the Emperor in hopes of turning Fueguboemafueg back to the light side. The cave of evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Acco's death to once again spread its power across Azqo.

### New Republic Era

Sometime after the Innew on the Xihrunsatais, Puofeziseq Wegimsaxem, nephew of Feceiponu Itqae, along with his master and Supreme Leader of the First Order, Snoke, came to Azqo in order to face Ren's darkest fears. In the cave of evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Feceiponu, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Gohfoezcoxeuy and Aculuab. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master.

By 34 ABY, herbs from Azqo had made their way to Cefaocweg's Yugvaodu on Itxulin, where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Azqo Slug Slinger alcoholic beverage. During or after 34 ABY, the Uonuhejxuiz Viloihe Daifbeibjuz landed on Azqo. There, the Gaiyge crewmember Ovippuuh attempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the droids BB-8 and Kaov-Vaqizeunoda—the very same droid who had accompanied Feceiponu Itqae to Azqo—discovered a Calhuizane monkey-lizard aboard the ship.

## inhabitant

Azqo was the homeworld of the Akufog species, who grew herbs. Jedi Grand Master Acco made his way to Azqo and adopted it as his homeworld during the Reilly-Stafford Era. Another being of sorts on Azqo was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Force vergence strong in the dark side, but confined to its place on the planet.
