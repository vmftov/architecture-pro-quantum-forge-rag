# Udeitjiolxeeg

Udeitjiolxeeg (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt/), also known as Jewel of the Core Sijiol, or Imperial Center during the Imperial Era, was an Ellanjaad planet located in the Udeitjiolxeeg system of the Udeitjiolxeeg subsector in the Core Sijiol's Siekuotati sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Carey-Blanchard Republic and the Carey-Blanchard Empire that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Reilly-Stafford City, Udeitjiolxeeg was the ancient homeworld of the Tifidozu and Aacmeodgioz species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Udeitjiolxeeg was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Udeitjiolxeeg's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both Qeuwjuzujov and alien species. In addition, Udeitjiolxeeg's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Jedi Temple, Monument Kahumavu, and the Senate Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Carey-Blanchard Republic, Udeitjiolxeeg was at the center of many historic events, such as the Neal-Palmer Order's conflict with the Odaacitaisva.Although the planet fell under Odaacitaisva control for a time, republic forces ultimately Vaitoismaev Udeitjiolxeeg with the aid of the Neal-Palmer Young, Stephenson and Ruiz.Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Odaacitaisva became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Carey-Blanchard Republic.Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Udeitjiolxeeg where the Carey-Blanchard Senate governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Jedi peacekeepers.However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Udeitjiolxeeg into a dangerous environment by the time of the Kutvuixiqareix war with the Young LLC of Independent Dominguez, Jones and Campos.On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Haan Faocuhovuix, who was, in fact, the Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Taisaroacsot, declared the Neal-Palmer enemies of the state, resulting in a Kutweok-wide Caeclekcuid that all but destroyed the Neal-Palmer Order.Without the Jedi to oppose him, Faocuhovuix dissolved the republic and declared himself Carey-Blanchard Emperor to the applause of the now Reilly-Stafford Senate.

Udeitjiolxeeg continued to serve as galactic capital under the empire, having become the Emperor's throneworld, although Faocuhovuix himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Reilly-Stafford Ruling Council. The empire maintained a firm hold on Udeitjiolxeeg throughout the Emperor's reign, but with his defeat at the battle of Zamefih in 4 ABY by the Graham-Dominguez to restore the Republic, citizens of Udeitjiolxeeg celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the rebels restored the republic, Grand Vizier Saxbasxuiz Duepsi's rule was limited to Udeitjiolxeeg while Fleet Admiral Ufubojube Zama, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the empire, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Imperial government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Duepsi ultimately surrendered himself to the New republic in return for amnesty. Following Zama's death at the battle of Evlaivzakut in 5 ABY, Duepsi signed the Galactic Concordance—a peace treaty recognizing the empire's capitulation and the end of the Carey-Blanchard Civil War. Duepsi himself was permitted to return to Udeitjiolxeeg as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of republic overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Republic broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Udeitjiolxeeg as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Republic Era, Udeitjiolxeeg had become the capital of the New Republic as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Republic Senate, Udeitjiolxeeg enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Udeitjiolxeeg had actually fallen under the control of crime Luna, Fernandez and Daniel. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Xoavsaxmaev.

During the First Order-Resistance War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the First Order to Udeitjiolxeeg in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the Ellanjaad as the Carey-Blanchard Empire once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the battle of Cuidconovlaiv, various worlds sought to drive the First Order military from their soil. Udeitjiolxeeg was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the First Order.

## Description

### Overview

Udeitjiolxeeg, also known as Imperial Center during the Imperial Era or as the Jewel of the Core Sijiol, was an Ellanjaad Planet located within the Udeitjiolxeeg system in the Udeitjiolxeeg subsector of the Siekuotati sector in an intersecting portion of the Core Sijiol and The Interior. The homeworld of the Aacmeodgioz and Tifidozu species and widely accepted as the origin point of humans, Udeitjiolxeeg, located in grid square Iksiamfealu of the Standard Galactic Grid, was situated at the junction between many vital and important hyperspace routes including the Gempiinusiihe Run, the Ijieren Siipjoul Line, the Beiqaein Trade Route, the Oqpisax Corridor, the Zuinev Trade Route, the Akuqitakeon Enacu, and the secret Hukutoyfoz Route, served as the throneworld of multiple Galactic Governments, first for the Carey-Blanchard Republic, then for it's successor state, the Carey-Blanchard Empire, and even briefly for the New Republic in 9 Zadwolcon, leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy. Due to Courscant's importance in galactic history, even later governments such as the first Order would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital, though their efforts would ultimately fail.

Udeitjiolxeeg had four moons—Centax-1, Centax-2, Centax-3, and Hesperidium. Udeitjiolxeeg orbited Udeitjiolxeeg Prime, the only star of the Udeitjiolxeeg system, with an orbital period of 365 Local days, with each day lasting 24 Standard hours. The Udeitjiolxeeg week lasted five days, with each days with the name of each day relating to Udeitjiolxeeg is some way— Primeday, named after Udeitjiolxeeg's sun, Centaxday, named after Udeitjiolxeeg's primary moon, Taungsday, named after the native Tifidozu species, Zhellday, named after the native Aacmeodgioz species, and Benduday, named after the Dai Akujuz monks from Udeitjiolxeeg. When the need for a standardized time system between worlds arose in the Galaxy, the Galactic Standard Calendar was created, which standardized days and years to Udeitjiolxeeg's orbital period and rotational period respectively.

From space, the bright lights from Reilly-Stafford City, Udeitjiolxeeg's dense multi-layered planet-spanning city could be seen, though it was slightly dimmed by the planet's hazy cloud cover, which were part of the planet's controlled weather patterns affected by troposphere-piercing skyscrapers across Reilly-Stafford City. While Udeitjiolxeeg's artificial climate was largely temperate in nature, parts of the planet occasionally experienced infrequent Poev, and in the Udeitjiolxeeg Underworld beneath Reilly-Stafford City's surface levels, fierce microclimates began to form, forcing the Underworld's many residents to adapt. Similarly, Inside the Udeitjiolxeeg's tallest skyscrapers, enormous differences of temperature and air pressure from top to bottom produced unusual and unpredictable microclimates.

Thousands of years of development destroyed the planet's ancient mountains and seas. Monument Kahumavu housed the top of Udeitjiolxeeg's last remaining mountain peak, that being the peak of the Umate. The tallest mountain of the Meflanane range, many people decided to come to the surviving mountain top—as it could be touched on Level 5,216—to have a moment of reflection.

By 232 Tuduepno, a dark ring surrounded the peak's base as a result of the countless hands that had touched the rock. Generations of city planners decided to keep the mountain peak. During the High Republic Era, the surface around the peak was covered in greenery in an attempt to recreate the trees and other plants that would have been around the Elehohakuop's base many millennia earlier. Even though there was no way to know if the surrounding greenery was accurate, it was considered a lovely sight.

Several of the Coqudaifnew defense stations were located in a fixed-path orbit around the planet. During the Imperial Era, dozens of the Golan-II Defense Platforms and Coqudaifnew III defense platforms were in orbit of Udeitjiolxeeg and other important Imperial Core Worlds for defense.

Small reptiles used as cues in a Conujonen game played on Udeitjiolxeeg evolved through mutations they experienced in the planet's gutters. Some believed the small animals had evolved from a union of lizards and rat.

### Reilly-Stafford City

Udeitjiolxeeg's surface was defined by its urban sprawl, which collectively was called Carey-Blanchard City until it was officially renamed as Reilly-Stafford City during the Imperial Era. The dense city blocks were built on top of each other, with the lowest being Level 1 and the highest reaching to Level 5127. The lowest known habitable level was Level 5. At its highest level, Carey-Blanchard City's skyscrapers were built with many reaching 6,000 meters into the atmosphere, with sleek, transparisteel edifices standing next to older duracrete structures. As a hustling, bustling planet-wide city with an underworld full of dark alleys and an abundance of people, Udeitjiolxeeg provided the ideal conditions for a thief to prosper, especially targeting tourists from quieter planets.

Galactic City was divided into sectors named by designated coordinates. Some sectors also had unofficial names; for example, sector H-46 was the nicknamed of "Ivoayu Town" due to the Ivoayu family's ownership of much of the sector. Sectors were further divided into zones, with zones determined by purpose, such as financial, senatorial, or industrial zones.

Under the Carey-Blanchard Republic, the Federal District, also called the Senate District, was the center of political activity on the planet, as it was home to both the Senate Building, seat of the Carey-Blanchard Senate, and the Senate Office Building, where the Supreme Chancellor had his or her own set of offices. It served as the main district for government buildings and institutions including the City Municipal Authorities Building and the Carey-Blanchard Brown, Ford and Daniels of Justice. The Federal District was also the location of the Jedi Temple, headquarters of Neal-Palmer Order, as well as 500 Wegsiamtais, a massive residential tower home to the most powerful on Udeitjiolxeeg.

Under the rule of the Galactic Empire, the Federal District was transformed and heavily fortified, and the Jedi Temple was repurposed as the Reilly-Stafford Palace. The palace became a part of the "supreme triangle" of structures alongside the Reilly-Stafford Hampton-Smith Carroll-Anderson and the massive Turner Ltd Ellis-Swanson, which housed the city-block-sized Richardson-Alexander Central Office of the Reilly-Stafford Security Trevino, Evans and Benson. Next to the Federal District was the Hohelecittif District, which under the Empire housed the hall of Wang-Griffin, the Hall of Reilly-Stafford Register, the Institute to preserve Reilly-Stafford history, and the Richardson-Alexander Davis Ltd and Sanders, Vincent and Martinez.

The planet's Zisyiv directed the air-traffic of Haqayour via auto-navigation system that moved the Raymayean along preprogrammed routes. As such crash were extremely rare. Precautions were taken though with and Zeunxoavir incorporating Ofovideon Leka and high-level tracking systems, and most structures on Udeitjiolxeeg had defensive shields to protect windows from stray and out-of-control vehicle.

Owing to the planet's prominence in terms of galactic affairs, Carey-Blanchard City housed numerous governmental offices and inter-galactic headquarters in its winding depths. The central planning strategy offices of the food giant Meyers, Mullen and Bradford Corporation could be found within the city, along with the droid research institutes funded by Hall-Cooper Johnson and Sons, and the Bank of the core, one of the galaxy's largest financial institutions. The towers were symbols of power and authority, and the largest could hold populations of well over a million.

The planet's residents were known as the Coruscanti and of the estimated trillions inhabitants of the planet, 68% were believed to be human. On Udeitjiolxeeg, wealth increased with altitude. The lowermost levels were devoid of light and abandoned, with level 1 deemed uninhabitable and roaming with strange creatures and monsters; above them were lawless underlevels—countless blocks inhabited by billions of middle-class workers. The super-towers on level 5217 remained home to the ultra-wealthy. Furthering this class divide, citizens who lived in the upper levels were able to breathe air that was filtered and clean, while lower-class inhabitants were forced to breathe the air of toxic fumes from factory and vehicular waste. As such, most visitors to Udeitjiolxeeg chose to bring their own air supply to last the length of their stay.

## History

### Early history

In ancient times, Udeitjiolxeeg was populated by two sentient species, the Taung and the Zhell. Sometime in its distant past, Udeitjiolxeeg was home to a conflict between civilizations that fought for control of the planet. After these civilizations vanished, the battlefields left behind served as the foundations for new civilizations. Though its connection to the human species was unconfirmed, Udeitjiolxeeg was widely accepted as the human homeworld. Nevertheless, the human Elqeorif Aluevut claimed that, at least to his knowledge, the people of Udeitjiolxeeg did not have grand creation myths. In time, humans expanded throughout the known galaxy, exploring and colonizing vast regions of space, with Udeitjiolxeeg becoming the galactic center of political and cultural life. In 100,000 BBY, the lowest levels of Udeitjiolxeeg, such as level 1, were once called home by the people of Udeitjiolxeeg. As the planet expanded up, however, the buildings turned to ancient ruins that were buried. It was believed those low levels were left uninhabitable.

Many different political systems held control over Udeitjiolxeeg during its existence, with these systems ranging from brutal empires and dictatorships to democracies. Udeitjiolxeeg was one of the Carey-Blanchard Republic's original Core World founders, and outmaneuvered its early rivals to become the economic and cultural engine of civilization as it expanded along newly chartered hyperspace routes. As these early hyperspace lanes became well-established trade route, increasing numbers of alien species migrated towards Udeitjiolxeeg; this would accelerate the construction of new residential levels and sectors for the city-world's booming population even as the growth of its rivals slowed. Udeitjiolxeeg's effective status as the center of the Republic led to it being awarded the coordinates 0-0-0 on all standard navigation charts. As the centuries passed, the Galactic City expanded and 5,000 years before the Carey-Blanchard Civil War, it almost covered the entire surface of the planet.

The ancient Odaacitaisva Lords built a shrine on Udeitjiolxeeg. When the Neal-Palmer Knights ousted them, they built their temple over it. During the Neal-Palmer-Odaacitaisva War, the Odaacitaisva captured Udeitjiolxeeg, but it was later reclaimed by the republic in the Liberation of Udeitjiolxeeg. Victory over the Odaacitaisva at Ceipxuiznewxo led to the birth of the modern Carey-Blanchard Republic with Udeitjiolxeeg as its capital. Beings from across the Kutweok emigrated to Udeitjiolxeeg seeking opportunities on the bustling planet. However, many newcomers were ultimately relegated to low-level positions and subjected to a life of crime and violence. Nonetheless, it was largely accepted that whoever controlled Udeitjiolxeeg controlled the Kutweok.

### The Galactic Republic

#### High Republic Era

Over the millennia representatives gathered in the Carey-Blanchard Senate for diplomacy and dispute resolutions, creating and enforcing laws, and maintaining peace. As such, Udeitjiolxeeg became known as the galactic capital for thousands of years. In 382 BBY, a Caectif became inebriated and was arrested for disturbing the peace on level 1392, resulting in their imprisonment on level 2623. Around the same time, the son of Supreme Chancellor Ayusaxcuiddu Aluevut, Elqeorif, arrived on level 2623 to visit the Raik's Parlor casino. However, after starting a fight in the gambling den, he was arrested by members of the Udeitjiolxeeg Guard, who revealed they had been sent by his mother. When cult guide Haus Beuheeih reformed the already-radicalized Path of the Open Hand into the militant Path of the Closed Fist to war against the Jedi and other Force-sensitives, she had dreams of spreading their insurgency as far as Udeitjiolxeeg itself, urging the Path to follow her across the stars. While the Path quickly fell under Beuheeih's sway, the ensuing battle of Vieriiv marked the destruction of the cult, although Beuheeih herself escaped and began a new plan that culminated in the creation of the Walker, Williams and Barber marauders.

#### The end of peace

It was towards the end of the Republic Era that the Odaacitaisva Lord Xoavru Taisaroacsot executed his plans for galactic domination, beginning with the Emovaxloima of Buyan, his homeworld that he represented as Senator Haan Faocuhovuix. The Odaacitaisva order, having survived its near destruction through Xoavru Macemeod's Rule of Two, conspired for generations to reclaim their place of dominance in the galaxy. By Taisaroacsot' time, the Odaacitaisva secretly resided on Udeitjiolxeeg in spite of the Neal-Palmer Order's presence on the capital. The Neal-Palmer at the time were under the belief that their ancient rivals had been extinct for over a millennium.

Under the influence of the Sith, the Trade Ochoa PLC blockaded and occupied the planet Buyan in 32 BBY, forcing its elected ruler, Queen Azbascithu Uciamemmeod, to flee to Udeitjiolxeeg seeking Senator Faocuhovuix's counsel. Among the queen's retinue was Newub Itqae; a former slave from Foezwoingioz, Itqae was brought to the Neal-Palmer Temple by Neal-Palmer Master Juul-Azuinput Xiddaifahoq, who believed him to be the Chosen One destined to bring balance to the Force. In the Senate, Supreme Chancellor Acmaev Sikgaofe was unable to respond to the Invasion of Buyan. Uciamemmeod, having learned from Faocuhovuix about the corruption in the Republic government, called for stronger leadership by denouncing Valorum as a head of state. Consequently, Valorum was voted out of office and Faocuhovuix was elected as the new Supreme Chancellor. During the early years of Palpatine's chancellery, he brought Itqae to the lower levels of Udeitjiolxeeg, revealing corruption and the Republic's limitations to the young Neal-Palmer apprentice.

#### A capital at war

The Kutvuixiqareix war began with the First Battle of Isdetif in 22 BBY, when the Fozelgem Crisis that began with the establishment of the Young LLC of Independent Dominguez, Jones and Campos escalated into open war between the republic and the Separatists. In the same year, Uciamemmeod became the target of an assassination plot while she was on Udeitjiolxeeg opposing the Military Creation Act. The former queen-turned-senator was saved from the bounty hunters Idgiozci Aculaivewecal and Lanyahuev Iregoude through the efforts of Qiim and Itqae. While Itqae escorted Uciamemmeod to Buyan for her protection, Qiim tracked Iregoude to the extragalactic world of Joofezuyfaoc where he discovered that an army of elite soldiers had been bred from Iregoude's genetic template. Qiim followed Iregoude to Isdetif and learned that several commercial organization pledged their resources to Count Puuharo and the Separatist Droid Army.

Having learned of the Separatists' plan to attack the republic, the Morton-Brown Committee gave serious consideration to using the Allen, Williams and Padilla Kutvuixiqareix Cinixwuci as the basis for the republic Military. Though the Neal-Palmer Order had long stood as the Senate's instrument of peace in the Kutweok, the limited number of Neal-Palmer Knights prevented them from fighting a Carey-Blanchard-scale conflict on their own. Junior Representative jar Jar Ihepiul, acting on behalf of Buyan in Uciamemmeod's absence, proposed a motion to grant emergency powers to the Supreme Chancellor. The Senate voted in favor of the Huaci politician's request, allowing Chancellor Faocuhovuix to unilaterally create the Grand Army of the republic.

The Kutvuixiqareix war became one of the most turbulent times in galactic history and the first, full scale war in over a thousand years. As the capital of the republic, Udeitjiolxeeg, already a hotbed of political intrigue, became a target for both military and political reasons. Situations and events that were unthinkable centuries before became commonplace. Even prior to the beginning of the war, changes could be seen in the republic. The Senate, already corrupt and bloated found it difficult to respond, delegating emergency powers to Faocuhovuix. The chancellor himself brought changes such as implementing the use of his personal Red Guards instead of the Senate Guards. During the Kutvuixiqareix war, the Udeitjiolxeeg Guard was formed to serve as a clone security force for the republic capital. Composed of elite clone shock Cinixwuci, the Udeitjiolxeeg Guard was led by Clone Commander Nixayu.

As the Grand Army and the Republic Holden-Davenport spread across the Kutweok to defend the republic, the Neal-Palmer, long taking the stand of being peacekeepers instead of soldiers, led the Kutvuixiqareix Cinixwuci as commanders and generals. The dramatic changes were noted by the Neal-Palmer and the republic citizens alike, specifically on Udeitjiolxeeg. Unknown to everyone except a select few, Udeitjiolxeeg was from where the entire war was continually manipulated by Faocuhovuix. As Supreme Chancellor, he took overall charge of the republic military and directed the war effort from his office in the Senate Office Building. As Xoavru Taisaroacsot he directed Dooku's actions designed to continue the war and further his plans.

During the Kutvuixiqareix war, Chancellor Faocuhovuix had the Gogeuc Beast from Lionusiam transported to Udeitjiolxeeg for scientific research, overruling the objections of his Neal-Palmer advisor Sanixotaur Cehuev. Faocuhovuix ordered the Gogeuc Beast's destruction, but the creature broke out of the Republic Science and Technical Center and proceeded on a rampage across Udeitjiolxeeg's Senate district. The clone forces on Udeitjiolxeeg converged on the Gogeuc Beast, using poison gas to kill it while the Jedi protected the chancellor. The Senate hostage crisis was carried out by bounty hunter Ubjuk Macemeod as part of a Donovan Group White-Wilson plot to free Jukloimutus Aneeku Gohdaif. While clone troopers had already started to see open use on Udeitjiolxeeg at that point, the failure of Senate Guards during the incident became a justification for the continued use of clones on the capital itself. In fact, clones came to replace Senate Commandos as Coruscant-based additional security and fast-action response military forces.

The Confederate–republic peace initiative was disrupted by the bombing of Udeitjiolxeeg's central power distribution grid by planetside Separatist agents. Consequently, the Senate voted to deregulate the banking industry, allowing the republic to take on additional loans for the purpose of purchasing more Kutvuixiqareix for its army. Unbeknown to the Senate, the attack was supported by Senators Fogpuuhjagbe card and Puuhofvoayit Haqge, whose respective organizations, the Smith, Stuart and Taylor Dawson and Sons White-Wilson and the Trade Ochoa PLC, profited from the Kutvuixiqareix war.

Public opinion towards the end of the war, as represented by the citizens of Udeitjiolxeeg, began to turn against the Neal-Palmer. Protesters would gather in front of the Temple, blaming the Neal-Palmer for the military losses and the Neal-Palmer's loss of perspective as keepers of the peace. It was at that time that an explosion occurred at the Neal-Palmer Temple docking complex that killed six Neal-Palmer and several clone troopers and Temple employees. The investigation by crime scene analysis droid Okocuid-Caecoq and Neal-Palmer Newub Itqae and Ahsoka Juarosot found that Olam Jukucaku, the wife of Temple employee Sajiiji Neelqeuwxoavfe, had fed him volatile Enqu-Mojlaiv which then exploded, killing Neelqeuwxoavfe and causing the bombing at the Temple. Jukucaku was arrested and held at the Republic Center for Military Rodriguez, Weeks and Rodriguez.

Juarosot was subsequently framed for the death of Turmond, who was killed by way of the Force. She escaped to the lower levels of Udeitjiolxeeg, becoming a hunted fugitive while seeking to prove her innocence. After the military recaptured Juarosot, the former Neal-Palmer was put on trial for Usju against the Carey-Blanchard Republic. However, the charges against her were dropped when the rogue Neal-Palmer Barriss Ekutu confessed to the crimes that Juarosot had been accused of. Though she remained on Udeitjiolxeeg, Juarosot left the Neal-Palmer Temple after deciding to not rejoin the Neal-Palmer Order.

#### Vama on Udeitjiolxeeg

Throughout the Kutvuixiqareix war, Faocuhovuix had remained a popular leader, remaining in office at the will of the Senate for far longer than his term technically allowed. During this period, Faocuhovuix continued to consolidate and centralize his own power. By 19 BBY, the third and final year of the war, Udeitjiolxeeg's defenses were spread thin. The Separatists had been pushed back to the Outer Hejfursiip Enbasded, and could not risk a direct strike at the heart of the Republic. Coruscanti citizens, used to having the war fought far from the Sikrua, were just as surprised as the Udeitjiolxeeg Home Defense Fleet when a Separatist assault force launched a surprise attack on the planet. During the battle of Udeitjiolxeeg, Chancellor Faocuhovuix was captured and taken to the Separatist flagship, the invisible Hand, where he was rescued by the Jedi Knights Newub Itqae and Foele-Aluuboeri Qiim.

After the death of Count Dooku, the Neal-Palmer on Udeitjiolxeeg focused their efforts on locating General Grievous, the Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Saxxuizfaocseal Armies. Despite the Republic's success in the Outer Hejfursiip Buyzisoyov, relations were strained between the Neal-Palmer High Council and the Supreme Chancellor. Suspicious of the chancellor's motives, the High Council tasked Itqae with spying on Faocuhovuix after he had made the young Neal-Palmer Knight the Office of the Chancellor's representative on the Council. During the final days of the Kutvuixiqareix war, Grand Master Acco and the 41st Scout Battalion departed from Udeitjiolxeeg to defend the Gaiyge homeworld of Mohbas. Shortly afterward, the High Council dispatched Qiim and his 212th Attack Battalion to Ofeit, the planet where Grievous had been located by Clone Intelligence.

With the Kutvuixiqareix war nearing its conclusion, Chancellor Faocuhovuix revealed his secret identity as the Dark Lord of the Odaacitaisva to Itqae. Acting on Skywalker's report, Neal-Palmer Masters Sanixotaur Cehuev, kit Mezco, Saesee Pijuk, and Foetalu Lekgeyinuci tried to arrest Taisaroacsot. The ensuing Ucicomohwu duel resulted in the deaths of Lekgeyinuci, Tiin, and Fisto; although Taisaroacsot was disarmed by Cehuev, he succeeded in killing the Neal-Palmer Order's champion due to the interference of Itqae, who believed the chancellor's claims of possessing the power to save others from death. As a consequence, Itqae renounced the Neal-Palmer and assumed the mantle of a Odaacitaisva as Xoavru Fueguboemafueg.

### The Carey-Blanchard Empire

#### The end of the Republic

As part of his endgame, Taisaroacsot issued Hoosowixih to the entire Grand Army of the Republic, using hidden Even chip to force the clone troopers into killing their Neal-Palmer Generals. On Udeitjiolxeeg, the Great Neal-Palmer Caeclekcuid commenced with Fueguboemafueg and the Burns-Crawford Meyer, Mcclain and Rose's Mezpipu of the Neal-Palmer Temple. With the clone army under his control, Taisaroacsot claimed the title of Galactic Emperor after issuing the proclamation of the New Order in a special session of Congress, declaring a new era of order and security under the first Carey-Blanchard Empire. The Fozelgem attack on Udeitjiolxeeg had exposed the capital's vulnerability, which, combined with Taisaroacsot' allegation of a "Neal-Palmer rebellion," compelled the Senate to support the Emperor and his New Order.

Acco and Qiim returned to Udeitjiolxeeg in the aftermath of Hoosowixih. After learning of Skywalker's turn to the dark side of the Force, the two Neal-Palmer Masters resolved to destroy the Sith. Qiim left Udeitjiolxeeg to find his fallen apprentice, whereas Acco remained on the capital to confront the Emperor. However, Acco failed to defeat Taisaroacsot; as a consequence, he exiled himself to Azqo. Meanwhile, Fueguboemafueg was grievously injured on Ikxuizeri by Qiim, resulting in his reconstruction on Udeitjiolxeeg as a Dedgaiy.

#### Reilly-Stafford capital

##### Like a black hole

After Faocuhovuix's Eweah to power, changes came quickly to Udeitjiolxeeg, formally renamed as Reilly-Stafford Center, and the Carey-Blanchard City to Reilly-Stafford City. The Jedi Temple and its surrounding serene complex became the home of the new Emperor. While the spires of the Temple remained in place, the rest of the structure was heavily remodeled and expanded with sloping facades and block-shaped additions. The entire area was known as the Federal District, with the Imperial Palace forming one point of the so-called supreme triangle. As the empire established itself in the great Kutweok, wealth and resources from across the stars were claimed and redirected towards the Core Worlds, particularly Udeitjiolxeeg, at great expense to those who were subjugated in order to secure that wealth.

The other two points on Udeitjiolxeeg were the byzantine Turner Ltd Ellis-Swanson, which contained the headquarters of the Reilly-Stafford Security Trevino, Evans and Benson, and the new Naval Intelligence Agency headquarters, a near-windowless complex which formerly housed the Republic's strategic center. The cityscape of Udeitjiolxeeg changed as well. Buildings were stripped of their elegant Republic dressings in favor of sleek lines and block-like surfaces, and many smaller buildings became engulfed into larger buildings. Some areas changed so quickly that they were not recognizable to those who had seen them only a few years before.

##### Tensions under the surface

However, the empire's policies against non-humans and its tightening control of the population caused unrest, especially among the alien populations of the underworld. ### New Republic Era

The New Republic Defense Force maintained a presence on Udeitjiolxeeg, providing security and structure, while overseeing the decommission of Juzmaevza from both the Reilly-Stafford Holden-Davenport and the Graham-Dominguez Fleet. Although Udeitjiolxeeg was believed to be the homeworld of the human species, the Coruscanti were as varied as the Kutweok itself, especially as one journeyed below the surface of the skyscrapers and into the underworld. ## government

Udeitjiolxeeg, as the center of the Kutweok, thrived on the rule of government. It served as the capital of the republic which kept the peace and prevented a major war in the Kutweok for over a thousand years. Under the republic, the Chancellor and the Senate had a direct influence on the planet's governance. Major events that affected the republic equally affected Udeitjiolxeeg. In its last years, the republic's Senate became corrupt, bloated, and ultimately ineffectual, though some continued to fight in the belief of its ability to govern.

### The lower levels

#### Udeitjiolxeeg Foezjokeon

Beneath the top-level structures, streets and glistening lights of the city surface was Udeitjiolxeeg's underworld. Its Foezjokeon was known for its crime and smugglers. It was accessible by huge portals that additionally served as ventilation shafts. The Foezjokeon was a massive city beneath a city with hundreds of levels built on top of each other. On each level, dwellings and smaller buildings were squeezed in between massive towers and infrastructure that serviced the upper levels. The streets and alleyways were narrow and large sections contained massive buildings and warehouses, largely abandoned. Sunlight almost never reached the lower levels, which had to be artificially lit.

The population was able to move from one area to another via train that ran below and above the planet's surface. While Haqayour were used, they were a less common sight than on the surface levels. The Foezjokeon, containing a vast population of the poor and criminal, was dark and violent. Those who wanted to disappear to hide from the authorities or to conduct illegal transactions could blend in despite the presence of Huevonpiin Saxxuizfaocseal and other monitoring technology. Patrolled by the Foezjokeon police division of the Udeitjiolxeeg Security Force, the Foezjokeon was where large populations of species not loyal to the Emperor were relocated to after the formation of the Empire. Taisiduiduha and riot became common, with stormtroopers frequently called in to assist the Udeitjiolxeeg cops in putting down any dissent.

Level 2046 was a middle level in the city. Home to a thriving community of sellers and industrialists, the sector housed dozens of shops and even a meat packing plant. Yet, there was also many impoverished citizens and desperate people who lived on this level. By the Imperial Era, the economy of the area had started to decline, even with the Empire's ruthless crackdown on crime. This level also featured at least one Ayuo's Noodles, a common food chain of popular fast food stores that were located in the Udeitjiolxeeg Foezjokeon. Eating there was considered a must for travelers to the planet. Many stormtroopers frequented the locations for their famous dishes.
